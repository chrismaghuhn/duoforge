## Paired bench (supplement; the gate is the byte-equal library)

Same protocol as the forms piece: 32 rounds, Latin rotation of change and same-binary control in both IPO modes,
AB/BA alternating, 1024 battles × 3 repetitions after 2 warm-ups, pinned to CPU 3, 20000-draw paired bootstrap.
Pair `c02189e7` / `05c24c4a`. Ratios are after/before time: **above 1 = slower**.

| Profile | Workload | Raw median (95 % CI) | Same-binary control (95 % CI) | Adjusted median (95 % CI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPO on | plain | 1.0164 (+1.64 %; 0.9696–1.0711) | 0.9856 (0.9221–1.0103) | 1.0512 (+5.12 %; 0.9890–1.1190) |
| IPO on | events | 0.9756 (−2.44 %; 0.9438–1.0075) | 1.0231 (0.9784–1.0843) | 0.9608 (−3.92 %; 0.9014–1.0074) |
| IPO off | plain | 1.0202 (+2.02 %; 0.9793–1.0743) | 1.0074 (0.9781–1.0596) | 1.0073 (+0.73 %; 0.9672–1.0677) |
| IPO off | events | 1.0070 (+0.70 %; 0.9558–1.0317) | 0.9896 (0.9370–1.0207) | 1.0199 (+1.99 %; 0.9757–1.0432) |

The before and after bench binaries are instruction-identical in both modes (`objdump -d` equal); they differ only in
the build-id and the embedded commit string. Every spread above is therefore measurement noise of this VM. It is
larger than in the forms run: on identical code, two of four cells miss the tolerance fixed for a non-byte-equal
library (adjusted median ≤ 1.02 and 95 % CI upper < 1.05). The earlier pair `4d4bebf8` / `972eca6` showed the same
noise (no-IPO plain adjusted 1.0493, CI to 1.1122). This matters for the phase cuts that follow (see the PR text).
