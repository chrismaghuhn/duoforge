## Paired bench (supplement; the gate is the byte-equal library)

The fixed protocol: 32 rounds, Latin rotation of change and same-binary control in both IPO modes, AB/BA alternating,
1024 battles × 3 repetitions after 2 warm-ups, pinned to CPU 3, 20000-draw paired bootstrap. The tolerance was fixed
in advance for the case that the library is not byte-equal: per profile, control-adjusted median ratio ≤ 1.02 and 95 %
CI upper bound < 1.05. Ratios are after/before time: **above 1 = slower**. The measured binaries were built in the
first proof (`342770b3` vs `3df38f1`); the merged head links the same library bytes (table above).

| Profile | Workload | Raw median (95 % CI) | Same-binary control (95 % CI) | Adjusted median (95 % CI) | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPO on | plain | 1.0177 (+1.77 %; 0.9921–1.0591) | 1.0161 (0.9835–1.0412) | 1.0154 (+1.54 %; 0.9637–**1.0521**) | upper bound missed |
| IPO on | events | 1.0135 (+1.35 %; 0.9776–1.0310) | 1.0056 (0.9786–1.0246) | 0.9949 (−0.51 %; 0.9622–1.0471) | met |
| IPO off | plain | 0.9758 (−2.42 %; 0.9475–1.0141) | 0.9935 (0.9660–1.0473) | 0.9802 (−1.98 %; 0.9409–1.0096) | met |
| IPO off | events | 0.9918 (−0.82 %; 0.9523–1.0152) | 1.0013 (0.9851–1.0356) | 0.9737 (−2.63 %; 0.9327–1.0089) | met |

The before and after bench binaries are instruction-identical in both modes (`objdump -d` equal). Their sections differ
only in `.note.gnu.build-id` and 38 bytes of `.rodata`, the embedded commit string. The IPO-plain upper bound of 1.0521
is therefore measurement noise on identical code; the same-binary controls' own 95 % CIs reach up to 1.0473. One repetition was
flagged disturbed (IPO control, before side). The run was not repeated.
