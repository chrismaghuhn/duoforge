## The step (the owner's option a)

6e-4 left `tests/interactions/test_pool_g75.c` (the cheap set) for the owner: its checks mixed three rows with two Mega
Stones, so no part of it was one subject. The owner chose to put the stones with the other Mega batches and keep the rest
as one batch test:

1. **`5bddd637`** groups the checks inside the source by subject, each check as it was: `check_rows` (Pure Power, Battle
   Armor, Scope Lens; Scope Lens leaves the stones' loop, its two checks written out), `check_stones` (Medichamite and
   Clefablite, and the stones that stay out: Starminite, Sharpedonite with Strong Jaw, Altarianite marked since A0),
   `check_pairs` (the two Mega pairs and the base abilities of their base formes) and `check_battle_armor_bases` (Falinks
   and Perrserker). The expressions of the executed checks are the same.
2. **`b2f3834a`** splits it by these functions with `tools/restructure/split_test.py`:
   `interactions/test_cheap_set_rows.c` gets `check_rows` and `check_battle_armor_bases`; `forms/test_mega_stones.c` gets
   `check_stones` and `check_pairs` as its seventh section, made by the merge of 6e-2 with G75 added
   ([specs/mega_stones.merge.json](specs/mega_stones.merge.json), [specs/g75.json](specs/g75.json)). The rest of
   `test_mega_stones.c` is byte for byte what it was, but the header's list of sections; `base_ability_legal` is the
   copy that batch 3's section already has, and `check_pairs` is `cheap_set_stones_check_pairs` there (the name of
   another section's function). [regen.sh](regen.sh) makes both files again: 2 of 2 byte-equal.

`tests/renames.tsv`: the G75 split is done with its two part rows; **no planned row is left**. The living references
follow: 5bp's Tests line names the two tests, 5ds's pin names `mega_stones`, and the comments of the registrations say
where the stones went. The registration keeps its flags.
