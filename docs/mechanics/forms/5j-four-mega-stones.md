# 5j. G18, four Mega Stones

- Area: forms
- Step: G18
- Tests: -

5j. **G18, four Mega Stones** (Tyranitarite 15 of the 227 survey teams, Baxcalibrite 13, Aerodactylite 1, Manectite 1; no engine rule and no public value: the Mega path is generic, the step marks four stones in the manifest and records what each Mega does). A stone is marked when the Mega forme's ability is already marked, a base forme of the holder has a marked ability, and the base forme has exactly one Mega. Nothing in the tables changes (the stones are pure data: their only callback is the `onTakeItem` of every Mega Stone, `STONE_TAKE_ITEM`).
   - **The pin.** No Champions-specific rule for any of the stones: `data/mods/champions/items.ts` has `inherit: true, isNonstandard: null` only (Tyranitarite :978, Baxcalibrite :82, Aerodactylite and Manectite likewise); the base items are `data/items.ts:7392` (Tyranitarite, `megaStone: {Tyranitar: Tyranitar-Mega}`, `itemUser`, `onTakeItem`) and :403 (Baxcalibrite). `canMegaEvo` of the mod (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:182-194`) returns `item.megaStone[baseSpecies.name]`, and the mod's `formeChange` (:56-109) keeps a Mega after it faints, prints `-mega` and counts as the Truant action. The mod has no `pokedex.ts`: the formes are those of `data/pokedex.ts`: Tyranitar-Mega (:4961-4974) Rock and Dark, 100/164/150/95/120/71, Sand Stream, 255 kg; Baxcalibur-Mega (:19056-19069) Dragon and Ice, 115/175/117/105/101/87, Thermal Exchange, 315 kg; Aerodactyl-Mega (:3190-3204) Rock and Flying, 80/135/85/70/95/150, Tough Claws, 79 kg (base :3176, Rock Head / Pressure / Unnerve); Manectric-Mega (:5952-5965) Electric, 70/75/80/135/80/135, Intimidate, 44 kg (base :5937, Static / Lightning Rod / Minus). `setAbility` (`sim/pokemon.ts:1908-1946`) ends the old ability, starts the Mega's own with a fresh state and runs its Start event even when it is the same ability (:1941-1944: only a Transform of the same ability does not): Sand Stream sets the weather again unless it is the same weather, Intimidate lowers the foes' Attack at the Mega Evolution, Thermal Exchange and Tough Claws have no Start.
   - **What runs where.** `dfi_run_mega` (`src/combat/forms/mega.h`) emits the forme line and the Mega line, then `dfi_entry_ability` for an ability with an entry effect (weather, terrain, Intimidate, Fairy Aura); the setup gate (`dfi_manifest_mega`, closure_member.c) needs the Mega ability and the stone marked. All four Mega abilities are marked (Sand Stream, Thermal Exchange since G14, Tough Claws, Intimidate); the base abilities that the recorded sets use are Sand Stream, Thermal Exchange, Rock Head and Lightning Rod (all marked).
   - **Not marked, and why.** Charizardite X (3 teams), Garchompite Z (15), Lucarionite Z (11), Absolite Z (5) and Raichunite X need more than a mark: **the tables link one Mega per base forme** (the generator takes the first Mega of the legal pool's order: gen_closure.py:2159-2163; Charizard links Mega-Y, Garchomp Mega, Lucario Mega, Absol Mega), read by closure_member.c (146-149, 254-258, 334-337, 387-399, 425-431), turn.c (`dfi_forme_of`, `dfi_run_mega`) and the data API (data_query.c:73-92); a recorded battle of Charizardite X failed with E_INVALID_ARGUMENT at the Mega choice (the holder is not "holding its own stone"), and the tables already carry "second Mega forme" as an unmodelled feature of those stones, so the half-modelled check refuses a mark. Their step is a model of Mega by stone (decision to follow, with Levitate for Garchomp-Mega-Z). Also unmarked, by the base forme or the Mega ability: Gardevoirite (Pixilate is marked, but Synchronize, Trace and Telepathy are not; follow-up once Trace lands), Froslassite (Snow Warning is marked, Snow Cloak and Cursed Body are not), Metagrossite (Tough Claws marked; Clear Body and Light Metal not), Glimmoranite (Adaptability marked; Toxic Debris and Corrosion not), Lucarionite Z (Aura Guard is unmodelled and Inner Focus is not marked), and Mega abilities that nothing models: Absolite Z Sharpness, Gengarite Shadow Tag, Swampertite Swift Swim, Blastoisinite Mega Launcher, Blazikenite Speed Boost, Dragoninite Multiscale, Pyroarite Fire Mane, Scizorite Technician, Alakazite and Meowsticite Trace, Cameruptite Sheer Force.
   - **Evidence.** Five recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; seed 1 for all; the Mega Evolution happens in every one and the Mega ability acts): `g18_tyranitar_replaces` (Sand Stream at the start; the foe's Pelipper comes in in turn 2 and Drizzle replaces the sand; then Tyranitar Mega Evolves and the Mega's Sand Stream replaces the rain, with the sand chipping Pelipper), `g18_tyranitar_same` (the Mega Evolution at turn 3 while the first sand is up: no second weather line and the sand ends on the turn that the first Sand Stream gave it), `g18_baxcalibur_thermal` (Thermal Exchange before the Mega: Flamethrower gives Attack +1; after the Mega Evolution, the turn it uses Protect, and then a Flamethrower and a Flare Blitz that each give +1; no line at the Mega Evolution), `g18_aerodactyl` (Rock Head becomes Tough Claws: Iron Head before and after, a Rock Slide with no contact), `g18_manectric` (Lightning Rod draws a Thunderbolt before the Mega; the Mega's Intimidate lowers Raichu and Kingambit, and Kingambit's Defiant answers). 125 pool battles and 672 stops (120 and 644 before), 47 marked moves as before; four items marked.
   - **Mutation checks.** Ten edits, each killed by the conformance or the pool tables test: no entry ability at the Mega Evolution, the weather restarted when it is the same, the Mega keeping the base ability, the Mega keeping the base stats, the forme line naming the base, no Intimidate entry at the Mega, and each of the four stones unmarked.
