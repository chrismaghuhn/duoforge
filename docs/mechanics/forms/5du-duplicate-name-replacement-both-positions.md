5du. **The duplicate name of a replacement of both positions in one step is refused at the switch-in (issue 602, the `illusion-duplicate-unrefused` row of issue 554; branch chris/illusion-duplicate-fix; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396 and the lane A campaign 4301).** Decision 0026 section 4 (amended by I2, lead decision A) refuses the disguise and its real member on the field together, at the switch-in that makes it (`dfi_switch_in`, `src/combat/flow/switch.h`). The check of the member's entry compared with the shown name (`illusion.shown`), which the fold (`src/combat/events.c`) writes only at the end of the step. When both actives of a side faint and the side replaces both in one step, the holder can enter first, disguised as the last standing member to its right, and that member second: the second switch-in saw no shown name, went through, and the step ended in E_INVARIANT (the knowledge and occupant invariants of `src/state/invariants.c`). Now `dfi_switch_in` also reads the disguise from the other position (`ability_state`, written at the holder's own switch-in, `dfi_illusion_disguise_up`): the real member of the disguise that the other position shows is refused E_UNSUPPORTED before any change and before its SWITCH event. The other order (the member first, the holder second) was already refused at the holder's switch-in, whose disguise would be the other active. The check sits in `dfi_switch_in`, so it applies to every entry of a step (a voluntary switch and a drag too) while the holder stands on the other position; only the replacement is tested. **Not covered here:** a holder that faints in the step it entered (its ability_state is cleared by the faint, its shown name is not written yet), after which its disguise member is dragged in beside it (Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw, Red Card): still E_INVARIANT at the step's end (issue #619); and Skill Swap on an Illusion holder succeeds although the pin's Illusion has failskillswap (a separate main bug, issue #620). No new public value; nothing in decision 0026 changes. **Evidence.** `fz_4301_17_p4`: the prefix (steps 0 to 4) of battle 17 of campaign 4301 (team D against team A; step 3 faints both of p1's actives, step 4 brings Zoroark-Hisui disguised as Pelipper, then the real Pelipper), with Bitter Malice (unmarked on main) replaced by Grass Knot, which Zoroark-Hisui learns (`data/mods/champions/learnsets.ts`) and which no step of the prefix uses; recorded from the pinned Showdown b2cb775 (`ps_trace.js`, `--check` matches; its logs and draws equal those of the campaign trace, step by step). `diff_driver.py corpus` on a scratch corpus holding only this battle: UNSUPPORTED at step 4 with the fix; ORACLE_GAP `illusion-duplicate-unrefused` without it (the runner answers DIVERGENCE at step 4, E_INVARIANT). The battle is not committed: it ends in a refusal, so it is no pool battle and no corpus entry, and no count pin moves. White-box `check_double_replacement` in `tests/interactions/test_pool_i2.c` (`duoforge.state.pool_i2`): i2_illusion_break at team start, made into that replacement of both positions (the two other brought members fainted, Zoroark and Gholdengo on the bench in that party order); the order of the two switches is set by the speed of the fainted members they replace (`dfi_key_of`). Refused: Zoroark first, then Gholdengo, into either position pair (before Gholdengo's SWITCH event), and Gholdengo first, then Zoroark. Not refused (DUOFORGE_OK): Gholdengo first into position 0, then Zoroark into position 1 (only fainted members to its right, no disguise); Zoroark alone into a fainted position; Gholdengo next to a Zoroark whose disguise broke; Gholdengo into the disguised Zoroark's own position. Mutant M1, the old check (this fix reverted): `duoforge.state.pool_i2` red (4 of its 240 checks: both holder-first replacements answer OK and Gholdengo's SWITCH is emitted), and the prefix battle is an ORACLE_GAP again; `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data`, `conformance_pool` and `corpus` stay green under it: no committed battle brings a holder and its disguise member in one step. Every other reference battle is unchanged (the full Release suite with the pinned checkout, and `capture_battle_pins.py --check` over 1197 battles). **Not changed here:** `fz_105_99` of issue 554 is in the cloud thread of issue 396 and was not replayed; the 36 battles of campaign 4301 need Bitter Malice marked (A0, branch chris/a0-part4), so their rerun belongs there, as does the support manifest's note that Bitter Malice and Night Daze wait for this gap.
