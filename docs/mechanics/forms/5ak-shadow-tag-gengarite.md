# 5ak. G41, Shadow Tag and Gengarite

- Area: forms
- Step: G41
- Tests: duoforge.abilities.shadow_tag

5ak. **G41, Shadow Tag and Gengarite** (the Mega ability of Gengar; no new public value, no new event, no state, no view change). Marked: the ability **Shadow Tag** (`data/abilities.ts:4156-4173`; the Champions mod does not override it) and the item **Gengarite** (its base ability Cursed Body is G27's). **No handler**: the ability is an engine row read by id. The turn code has one predicate, `dfi_switch_trapped` (`src/combat/turn.c`), and the request builder (`src/state/request.c`, `dfi_slot_candidates`) asks it only at a **TURN** boundary: a standing Pokemon with a standing foe that has Shadow Tag, that is neither a holder itself nor a Ghost type (by its current types: `typechart.ts:204`, `trapped: 3`; Soak's Water makes it trappable again), is offered no switch, in either slot. In doubles every foe is adjacent (`isAdjacent`), so the foe's both actives count. Replacements, pivots (a move's selfSwitch, Emergency Exit, Eject Button) and forced switches ignore it (the reference's `canSwitch` for those is the reserve test alone); the trap is recomputed from the board at the next TURN boundary, so a holder that has fainted or left by then traps nobody (`nextTurn` clears `trapped` and runs `TrapPokemon` again, `sim/battle.ts:1726-1730`).
   - **Why the exclusion leaks nothing** (decision 0007, the information rule of 0018 section 1: a fact is public when the game shows it or it follows from the open team sheets and the lines). At the pin the first active gets `trapped: true` and the last active only `maybeTrapped`; a switch of the latter is refused with `[Unavailable choice]` and an updated request under a new rqid (`sim/side.ts:527-534`, `984-1000`, `sim/pokemon.ts:1112-1134`), which is how the game hides whether a foe might have a trapping ability. Under Open Team Sheets that doubt does not exist: the Mega forme and its single ability Shadow Tag follow from the Gengarite on the sheet and the `-mega` line, a Trace copy is a `-ability|X|Shadow Tag|Trace|[from] ability: Trace|[of] Y` line (the view's `ability_now`, decision 0018 section 6.1), a type is the forme's or Soak's `-start|X|typechange|Water`. The trap state is therefore a pure function of public information, and a domain without the switch tells the player nothing it cannot derive. The engine offers no `maybeTrapped` state and no refusal code; the differential harness maps the pin's last-active asymmetry instead (below).
   - **The cases, derived or refused.** (1) Ability suppression: Neutralizing Gas is not a row of the pool, Gastro Acid is unmarked, Mold Breaker does not touch it; (2) Skill Swap, Role Play, Entrainment, Simple Beam, Worry Seed, Wandering Spirit and Mummy are unmarked (Trace is the one that moves an ability, and its line is public); (3) a holder is exempt itself, and its ally is not trapped by it (`pokemon.hasAbility('shadowtag')`, only foes are asked); two holders on both sides trap each other's partner only; (4) Ghost immunity by current types, Soak included; (5) Shed Shell and Run Away (`onTrapPokemon` at priority -10, after Shadow Tag's, `data/mods/champions/abilities.ts:71-80` for the ability) are unmarked, so no battle has them; (6) a holder that faints or leaves in the turn does not trap at the next turn (the boundary is recomputed from the board), and the PIVOT and REPLACEMENT boundaries in between are free; (7) the first and the last active are treated alike, because both are derivable. Illusion is unmarked. `tests/abilities/test_shadow_tag.c` names each unmarked row that would free, move, suppress or hide: marking any of them fails there until `dfi_switch_trapped` reads it. Python has no rule: the live bot still reads Showdown's request (HauptSession's flow for `[Unavailable choice]`).
   - **Harness** (`tools/reference/ps_play.js`, no recorded value): the sampler reads each Pokemon's own `trapped` flag (the request of the last active has only `maybeTrapped`), and the domain check accepts exactly one request change, the refused switch of the last active that turns its `maybeTrapped` into `trapped: true` and marks the request `update: true` (`sim/side.ts:521-534`; an `rqid` change would do too), keeping the sample; any other change, or that one without the marker, stays a dropped sample. Measured: a first campaign (seed 4143) judged 1407 requests, 98 of them changed in this way (111 requests carried `trapped: true`, 30 only `maybeTrapped`); with the marker accepted, a second campaign (seed 4144) drops 0 of 1446.
   - **Evidence**: six recorded POOL battles (`g41_shadow_tag_second`: the Gengar in the second position of its side, so a probe of the foe's first position alone fails; `g41_shadow_tag_fainted`: the foes take down the Gengar's ally and both reserves and then the Gengar, which stays in its position with no reserve, and from the next TURN boundary traps nobody; and the four that follow: `g41_shadow_tag`: the Gengar Mega Evolves at turn 1 and both foes are trapped from turn 2, with a U-turn pivot; `g41_shadow_tag_ghost`: a Gholdengo free, its partner trapped and pivoting; `g41_shadow_tag_soak`: a Dragapult trapped after Soak; `g41_shadow_tag_trace`: a Gardevoir's Trace copies Shadow Tag after a replacement, traps the Kingambit beside the Gengar and is itself exempt), `tests/abilities/test_shadow_tag.c` (the domain at the turns of those battles, the pivot boundaries and the guard), `checkG41` (the pinned handlers on stubs and the one immune type). Eight mutants of the rule (holder not exempt, Ghost not exempt, a fainted holder still trapping, the own side asked instead of the foes, only the foe's first position asked, trapped at every boundary, never trapped, only the first position trapped) are each caught by `duoforge.abilities.shadow_tag`; two of them survived the first round (the fainted holder and the first-position probe), which is why `g41_shadow_tag_fainted` and `g41_shadow_tag_second` exist. Differential: 300 battles of two Mega Gengar teams (no Substitute) pass in 300 of 300 with 1446 judged requests (about one in ten with a trap in the request), plus 300 earlier battles with the unmapped request change (299 pass, one unsupported: a poison KO with a Tailwind tie, unrelated to the trap). **Known gap**: Gengar sets with Substitute stay blocked (Substitute is not marked).
