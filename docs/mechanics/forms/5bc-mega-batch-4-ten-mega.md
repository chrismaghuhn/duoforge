   - **Review rework (the lead's review of PR #281).** (1) The pin's test is `source && target === source` (data/abilities.ts:2262 for Keen Eye, :447 for Big Pecks): only a drop the holder causes itself is let through. In the engine `DFI_POSITIONS` is the holder itself (the comment of `dfi_boost`), `source == flat` is the holder too, and a drop by another Pokemon is blocked, as the pin does. The pin's third case, a drop with no source at all, cannot be written in the engine (the source is a position or `DFI_POSITIONS`) and cannot occur for these stats in the pool: the pin's negative Attack, Defense and accuracy boosts without a source are the Max moves (Max Phantasm, data/moves.ts:11350, and the boost at :11484), `isNonstandard: "Past"`, not in Champions. The condition is therefore left exactly as it is, with the reasoning in the comment of the block. Hyper Cutter (G39) has the same shape and the same reasoning, so it is not changed. (2) The six survivors, by reason. The source checks (Keen Eye on accuracy, Big Pecks on Defense) are unreachable: no legal Keen Eye forme lowers its own accuracy, and the only Big Pecks forme, Pidgeot, learns no move that lowers its own Defense (the Keen Eye formes that learn Close Combat, Farfetch'd and Lycanroc, lose their own Defense and Special Defense, which Keen Eye does not block). The cap at -6 (`<=`) and the FAIL event id are unreachable: a primary accuracy or Defense drop caused by another Pokemon is in no marked move, so the non-secondary branch never runs. The two boost checks are equivalent: a zero boost cannot lower a stat, so the veil changes nothing. A white-box test through the step API would need a legal primary drop or a legal self drop, and `dfi_boost` is static, so these four cannot be closed without a new test hook or a marked move; they are left as survivors with these reasons. Caught stays 19 of 25 (the engine code is unchanged since the mutation run, only comments moved).
   - **Regenerator on a pivot switch-out (a fix of G39 Regenerator on a pivot switch-out, examined; no engine change).** `duoforge.python.replay` (`SpectatorTest.test_view_equals_duoforge`) reported `53 != 20` for `g51_dragalgite` at k=3, side 0 (Dragalge-Mega, Regenerator, leaves by Flip Turn). The engine is correct. A white-box test (`check_regenerator_pivot` in `tests/abilities/test_regenerator_pivot.c`) checks the state at the pivot request, after step 2: Dragalge has not left and has not healed, its own HP is 35 of 172 and the opponent's view is 20 percent. After the answer (`switch 3`) the heal of a third (57, to 92 of 172) comes first, and the opponent's view is 53 percent: the HEAL event (cause ABILITY, Regenerator) reaches the fold, which attributes it to the leaving member. The mismatch is in the spectator's point placement, `python/duoforge_replay/points.py`: a switch point is the `|switch|` line, and the observation is the fold of the lines before it, so for a pivot whose switch-out heals, the spectator's point includes the heal (`-heal ... [from] ability: Regenerator|[silent]` precedes `|switch|p1a: Staraptor|...|[from] Flip Turn` in the trace), while the request was made before it. The engine's value at the request is the one a player sees. The fix belongs in the spectator's point placement (the lead's lane, python/duoforge_replay, not edited here); until then `duoforge.python.replay` stays red on this battle.
