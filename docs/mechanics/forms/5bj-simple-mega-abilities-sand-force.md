  - **Rules (pin b2cb775).** Sand Force (`data/abilities.ts:3956-3973`): onBasePowerPriority 21, chainModify([5325, 4096]) for a Rock, Ground or Steel move of its holder in Sandstorm (the battle's effective weather), and onImmunity 'sandstorm' false. Stalwart (`4503-4513`): onModifyMove sets `tracksTarget`; RedirectTarget is not run for such a move (`sim/pokemon.ts:829`), and ModifyMove runs before getMoveTargets (`sim/battle-actions.ts:431-439`, `:467`), so Follow Me and Lightning Rod do not redirect the holder's moves. ## Support

**Sand Force, Shell Armor, Filter and Stalwart, and the Mega Stones Garchompite, Steelixite, Slowbronite, Scolipite, Aggronite and Skarmorite (step G63, decision 0015 entry 5bj)**: Sand Force (x1.3 for a Rock, Ground or Steel move in Sandstorm; no Sandstorm damage) and Filter (x0.75 on a super effective hit, as Solid Rock) are engine rows; Shell Armor takes no critical hit (the crit roll is still drawn); Stalwart keeps its single-target moves from Follow Me and Lightning Rod. The marks also open these base sets that carry the ability (no change to their other abilities): Sand Force on Hippowdon and Excadrill; Shell Armor on Torterra, Samurott, Torkoal (Drought is the marked set, Whitesmoke stays refused) and Goodra-Hisui (its Sapsipper and Gooey stay refused); Filter on Mr. Mime; Stalwart on Archaludon.
