# 5dw. The faint-held name's member dragged in the step its holder entered is refused at the drag-in (issue 619, found by the review of 5du; branch chris/illusion-faint-drag-fix).

5dw. **The faint-held name's member dragged in the step its holder entered is refused at the drag-in (issue 619, found by the review of 5du; branch chris/illusion-faint-drag-fix).** A holder that enters disguised and faints in the same step without a hit (a KO that is not a hit, here Stealth Rock at its entry) keeps its name on the fainted position (decision 0026 section 4, "A faint while disguised"); a hit always breaks the disguise first. If the name's real member is then dragged in on the other position later in that step (Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw or Red Card, all marked), no check of `dfi_switch_in` (`src/combat/flow/switch.h`) saw it: the faint had cleared the holder's `ability_state` (`dfi_process_faints`), so the check of 5du did not apply, and the one-name check and the faint-held check read `illusion.shown`, the name as the previous step's fold left it (the fold writes it only at the end of a step), which knows nothing of the entry in this step. The drag went through, its DRAG line cleared the name in the fold, the fainted position's foe occupant was the holder, never seen, and the step ended in E_INVARIANT (`DFI_INV_SEEN_MASK`, `src/state/invariants.c`). **The governing rule** is lead decision B of decision 0026 (the clear of a faint-held name: the real disguise member entering, by SWITCH or DRAG, while the holder stands fainted on the field or on the bench, is refused), not decision A of 5du (that needs the holder's disguise up, and the faint dropped it). **The fix** is one mechanism, no new state: `dfi_switch_in` computes the side's shown name as the fold has it at that point of the step (`dfi_illusion_shown_now`: the tail's `illusion.shown`, then the step's own SWITCH, DRAG and ILLUSION_END events of the side, in order, as `dfi_fold_illusion_switch` and the break apply them: a disguised entry sets its disguise, an entry of the name's real member ends the name, the break ends it, a faint keeps it), and the three checks that read the shown name (the one-name check, the second-name check and the faint-held check of lead decision B) read it instead of `illusion.shown`; the one-name check's "the other position shows the name" is `dfi_illusion_shows_name` (the disguise up, or the holder fainted there: `dfi_illusion_shown_occupant` with the step's name). In the case of this issue the one-name check is reached first (its other position holds the holder fainted under the name) and the faint-held check would refuse it as well: E_UNSUPPORTED before any change and before the DRAG event. **No fallback:** under pool rules the step's name needs the step's events; without an event buffer, or with one past its bound (`overflow`), `dfi_switch_in` answers E_INVARIANT before any change, never the tail's name in their place. Every production step has the buffer: `dfi_turn_run` and `dfi_turn_start` are called only from `src/state/request.c` (`dfi_battle_step_observed`, with its staged buffer, which every public and internal step entry reaches: `duoforge_battle_step`, `_step_events`, `dfi_battle_step_tape`, `dfi_battle_step_events_tape`; the batch, `src/batch/batch.c`, and the search, `src/search/search.c`, step through these), and request.c fails an overflowed step with E_INVARIANT anyway. **Outcome changes besides the refusal.** (a) A name that a break ends earlier in the step no longer refuses anything: a holder disguised as X from the step's start, knocked out by a hit (ILLUSION_END before FAINT, the fold clears the name), and X dragged in later in the step was refused E_UNSUPPORTED by the one-name check (it read the start's X and the fainted holder) and is now played; likewise the holder dragged back after such a break under a new disguise Y is no longer refused by the second-name check. Both match the fold and the pin (the break clears the name before the faint). (b) A name that its own member's entry ends earlier in the step: with four brought members a second entry of the holder under another name needs the first name's member to enter in between, which ends that name, so the new disguise is allowed where the start's name refused it. **The variant of two entries** (the review: the holder entering twice in one step through drags) is covered by the same reading: a second entry of the holder compares with the name of its first; it is pinned white-box with a synthetic earlier SWITCH line (below).
