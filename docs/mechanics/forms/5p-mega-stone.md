# 5p. G23-A, the Mega of a stone

- Area: forms
- Step: G23-A
- Tests: duoforge.state.mega_by_stone, duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.data.api

5p. **G23-A, the Mega of a stone** (the data model of the second Megas; no mechanic, no new battle, and no table layout change). G18 found that the tables link one Mega per base forme, so Charizardite X, Garchompite Z, Lucarionite Z, Absolite Z and Raichunite X could not be marked, and that Meowsticite (one stone, two base formes) was stopped by the Python data layer. This step makes the Mega a function of the pair (base forme, stone); marks and Levitate follow in the next step (G23-B/C).
   - **The lookup.** `dfi_mega_of(species, item)` (closure_member.c; `item` is 1 + the id, as in the member): the base forme's own link when the stone is the stone of that link (`mega_forme`, `mega_item` of the forme row), else the stone's own row (`mega_base`, `mega_forme` of the item row, its first pair). Every pair of the pool is one of the two (Charizard-Mega-X: the item row of Charizardite X; Meowstic-F-Mega: the forme row of Meowstic-F), which the generator checks and fails the build for a pair that neither names; so the tables keep their layout and their columns, and the first-link meaning of the old fields (`forme.mega_forme`, `duoforge_forme_info.mega_*`) is unchanged. O(1), no heap. A Mega forme, an item that is no stone of the forme and an id beyond the table give `DFI_FORME_NONE`.
   - **Who reads it.** The setup and state checks (`mega_capable`, the evolved member's stats and ability, `dfi_manifest_mega_of`: the support is that of the pair's own Mega ability), `dfi_run_mega` (the forme line) and `dfi_forme_of` (the types of an evolved member: it still holds the stone, which Knock Off never takes from its own species), and the data API. The member's state does not change (is_mega and the item decide), so the canonical state encoding and the context fingerprints of CLOSURE and TEAM_C are untouched; their tables are byte for byte the same, and every recorded battle and golden passes unchanged (conformance_pool.h is identical).
   - **Tables.** The five second Mega Stones had the unmodelled feature "second Mega forme X of Y"; it is gone (their callback is the `onTakeItem` of every Mega Stone, data of the link). The handler column of those five items changes, so the pool tables, their hash and the POOL fingerprints change (CLOSURE and TEAM_C do not). 39 items are unmodeled (44 before). The stones stay unmarked in the manifest: Absol-Mega-Z, Lucario-Mega-Z and Raichu-Mega-X bring abilities that nothing models, and Charizardite X and Garchompite Z are marked with their battles in the next step.
   - **Public API (owner's OK, 2026-10-03, relayed by HauptSession).** `duoforge_mega_info {base_species, stone, mega_species, mega_ability, supported}` (5 words), `duoforge_data_mega_count` and `duoforge_data_mega_at` (ascending item id). `duoforge_forme_info` and every old field keep their exact meaning (the first Mega of the forme). `supported` says what `mega_supported` says, for the pair; whether the stone is marked is the ITEM table's answer. The layout dump pins the struct and `python/duoforge/_layout.py` has its dtype. The two functions are bound in `python/duoforge/data.py` (decision 0020's binding of the data API) as plain ctypes wrappers (`mega_count`, `mega_at`), tested against the C values in `python/tests/test_data.py`.
   - **Consumers for HauptSession** (not touched here): `python/duoforge_live/data.py` `mega_of` (:130-138) reads the item row only, the first pair of a stone, so Meowstic-F with Meowsticite and every second Mega are `FORME_CHANGE` stops in `lines.py:316` and a wrong `mega_capable`/ability in `tracker.py:101,498`; the same two-step lookup (the forme's link first, then the item row), or `duoforge_data_mega_at`, fixes it.
   - **Tests.** `duoforge.state.mega_by_stone` (new): every Mega forme row of the pool is reached from its base forme holding its own stone and from no other pair; the evolved member has the stats and the ability of that Mega's row and the HP of the base forme and passes the whole member check; a holder of another forme's stone does not evolve. `duoforge.data.pool_tables`: the lookup for every Mega forme, the five second Megas and Meowsticite by name, the refusals, and the support of a pair over a manifest without Tough Claws (Charizardite X unsupported, Y not). `duoforge.data.api`: the count, the ascending order, the old single link as one of the list, the refusals with the output untouched, the pairs by name, the per-pair `supported`, and the oracle of the setup differential now asks the new functions. Nine mutation checks, all killed: no pair from the stone row, none from the forme link, the stats or the ability of the linked Mega, any stone of any pair counting, the support of the first Mega for every stone, a count that ignores the second stone, an info that names the first Mega, its support from the first Mega.
