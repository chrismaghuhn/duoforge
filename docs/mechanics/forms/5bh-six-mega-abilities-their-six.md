# 5bh. G59, six Mega abilities and their six Mega Stones: Mega Launcher, Huge Power, Thick Fat, Fire Mane, Spicy Spray and Mega Sol

- Area: forms
- Step: G59
- Tests: duoforge.forms.mega_stones, duoforge.abilities.mega_launcher, duoforge.abilities.mega_sol

5bh. **G59, six Mega abilities and their six Mega Stones: Mega Launcher, Huge Power, Thick Fat, Fire Mane, Spicy Spray and Mega Sol** (the Mega ability of each forme, one stone each: Blastoisinite, Mawilite, Venusaurite, Pyroarite, Scovillainite, Meganiumite; no public value, no new state, no new event, no new draw site, no view change, no version bump). The six abilities are engine rows (`ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator, handler NONE) that `src/combat/turn.c` reads by id; the six stones are marked on the Mega-by-stone model of 5p (their base abilities Torrent, Rain Dish, Hyper Cutter, Intimidate, Chlorophyll, Unnerve and Overgrow are marked already). The pinned texts are `G59_ABILITY_FACTS` (whole callbacks, whitespace collapsed), checked by `check_g34_facts`. The Champions mod overrides none of them, except that Fire Mane, Spicy Spray and Mega Sol inherit (`inherit: true`, `isNonstandard: null`, `G59_INHERIT_ONLY`), which the generator allows and nothing else. Mega Sol's side in `sim/pokemon.ts` (`effectiveWeather`) and the sunnyday handler it calls are checked by `check_g59_facts`.
   - **Mega Launcher** (`data/abilities.ts:2546-2556`, onBasePowerPriority 19, the Sharpness slot): chainModify(1.5) for a pulse move of its holder, using the public static flag PULSE (decision 0020, now read by the engine for this one bit). It sits after Sharpness and before Muscle Band, the type boosters and the terrains.
   - **Huge Power** (`:1886-1896`, onModifyAtkPriority 5): x2 for a physical move of its holder, in the ModifyAtk chain. The event follows the move's category, so Body Press (Defense as the stat) is doubled, and Foul Play (the target's Attack) is doubled by the USER's ability, the user being the event's holder.
   - **Thick Fat** (`:5014-5033`, onSourceModifyAtkPriority 6 and onSourceModifySpAPriority 5, breakable): x0.5 for an Ice or Fire move against its holder, the defender's ability, in the attacking chain of either category. No Mold Breaker is marked, so the breakable flag has no interaction (guarded by a test).
   - **Fire Mane** (`:1295-1315`, onModifyAtkPriority 5 and onModifySpAPriority 5): x1.5 for a Fire move of its holder, either category.
   - **Spicy Spray** (`:4466-4475`, onDamagingHit): after every damaging hit, contact or not, `source.trySetStatus('brn', target)` with no roll. The attacker is burned through `dfi_try_status` (the immunities, a status already up, a fainted attacker and Synchronize on the attacker all as for the other sources). It runs also when the hit knocked the holder out, as Flame Body does.
   - **Mega Sol** (`:2558-2571`, onWeatherModifyDamagePriority 1): the holder's own handler, first of the WeatherModifyDamage event, calls sunnyday's onWeatherModifyDamage with the holder's moves read as sun (Fire x1.5, Water x0.5, `data/conditions.ts:556-578`) and returns the damage (a fast exit), so the field's own weather handler does not run for those moves. `dfi_get_damage` replaces the field's rain and sun modifier by Mega Sol's for the holder. **Weather-reading moves are refused**: `sim/pokemon.ts:2190-2198` makes effectiveWeather sun for every effect of a Move, Weather or Mega Sol itself while the holder is the active Pokemon, so Solar Beam (its charge and its onBasePower, `data/moves.ts:17224-17258`), Weather Ball (type and power) and Thunder and Hurricane (their accuracy) would read another weather than the engine's. With the field in sun the two agree; otherwise `dfi_mega_sol_refused` returns E_UNSUPPORTED at the move's own use, after BeforeMove. Meganium's legal learnset has Solar Beam and Weather Ball among the marked moves; Thunder and Hurricane are not in it. Meganium learns no Fire or Water move other than Weather Ball, which is Fire only under sun, so the replaced Fire and Water lines of the modifier are equal to the sun modifier in every legal battle; they are not separately observable (stated in the test).
   - **Evidence**: recorded POOL battles `g59_*` (genders in each paste; the Mega Evolution is on the turn of the plan, the forme and stone in the trace): `g59_mega_launcher` (Blastoise (M) with Aura Sphere before and after its Mega, in Pelipper (M)'s rain), `g59_huge_power` (Mawile (F) Mega Evolves on turn 1 and uses Foul Play, then Iron Head; both foes aim at Gholdengo), `g59_thick_fat` (Venusaur (M) Mega Evolves on turn 1; Milotic (F)'s Ice Beam and Incineroar (M)'s Flare Blitz both halved by its defender's ability), `g59_fire_mane` (Pyroar (M) Mega Evolves on turn 1 and uses Flare Blitz, then Heat Wave, and Dark Pulse, which is not Fire), `g59_spicy_spray` (Scovillain (F) Mega Evolves on turn 1; Gholdengo (genderless) is burned by its non-contact Shadow Ball, and Kingambit (M) by a later hit), `g59_spicy_spray_contact` (Sneasler (M) is burned by its contact Close Combat), `g59_mega_sol` (Meganium (F): Sunny Day, then Solar Beam with no charge turn under the Mega and Weather Ball as a Fire move in sun on the third turn).
   - **Tests**: `tests/forms/test_mega_stones.c, tests/abilities/test_mega_launcher.c, tests/abilities/test_mega_sol.c` (`duoforge.forms.mega_stones, duoforge.abilities.mega_launcher, duoforge.abilities.mega_sol`): the marks, the handler column and the families of the six, the six stones and their base abilities, the Mega of each stone (`dfi_mega_of`), Mold Breaker unmarked, the PULSE bit of the pulse moves, and the refusal of `g59_mega_sol` with the field's weather changed to rain before its Solar Beam step and before its Weather Ball step (played as recorded when unchanged).
   - **Generator tests**: `SmallRulesG59` in `tools/datagen/test_gen_closure.py` (every fact demanded, the inherit-only rule, the two Mega Sol facts).
   - **Numbers and open items**: the mutation checks and the 300-battle campaign are listed in the report of this step (not yet run at the time of this entry).
