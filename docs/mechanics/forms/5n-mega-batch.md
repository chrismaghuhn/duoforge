# 5n. The Mega batch (#158), nine Mega Stones

- Area: forms
- Step: -
- Tests: duoforge.items.knock_off

5n. **The Mega batch (#158), nine Mega Stones** (nine stones: Gardevoirite 11 of the 227 survey teams, Abomasite, Barbaracite, Beedrillite, Falinksite, Hawluchanite, Malamarite, Sceptilite and Scraftinite; no new rule for any of them, no table change, no public value; one fix of Unburden that Hawluchanite needs, below). The rule of G18: a stone is marked when the Mega forme's ability is marked, a base ability of the holder is marked, and the base forme has exactly one Mega. The step was found by listing every stone of the legal pool (`data/formats/pools/reg-mc.json`: `mega_items`, the Mega's `abilities_legal`, the manifest at 0.32.0): these nine are the whole list that passes.
   - **The pin.** The stones carry no Champions rule (`data/mods/champions/items.ts`: `inherit: true, isNonstandard: null`), `canMegaEvo` and `formeChange` of the mod are the generic path (`scripts.ts:56-109`, `:182-194`), `setAbility` runs the Mega's Start (`sim/pokemon.ts:1923-1944`). The Mega formes (`data/pokedex.ts`): Gardevoir-Mega :5512 Psychic/Fairy Pixilate (base Trace, marked since AC1: Trace's copy is replaced at the Mega, `ability_now` is zeroed), Abomasnow-Mega :8425 Snow Warning, Barbaracle-Mega :12957 Tough Claws, Beedrill-Mega :341 Adaptability, Falinks-Mega :16860 Defiant, Hawlucha-Mega :13149 No Guard, Malamar-Mega :12915 Contrary, Sceptile-Mega :5058 Lightning Rod, Scrafty-Mega :10476 Intimidate.
   - **Not marked, and why.** Meowsticite: one stone names two (base, Mega) pairs (Meowstic-M and Meowstic-F) and its item row links the first only (`mega_base` is one forme), so a Meowstic-F battle trips the Python data layer's `mega_of` (the replay test stops on FORME_CHANGE); it belongs to the Mega-by-stone model (G23-A). Everything the batch of G18 listed stays as it was (the second-Mega stones, the stones with an unmarked base ability or Mega ability).
   - **Unburden, an engine fix that Hawluchanite needs.** Hawlucha has Unburden and holds its own stone, so a Knock Off at it is refused by the stone (G16) and Unburden's `onTakeItem` has answered first (`data/abilities.ts:5240-5242`: the volatile is added whether or not the item is then taken), a case that G16 left out of its supported setups because Hawluchanite was unmarked. A campaign (120 battles, seed 9181, two teams with the nine Megas against the closure teams) found it: the reference had the volatile, the engine not. Three changes: `dfi_knock_off` sets the volatile before the stone's refusal; Unburden's Speed doubling asks for no item, as the pin does (`:5247-5251`, `dfi_speed_key`); the state invariant allows the volatile with the item held for a holder of its own stone (`mega_capable`) only. And the Mega Evolution ends the old ability first (`sim/pokemon.ts:1923`, Unburden's `onEnd` removes the volatile, `:5243-5245`): `dfi_run_mega` clears it. The public view (`DUOFORGE_POSITION_FLAG_UNBURDEN`, "Unburden doubles the Speed") shows it only while the item is gone: nothing says the quirk, and the Python spectator could not infer it; the harness expects the same (`conformance_compare.c`). CLOSURE and TEAM_C are unchanged (their Unburden holders lose the item first, and no Mega Stone holder has Unburden).
   - **Evidence.** Seven recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; seed 1 except `g24_hawlucha_malamar`, seed 3: the first Focus Blasts miss before the Mega and none after it): `g24_gardevoir_pixilate` (Trace copies a foe's ability; the Mega replaces it with Pixilate: Hyper Voice is Normal in turn 1 and Fairy with the 1.2 after the Mega Evolution), `g24_gardevoir_ghost` (against Annihilape, Fighting and Ghost: the Normal Hyper Voice is immune before the Mega, the Fairy one hits super effectively after it), `g24_abomasnow_scrafty` (Snow Warning at the start and no second snow at the Mega; the Mega Scrafty's Intimidate lowers Abomasnow and Milotic; Weather Ball is Ice in the snow), `g24_barbaracle_beedrill` (Beedrill's Poison Jab goes from the same-type 1.5 to Adaptability's 2 at its Mega; Barbaracle's Tough Claws before and after), `g24_hawlucha_malamar` (turn 1: Malamar's Knock Off at Hawlucha: no boost, no removal, the volatile set, and Hawlucha is not faster in turn 2 under the foe's Tailwind; turn 3: Hawlucha's Mega ends the volatile and No Guard makes Focus Blast hit; Malamar's Superpower raises with Contrary), `g24_sceptile_raichu` (Raichu's Thunderbolt is resisted before Sceptile's Mega and absorbed by Lightning Rod after it), `g24_falinks_pelipper` (genderless Falinks: Defiant answers Incineroar's Intimidate at the start and its Parting Shot after the Mega). 156 pool battles and 838 stops (149 and 801 before the step, main after G21). `duoforge.items.knock_off` reads the volatile, the stone, the view and the invariant after Knock Off and after the Mega.
   - **Mutation checks.** Fifteen edits, each killed by the conformance, the pool tables test or `duoforge.items.knock_off`: the volatile set only after the item is taken, the invariant refusing the volatile with the stone held, the Speed doubling ignoring the held item, the view showing Unburden with the item held, the Mega Evolution keeping the volatile, Trace not replaced at the Mega, and each of the nine stones unmarked.
