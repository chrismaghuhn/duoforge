5ag. **Mega batch 2: Swampertite, Metagrossite, Lucarionite Z with Aura Guard, and Froslassite** (the outlier Mega block of the owner's wind-down; per 100 teams in `usage.json`: Metagross-Mega 6.135, Lucario-Mega-Z 4.990, Froslass-Mega 4.573, Swampert-Mega 3.625). Four stones on the Mega-by-stone model of 5p, and one engine rule. No public value, no new state, no view change, no version bump (the library version stays what main has).
   - **The pin.** The stones carry no Champions rule (`data/mods/champions/items.ts`: `inherit: true, isNonstandard: null`). Each Mega forme has one ability (`data/pokedex.ts`): Swampert-Mega Swift Swim (G22), Metagross-Mega Tough Claws (step 11), Froslass-Mega Snow Warning (5d), Lucario-Mega-Z **Aura Guard** (`data/abilities.ts:310-319`: `onSourceModifyDamage`, `if (move.flags['contact']) return this.chainModify(0.5)`, `flags: {breakable: 1}`, tagged `isNonstandard: "Future"`, which the pinned validator accepts for the Mega forme; the Champions mod has no entry). Base abilities: Swampert Torrent, Metagross Clear Body (G30), Lucario Inner Focus (G22), Froslass **Cursed Body** (step G27; the other, Snow Cloak, is not marked and is not needed).
   - **Aura Guard** (`turn.c`, `dfi_get_damage`): one more modifier of the ModifyDamage chain, 2048/4096, for a hit with the contact flag on a holder of the ability. It is the target's one ability, so a hit has at most one of Solid Rock, Multiscale and Aura Guard (`DFI_MODIFY_DAMAGE_MAX` stays 6) and the commutation test of G34 covers it: of the 191 subsets that one hit can have, **113 commute and 78 do not** (G35: 143, 85, 58); the new refusals are the Expert Belt with Glaive Rush or with a Friend Guard partner at an Aura Guard holder, like Multiscale's. `breakable` matters only with Mold Breaker, which is not marked. Aura Guard is an engine row of the generator (`ENGINE_ROWS`, fact text checked by `MEGA2_ABILITY_FACTS`; handler NONE, 153 unmodelled abilities), and the converter knows its modifier (`modify_damage_values`, the exclusive group of the target's ability).
   - **Marks.** The items Swampertite, Metagrossite, Lucarionite Z and Froslassite (handler NONE already) and the ability Aura Guard. The support of a pair is that of its Mega ability (5p). Lucarionite (the other Lucario stone, Adaptability) stays unmarked: nobody asked for it.
   - **Tests.** `duoforge.forms.mega_stones, duoforge.abilities.aura_guard` (new, white-box): the marks, the Mega of each pair and its ability, Aura Guard as the only ability of one forme, `dfi_mods_commute` against a brute force over the 191 subsets, and the engine's refusal itself (the Close Combat of `mb2_aura_guard_chain` is played with Life Orb, Reflect and Aura Guard, and with a Friend Guard on the Reflect user; with an Expert Belt in place of the Life Orb it is E_UNSUPPORTED). `test_trace_to_c.py` (`test_aura_guard_ties_mega_batch_2`) and `test_gen_closure.py` pin the converter and the generator side.
