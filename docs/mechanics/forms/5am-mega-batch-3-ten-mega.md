5am. **G43, Mega batch 3: ten Mega Stones, mark only** (Dragoninite, Glimmoranite, Blazikenite, Absolite Z, Raichunite X, Lopunnite, Alakazite, Meowsticite for both Meowstic formes, Scizorite and Galladite). Mark only on the Mega-by-stone model of 5p, as Mega batch 2 (5ag): no rule, no engine row, no handler, no state, no public value, no version bump. The ten stones were handler NONE rows already, so `pool_tables` (canonical sha256 `2515ab6a…`, `gen_closure.py --pool --check` ok) and the POOL fingerprint are unchanged; the change is the manifest, the tests and the battles.
   - **The pin.** Each stone is a `megaStone` item of `data/items.ts` (Absolite Z 46-57, Alakazite 215-226, Blazikenite 568-579, Dragoninite 1511-1522, Galladite 2374-2385, Glimmoranite 2508-2519, Lopunnite 3469-3480, Meowsticite 3927-3942, Raichunite X 5035-5046, Scizorite 5530-5541) and `inherit: true` in `data/mods/champions/items.ts` (14-17, 46-49, 110-113, 226-229, 366-369, 394-397, 538-541, 626-629, 762-765, 838-841). The Mega formes (`data/pokedex.ts`): Dragonite-Mega 3365-3379, Glimmora-Mega 18587-18600, Blaziken-Mega 5117-5131, Absol-Mega-Z 6722-6736, Raichu-Mega-X 837-851, Lopunny-Mega 7932-7945, Alakazam-Mega 1616-1630, Meowstic-M-Mega 12743-12759, Meowstic-F-Mega 12760-12776, Scizor-Mega 4400-4413, Gallade-Mega 8661-8675. The Champions mod has no entry for any of the abilities below.
   - **The support.** The Mega abilities were already marked: Multiscale, Adaptability, Speed Boost, Sharpness, Electric Surge, Scrappy, Trace, Technician and Inner Focus. The base abilities the recorded battles use are marked and legal for their base formes (`data/pokedex.ts`): Inner Focus (Dragonite, Alakazam), Toxic Debris (Glimmora), Speed Boost (Blaziken), Justified (Absol), Lightning Rod (Raichu), Limber (Lopunny), Prankster (Meowstic) and Competitive (Meowstic-F), Technician (Scizor), Sharpness (Gallade).
   - **What the Mega changes, and why it needs no rule.** The types of an evolved member come from its forme row (`dfi_forme_of`). Absol-Mega-Z is Dark/Ghost (base Dark) and Lopunny-Mega is Normal/Fighting (base Normal); the others keep their base types. The weights differ (Dragonite-Mega 290 kg against 210 kg); Low Kick and Grass Knot read the Mega forme's weight (`src/combat/turn.c (dfi_get_damage)`), as they did for every earlier Mega. Trace (Alakazam-Mega, both Meowstic-Megas) copies a foe's ability after the Mega Evolution along the existing path (AC1, `dfi_trace`); the foes of these battles hold marked abilities only. Multiscale (full HP only), Inner Focus (no flinch) and the rest are the engine's existing rules.
   - **Evidence.** Eleven recorded POOL battles, `g43_<stone>` (`g43_meowsticite_m` and `g43_meowsticite_f`), every gendered Pokemon's gender stated in its paste, each holder Mega Evolving on turn 1: `g43_dragoninite` (Dragonite (M); the first Fake Out hits it at full HP right after the Mega, Multiscale's halving checked by the HP lines; the second one hits it below full HP); `g43_glimmoranite` (Glimmora (F); Sludge Bomb after the Mega, Adaptability's STAB); `g43_blazikenite` (Blaziken (M); Speed Boost raises its Speed at the end of the turn); `g43_absolitez` (Absol (M); the Mega-Z forme is Ghost, so the two Normal Fake Outs show `-immune`, and Night Slash is Sharpness's); `g43_raichunitex` (Raichu (F); `-fieldstart Electric Terrain [from] ability: Electric Surge`); `g43_lopunnite` (Lopunny (F); Quick Attack hits Gholdengo, a Ghost type, under Scrappy); `g43_alakazite` (Alakazam (F); Trace copies Raichu's Lightning Rod after the Mega); `g43_meowsticite_m` (Meowstic (M)) and `g43_meowsticite_f` (Meowstic-F (F)); Trace copies Incineroar's Intimidate after the Mega; `g43_scizorite` (Scizor (M); Bullet Punch under Technician, HP lines); `g43_galladite` (Gallade (M); Inner Focus, the two Fake Outs in turn 1 do not stop its Close Combat).
