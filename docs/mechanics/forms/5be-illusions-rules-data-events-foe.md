5be. **I2a, Illusion's rules, data, events and foe view (decision 0026, amended by I2)**. Milestone (a): the Illusion row of the pool data (the engine row `illusion`; its manifest mark stays uncommitted until the encoders refuse Illusion, step I2b). Milestone (b): the disguise at switch-in from `party_order` (decision 0026 section 2: the last non-fainted member to the right of the holder), the break on a damaging move hit by any user, an ally included (`DamagingHit` of the pin, `sim/battle-actions.ts:1118-1130`: not recoil, Life Orb, Rocky Helmet, residual or hazards), the drop on a faint (the faint line names the disguise), the unbroken switch-out, Trace's notrace (Illusion now carries the flag too, so the generator's expectation becomes `{Trace, Illusion}`), and the fold of the foe's events. The public value of 0026 section 6 this step carries is the event kind `DUOFORGE_EVENT_ILLUSION_END` = 46 (both buffers, at the break). The SWITCH event keeps its meaning in both buffers: the foe's copy names the shown roster index, the owner's the true one, carried by an internal side channel that the projection strips. Milestone (c): the foe's observation (occupant, status and HP from the fold, the disguise row from `ill_override`) and bit 19 `ILLUSION_UP` on the owner's own side only (0018 is amended). Milestone (d): the converter's `replace` and `-end|Illusion` rules, and the owner's true identity from its recorded choice and the request data. The rest of 0026 (the public record mask, ILLUSION_POSSIBLE, the encoder refusal, the information tests) is I2b. No recorded battle counts toward the closure gate; the Transform clause waits for 0028.
   - **Tail rev 5.** The rev 4 part is unchanged (288 bytes); after it come 60 bytes: per side the Illusion state (18 bytes: `shown`, `override[4]`, `snapshot[9]`, `pending[4]`), per position two lane A bytes (`slot_pending`, `future_sight`), then 16 bytes of reserve. Nothing writes them: every byte is zero, and any other value is refused (`TAIL_SIDE` for the Illusion state, `TAIL_POSITION` for the position bytes, `TAIL_RESERVED` for the reserve). The state is 1357 bytes under the POOL kinds; schema 0x0503 is the only one accepted there, and 0x0403 is refused since rev 5. CLOSURE and TEAM_C are byte for byte unchanged.
   - **The public record grows.** The state array of `duoforge_public_state` holds the POOL state, so `DUOFORGE_VIEW_STATE_MAX` is 1357 and the record is 1396 bytes (was 1336). This is the size of the canonical state, not a new field; decision 0026 section 3 says that no view struct grows, which the lead should weigh against this.
   - **The causes call (C only).** `duoforge_battle_public_causes` and `duoforge_batch_public_causes` write the mask of the causes that the player's view decides: `DUOFORGE_PUBLIC_CAUSE_VISIBLE_SLEEP` 1 and `_VISIBLE_CONFUSION` 2 (the test of `dfi_view_counter_support`, now one predicate with the refusal of `duoforge_battle_public`), `_ILLUSION_POSSIBLE` 4, which stays 0 until Illusion. A mask of 0 with a refusing record means another refusal.
   - **Evidence.** The byte sweep of `tests/test_pool_tail.c` covers all 348 tail bytes against the state model (`state_v3_model.py --pool-tail`); the in-memory cases cover the new fields. Targeted tests: `duoforge.state.pool_tail`, `duoforge.view`, `duoforge.view.causes` (sleep only, confusion only, both, neither, a bench sleep seen by its own side only, a PIVOT refusal with mask 0, hidden counters and RNG, the arguments, the batch for 1 to 16 workers), `duoforge.python.layout`, `replay` and `live`. Mutation checks, 9 of 9 caught: the record ignores the causes, the causes call drops the confusion bit, encode/decode/invariant each skipping one rev 5 byte, the reserve check skipping its last byte. Campaign: `diff_driver.py random`, seed 20261009, 50 battles over teams D and E of mega-batch2 with A (POOL): PASS 50, DIVERGENCE 0, UNSUPPORTED 0.
   - **Version.** No bump in this step; the MINOR is assigned at merge.
