5do. **The random target of issue 542: the recharge turn, the Outrage lock and Encore on a non-Ghost Curse (branch claude/project-thread-fxsdy2; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** The issue asked whether the engine or the converter was wrong. **The engine.** The converter rightly drops the draws of getActionSpeed (`sim/battle.ts:2644`, "target computed for priority"): they decide nothing. The draw the engine expected and the tape lacked is runMove's own (`sim/battle-actions.ts:222`), and Showdown makes none there when the user has Stalwart: getTarget returns the action's original target while it is active (`sim/battle.ts:2437-2447`, tracksTarget). Every one of the 130 cases, rerun on this branch, is one of three kinds. (a) **The recharge turn of a Stalwart user** (64, fz_138_78): the recharge action's location is the Pokemon's lastMoveTargetLoc (`sim/side.ts:675-689`, `sim/pokemon.ts:913`), resolveAction makes its original target (`sim/battle-queue.ts:263-273`), and runMove draws only when that Pokemon has left. The state keeps no such location, so whether the turn draws is not known: `dfi_run_recharge` now refuses the recharge turn of a Stalwart user (E_UNSUPPORTED) instead of drawing. (b) **Outrage (and every randomNormal move) of a Stalwart user** (36, fz_237_109): already refused by 5dn (k); no divergence remains. (c) **Encore into a non-Ghost Curse** (22, fz_291_32): fixed by 5dm (d); these pass, with eight more of the 130 that the fixes of 5dm and 5dn already cover. **Evidence.** The refusal stops its battle, so `duoforge.moves.recharge` pins it white-box (g17_hyper_beam after step 1: the recharge turn is played without Stalwart and refused with it); the test fails with the fix reverted. Rerun on this branch, the 130 cases: 30 pass, 100 stop with E_UNSUPPORTED (64 at a refused recharge turn, two of them at an earlier one than the fuzzing reported, 36 at 5dn (k)); none diverges. No reference battle changes. **Gap.** Playing these turns needs lastMoveTargetLoc in the state (for the recharge, the lockedmove and the first randomNormal use, whose original target is resolveAction's draw at queue time, which the converter would then keep); the foe's location is hidden, so the view and the rebuild would have to hide and sample it. That is a state and view decision, not made here.
