5ga. **-, The equal-Speed tie of the residual no-order groups (issues 630 and 633)** (HS block, id given by the HS on 2026-10-23; restructure window, branch `chris/competent-brahmagupta-c1d564`; design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-residual-noorder-tie-design.md`, PR #648; no new public value, draw site or residual kind). Pin: `sim/battle.ts:429-460` (`speedSort`), `:484-575` (`fieldEvent`), `data/conditions.ts:253-285` (`lockedmove`).
   - **The pin.** `fieldEvent('Residual')` sorts every handler once and shuffles each group of equal handlers as it is placed, all before the first handler runs. The no-order handlers sort last, the fastest Speed first; a group of one Speed holds every no-order handler of every Pokemon of that Speed (the counters of the turn, Ally Switch's volatile, `mustrecharge`, a charge, and `lockedmove`, the only callback among them).
   - **The engine.** Phase 2 of `dfi_residual_events_run` (`dfi_residual_order`, `src/combat/turn.c`) ends with a third `dfi_residual_sort` pass over the rest of the list. It draws, with `SPEED_TIE` (site 1, the same `speedSort` shuffle as every other residual tie, in `ps_trace.js` and `trace_to_c.py` too) and the group's size as the bound, exactly the groups with two callbacks or more: two lockedmoves of one Speed. Groups with fewer are not drawn there (option b of the design): the conversion drops their draw, and the lone Protect / stall pair keeps its draw at a battle end (`dfi_residual_pair_draws`), so every committed tape stays valid. Phase 6 (`dfi_residual_noorder`) runs a drawn group in its drawn order (`dfi_noorder_drawn`): each lock at its own entry (a marked lock ends with the fatigue and no faint point, any other lock's onResidual is followed by a faint point), a position's other no-order handlers at its first entry among them. Every other group runs as before, by position in Speed order.
   - **The unknown pre-sort order.** A Pokemon's volatiles are added in an order the state does not hold. `dfi_residual_order_ambiguous` now treats a lock and a counter of one Pokemon as two entries whose order can show (`dfi_residual_distinct`), and no longer assumes a lock is older than every counter (`dfi_residual_is_old_ending`: a lock started this turn is younger than a Helping Hand its holder got before it moved). A drawn group whose holders also have counters is then refused with that reason; two locks alone are played.
   - **Refusals removed.** Issue 633 case 1 for the drawn groups: a battle that ends at a faint point inside a group of two lockedmoves of one Speed (an equal-Speed neighbour at a battle end) is played; the handlers after the faint point stay unrun, as in the pin.
   - **A silent divergence of main, fixed.** A lock holder whose faint is queued and not yet shown (a Perish Song end earlier in the residual) keeps its handlers in the pin, which skips only a fainted holder (`sim/battle.ts:514-517`): its lock's onResidual runs and the faint point after it shows the queued faints there. The engine's lock handler skipped any holder at 0 HP, so the faint lines came after the `upkeep` line instead. Now the faint point is run for it (`dfi_noorder_lock`); the end of a marked lock confuses nobody at 0 HP (`addVolatile`, `sim/pokemon.ts:1975`) and the faint clears the lock. Found by the fuzz run 6631 (8 battles, identical on main); recorded battle `noorder_lock_pending_faint`.
   - **Refusals that stay or are new** (`E_UNSUPPORTED`, the battle unchanged):
     - an undrawn group (fewer than two callbacks) with an equal-Speed neighbour at a battle-ending faint point: the pin draws, the conversion drops the draw, the order is not known (issue 633 case 1 for these groups);
     - a holder with a lock and a counter that keeps at its own battle-ending faint point (issue 633 case 2: which faint point came first is not drawn);
     - in a drawn group, a battle end while a standing Pokemon of the group has both a lock and counters, or has run some of its counters but not all (the engine runs a position's counters together), and a pending faint at a counter that keeps of a position with two counters or more in a drawn group;
     - new: the Protect / stall pair draw when an equal-Speed lock holder stands (a bigger group; before, the engine drew it while the conversion's `lock_counter_tie` dropped it, a silent divergence), and when phase 2 drew a slower no-order group (the pin draws the pair first: the tape's order).
   - **Evidence.**
     - Recorded battles against the pin `b2cb775b` (pool): `noorder_tie_mirror_outrage` (Garchomp and Salamence at Speed 122 lock into Outrage; DIVERGENCE on main at step 1, the tape one draw short), `noorder_tie_perish_outrage` (Perish Song ends side 1's last two Pokemon while both hold fresh Outrage locks at one Speed; the first lock's faint point ends the battle; UNSUPPORTED on main at step 7), `noorder_tie_both_marked` (both holders put to sleep before their locked turn, both locks end with the fatigue in the drawn order, Garchomp's line and confusion draw first; DIVERGENCE on main at step 1). All three PASS.
     - White-box: `duoforge.state.pool_lockfam_sleep` scenario `phase6_equal_speed_refused` (its name from #632) is played now: over 16 seeds both orders occur (7 with Kangaskhan's fatigue before Annihilape's battle-ending faint point, 9 with the battle ended first). `duoforge.combat.residual_order` has lock examples and 10,000 random lock lists (own generator, so the 40,000 plain lists are unchanged): every unrefused list keeps its callback order under every arrangement and draw.
     - Mutants, each killed: the third pass removed (`pool_lockfam_sleep`, `diff_replay`); phase 6 ignoring the drawn order (`pool_lockfam_sleep`, `diff_replay`); the group's callbacks counted from the position's own entry instead of the group's start (`pool_lockfam_sleep`; a bug the scenario found during the work); a lock and a counter of one Pokemon not distinct (`residual_order`); a lock old-ending again (`residual_order`).
     - P2: the 2,808 executions of the 1,241 committed battles have unchanged digests. Perft and `BENCH_SHA256` are unchanged: their workloads have no residual with two lockedmoves (0 counted with an instrumented build) and none of the changed refusals.
   - **Open.** The converter's `residual-tie-callbacks` refusal for a group with a lock callback and flinch or stall (issue 554) is a tool change for the content or fuzz lane; the engine refuses most of those groups through the arrangement test anyway, since their pre-sort order is not stored. The undrawn-group refusals above need the conversion to keep those draws (option a of the design). Ally Switch's volatile that keeps (its second residual) is not a faint point in phase 6, as before this change.
