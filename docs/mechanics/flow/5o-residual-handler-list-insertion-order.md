5o. **The residual handler list and the insertion-order refusal (#154, the AWS triage of 2026-10-03).** The engine's residual list is now the pin's `fieldEvent` list (`sim/battle.ts:484-523`): per active Pokemon its status, its volatiles (the duration counters, Heal Block at order 20, Throat Chop at 22, Encore at 16), its item, then Grassy Terrain's heal, sorted once by `speedSort`, whose swaps move tied entries, so every entry decides the pre-shuffle order of a tied group, the entries that never act included. The order in which a Pokemon's volatiles were added is not stored, so the engine lists them in one fixed order and refuses (`E_UNSUPPORTED`, never a guess) the lists in which a different order could change an outcome (`src/combat/residual_order.h`: a cheap test, then an exact one that runs the sort over every order of the volatiles and every value of every draw; `tests/test_residual_order.c` proves it by brute force against its own model of the reference's sort).
   - **Measured rates** of that refusal (battles with a refusal, local runs under the machine lock unless noted): the mirror campaign over the Throat Chop, Heal Block and Encore teams 1 of 1200; the watchdog's first round on 6f47f3e, g9-encore, 2 of 600; a further Encore-team campaign (seed 9900000) 4 of 1200, all four in the DD pairing; the DD pairing overall (the Encore team D against itself) 7 of 225, about 3.1 percent; every other pairing 0, among them D against the registry Encore team (0 of 200); and a registry-mix-size random run (44 registry teams in 500 random pairings, seed 9810000) 0 of 3000, about 51,000 steps. The domain gap of the watchdog run (16 of 28,610 samples) is exactly the two refused battles' samples, which the domain check does not count as agreed.
   - **The reason it is rare.** A list is refused only when a Pokemon holds volatile handlers of two different sort keys (a Protect-style counter next to Encore, Heal Block or Throat Chop) and a group of equal-speed callbacks that draw sits at or after the earliest of those orders and is moved by the sort's swaps. That needs two such Pokemon at one Speed: in practice a mirror of a team with several Encore users.
   - **Why the insertion order is not stored.** The refusal ends an environment of a learner's training; at these rates the loss is the Encore mirrors only, and the storage (a tail revision, the codec, the converter and the view) is a larger change than the loss. HauptSession agreed (2026-10-03).
   - **The fix kept on file,** should training ever use such mirrors: one byte per position in the POOL tail that holds the rank of each volatile class (counters, Heal Block, Throat Chop, Encore) as it was added, which the list builder would read instead of the fixed order; the exact test would then be needed only for lists whose ranks tie. It needs the next tail revision, so it would travel with the next mechanic that needs one.
