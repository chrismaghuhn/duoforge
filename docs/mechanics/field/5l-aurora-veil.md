# 5l. G20, Aurora Veil

- Area: field
- Step: G20
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g20

5l. **G20, Aurora Veil** (2.28 percent of the replay decisions, decision 0018 tier 1; the first mechanic that fills `aurora_veil_turns` of the tail, rev 2 as it is). Aurora Veil keeps a handler id of its own (`AURORA_VEIL`: its `onTry` and the side condition that no column holds), and the turn code implements it; it is marked with the view bit 3.
   - **The pin.** `data/moves.ts:830-877` (the Champions mod does not override it): Ice, Status, PP 20, flags snatch and metronome, `sideCondition: 'auroraveil'`. `onTry` (:840) returns `field.isWeather(['hail', 'snowscape'])`: the move fails with `-fail` and `[still]` outside snow (hail is not in the format; nothing suppresses a weather here, Cloud Nine and Air Lock are unmarked). `durationCallback` (:842-847): 8 turns when the user holds Light Clay, else 5; `addSideCondition` does not restart an active condition, so a second cast fails the same way. `onAnyModifyDamage` (:848-860) for a target of the holder's side other than the attacker returns without an effect when the side has Reflect and the move is Physical or Light Screen and it is Special, and otherwise, unless the hit is a critical hit (or `infiltrates`, which no marked move or ability has), chains 2732/4096 with two Pokemon per side. So a hit is reduced once, by Aurora Veil or by the screen of its category. `onSideStart` and `onSideEnd` print `-sidestart|side|move: Aurora Veil` and `-sideend|...`; the end is in the residual at order 26, sub-order 10, after Reflect (1), Light Screen (2) and Tailwind (5). The generator reads the `onTry` and the whole condition text and refuses a changed pin.
   - **State.** The tail's `aurora_veil_turns` of the side (0 to 8; nothing clears it but its end, so it outlives the snow). The residual counts it down with the other three side conditions; the two sides' same condition ending in one residual are ordered by the existing `SPEED_TIE` shuffle (decision 0007 section 6).
   - **Event (new public value, the blanket OK of 2026-10-03).** `DUOFORGE_SIDE_AURORA_VEIL` = 4, the amount of `SIDE_START` and `SIDE_END`.
   - **View.** Decision 0018: the side's `aurora_veil_turns`, public, both viewers, supported bit 3; the rows are derived from the protocol lines (`tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py`) and compared after every step (`duoforge.state.pool_g20`).
   - **Tables.** The special id AURORA_VEIL (27, after G15's EXPANDING_FORCE and G19's GLAIVE_RUSH) is new, so UNMODELED moves to 28: the pool tables, their hash and the POOL fingerprints change (CLOSURE and TEAM_C do not). The merger of a parallel step that also adds a handler id renumbers through the generator.
   - **Evidence.** Six recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; seeds picked by predicates): `g20_aurora_veil_a` (cast on turn 2, hits of both categories and a spread move, the screen outlives the snow, its end line), `_clay` (Light Clay: eight turns, a critical hit that is not reduced), `_fail` (no snow: Drizzle replaced the snow; Snowscape; success; a second cast fails), `_screens` (Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil on one side: one reduction per hit, a critical hit under the screens), `_pair_a` and `_pair_b` (both sides cast on one turn, the two end lines in both orders of the shuffle). The harness records `aurora_veil` in a state only while a side has it, so no older trace changed; the converter keeps the end tie like the other side conditions and drops the ModifyDamage tie, which cannot apply two reductions to a hit. Mutation checks (no snow test, a recast, Light Clay ignored, a wrong duration, no modifier, a reduced critical hit, a double reduction with a screen, no end line, no end-tie draw, the end order, a wrong side constant, no failure line, an unfilled and a swapped view) each fail a test; leaving Aurora Veil out of the residual's sort list changes nothing that shows (duration handlers tie without a draw), so that one is an equivalent mutant.
