# 5bf. G55, the rock items: Damp Rock, Heat Rock, Smooth Rock, Icy Rock and Terrain Extender

- Area: field
- Step: G55
- Tests: duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.state.pool_setup, duoforge.items.duration_items, duoforge.state.pool_weather, duoforge.state.pool_terrain, duoforge.state.invariants, duoforge.state.pool_g49, duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data

5bf. **G55, the rock items: Damp Rock, Heat Rock, Smooth Rock, Icy Rock and Terrain Extender** (decision 0015 section 4, step G55; the duration rows that step G49 (5ao) left out). No new public value, no event kind, no view change, no version bump. The public `weather_turns` and `terrain_turns` already allow 8 (decision 0018, section 6 of the view extension).
   - **The rule.** A weather or terrain lasts 8 turns when its setter holds the lengthening item at the moment the field starts, else 5: Damp Rock for rain (Rain Dance and Drizzle), Heat Rock for sun (Sunny Day and Drought), Smooth Rock for sand (Sandstorm and Sand Stream), Icy Rock for snow (Snowscape and Snow Warning), and Terrain Extender for each of the four terrains (Electric, Grassy, Psychic and Misty, by move or by surge). The setter is the move's user or the holder of the ability, since `Field.setWeather` and `setTerrain` pass it as the source of the durationCallback (`sim/field.ts:41-42`, `:78-81`, `:147-149`). The item is read when the field starts. A Knock Off before the setting takes the rock first (`-enditem ... [from] move: Knock Off`), so the field lasts 5.
   - **The pin.** `data/conditions.ts` has `source?.hasItem('damprock'|'heatrock'|'smoothrock'|'icyrock')` in the durationCallbacks of Rain Dance, Sunny Day, Sandstorm and Snowscape (lines 481, 551, 633 and 671; each `duration: 5` and `return 8`). `data/moves.ts` has `source?.hasItem('terrainextender')` in the durationCallbacks of Electric Terrain, Grassy Terrain, Misty Terrain and Psychic Terrain (lines 4511, 7687, 12165 and 14109). The five items are data rows (`data/items.ts`, `1261`, `2841`, `3010`, `5820` and `6283`). `data/mods/champions/` overrides none of them nor any of these callbacks. `tools/datagen/gen_closure.py` checks the texts (`G55_FACTS`, `check_g55_items`, and the existing `WEATHER_FACTS`, `G32_WEATHER_FACTS` and `G25_FACTS`); `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` (`checkG55`) runs the pinned callbacks with each item.
   - **The engine.** `dfi_weather_rock` in `src/combat/field/weather.h` (the rock of a weather) and `dfi_field_start_turns` in `src/combat/field/duration.h` (8 or 5 from the setter's item), used at the four sites that set a weather or terrain (the two move sites of `dfi_run_move` and the two ability sites of `dfi_entry_ability`). Trick Room keeps 5. The state bound is a new internal constant `DFI_FIELD_TURNS_EXTENDED_MAX` (8, `src/state/battle_internal.h`). `DFI_FIELD_TURNS_MAX` stays 5 for Trick Room: raising it, as the step's brief first read, would have set Trick Room to 8 as well. The invariant (`src/state/invariants.c`) and `tools/state_model/state_v3_model.py` (`FIELD_TURNS_EXTENDED_MAX`) use 8 for weather and terrain only.
   - **The tracker.** `python/duoforge_live/tracker.py` (`_extended_field`, `FIELD_TURNS_EXTENDED`, from PR #268) already sets 8 for the same items. Its tests agree with the recorded battles: `duoforge.python.replay`, `duoforge.python.live` and their unit tests pass unchanged, with no tracker change in this step.
   - **Marks and tables.** The five items are marked in `src/data/support_manifest.c` with handler NONE, as ENGINE_ROWS of `gen_closure.py` and `pool_families.js`, with no family. Regenerated `pool_tables.{h,c}`: the header is unchanged, the `.c` has canonical hash `033bf62d382f032ac9e5b68e9ccc16585f1101ca074cf5b7786b754d9c40d894` (the POOL table hash of `tests/test_pool_tables.c` and `tools/state_model/state_v3_model.py`), and the POOL fingerprints are `KP 136e218e...` and `KPD baa3da3a...` (`tests/test_pool_setup.c`). Unmodelled items go from 30 to 25.
   - **Evidence.** Fourteen recorded POOL battles, `g55_*`, all with genders stated (`tests/reference/specs/g55_*.json`, traces under `tests/reference/traces/`, `"max_steps": 9`, the field's end shown in each). Ability setters: `g55_damp_rock_drizzle` (Politoed (M)), `g55_heat_rock_drought` (Torkoal (M)), `g55_smooth_rock_sand_stream` (Hippowdon (M)), `g55_icy_rock_snow_warning` (Abomasnow (M)), `g55_terrain_extender_electric_surge` (Pincurchin (M)), `g55_terrain_extender_grassy_surge` (Rillaboom (M)) and `g55_terrain_extender_psychic_surge` (Indeedee-F (F)).
