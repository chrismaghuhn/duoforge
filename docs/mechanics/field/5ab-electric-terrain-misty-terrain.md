# 5ab. G25, Electric Terrain and Misty Terrain

- Area: field
- Step: G25
- Tests: duoforge.mechanics.electric_misty_terrain

5ab. **G25, Electric Terrain and Misty Terrain** (the second and third terrain after Psychic Terrain, on the path of step G15: Electric Terrain 1.10 percent of the replay decisions, decision 0018 tier 1; Misty Terrain tier 4). The step marks the two terrain moves, the entry setter Electric Surge, the two seeds, and the two moves that read the terrain: Rising Voltage and Terrain Pulse. Tables: `ELECTRIC_TERRAIN`, `MISTY_TERRAIN`, `RISING_VOLTAGE` and `TERRAIN_PULSE` are new named handler ids 28 to 31 (`G25_HANDLERS` of the generator; UNMODELED moves to 32), the terrain family column has the codes 3 (Electric) and 4 (Misty), which are the engine's `DFI_TERRAIN_*`, Electric Surge is a terrain setter and Electric Seed and Misty Seed are `ENGINE_ROWS` items, so the POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step. Misty Surge is not in the pool. **Public values** (additive, approved by the owner (2026-10-03)): `DUOFORGE_FIELD_ELECTRIC_TERRAIN` = 4 and `DUOFORGE_FIELD_MISTY_TERRAIN` = 5, the detail of a FIELD_START and FIELD_END event (own commit); the terrain values 3 and 4 and the view bits 5 and 32 are the ones of decision 0018 and are now produced; the line `-activate|target|move: X Terrain` that a status refused by a terrain shows is the generic ACTIVATE event with the terrain move as its cause (no new value).
   - **The pin.** `data/moves.ts`: Electric Terrain 4497-4555, Misty Terrain 12151-12208 (the Champions mod overrides neither; both are `terrain:` moves with a condition of 5 turns and `durationCallback` 8 for Terrain Extender, which is not marked), Rising Voltage 15137-15156, Terrain Pulse 19265-19301; `data/items.ts` Electric Seed 1799-1821 and Misty Seed 4200-4222, which are Grassy Seed (2595-2614) for their terrain and Defense or Special Defense (the generator checks the text, `SEED_PAIRS`); `data/abilities.ts` Electric Surge 1179-1187 (`onStart` calls `setTerrain`, the same pattern as Psychic Surge). The generator reads the whole callbacks of both terrains, of Rising Voltage and of Terrain Pulse as `G25_FACTS` (whitespace aside) and `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` calls the pinned handlers (`checkTerrains`): the modifiers, the refusals and which of them print a line, the durations, the seeds, Terrain Pulse's types and power, Rising Voltage's power.
   - **setTerrain** (`sim/field.ts:130-157`, called from moveHit, `sim/battle-actions.ts:1252-1255`): the same terrain is not restarted and the move fails (`-fail`, as for the weather moves), a different one replaces it for 5 turns without a line for the old one, `-fieldstart|move: X Terrain` with `[from] ability: Electric Surge` and `[of]` for the ability, and then `TerrainChange` runs the seeds (`dfi_terrain_change`). The ability and the seeds run as for Psychic Surge and Psychic Seed.
   - **Damage.** Electric Terrain: x5325/4096 for a grounded user's Electric move (onBasePowerPriority 6, the slot of Grassy Terrain and Psychic Terrain: one terrain at a time), Misty Terrain: x0.5 (2048/4096) for a Dragon move at a grounded target (the defender's grounding), one chained modifier with the others. Rising Voltage: `basePowerCallback`, 140 for a grounded target in Electric Terrain whoever the user is (the move's own 5325 follows); Terrain Pulse: `onModifyMove` doubles the power in any terrain for a grounded user and `onModifyType` gives the terrain's type (Electric, Grass, Fairy, Psychic) for a grounded user. The user's and the target's grounding are the Flying type alone, as for Expanding Force (Levitate, Air Balloon, Ingrain, Gravity, Magnet Rise, Smack Down and Roost are UNMODELED rows; `test_pool_rows.py`).
   - **Status.** `Pokemon.setStatus` (`sim/pokemon.ts:1684`): the already-has check and the type immunities first, then the SetStatus event. `Battle.resolvePriority` gives a speed only to the effects of a Pokemon (`sim/battle.ts:1001-1003`) and `comparePriority` sorts by speed before the sub-order (:404-411), so the terrain's `onSetStatus` (the field's handler, speed 0) runs after Thermal Exchange and Flower Veil, which refuse first and end the event; the engine checks the terrain after them and before the sleep counter is drawn. Electric Terrain refuses sleep for a grounded Pokemon, Misty Terrain every status, both by returning false; the `-activate` line shows for a move's own status (Electric: `effect.effectType === 'Move' && !effect.secondaries`; Misty: `effect.status`, which a move whose status is a secondary does not have) and for neither a secondary, an ability (Poison Touch) nor an item. Misty Terrain's `onTryAddVolatile` stops confusion on a grounded Pokemon (the line for a move without secondaries; the engine adds only a secondary's confusion, so no line). Yawn, Rest, the Electric Terrain Yawn refusal and every other source are UNMODELED rows.
   - **The lines.** Misty Terrain's `onFieldEnd` prints `-fieldend|Misty Terrain` with no "move: " (`data/moves.ts` 12198-12200 at the pin; the others have it); the converter maps it, and only it, to the Misty end event. The converter's state table has the terrain values 3 and 4.
   - **What stays unmarked** (each reads the terrain by a callback of its own and so is an UNMODELED row; `test_electric_misty_terrain.c`): Surge Surfer, Mimicry, Seed Sower, Grass Pelt, Terrain Extender, Ice Spinner, Misty Explosion. **Steel Roller** is a handler since step G34 and ends Grassy and Psychic Terrain there; this step extends its terrain end to Electric and Misty Terrain (`dfi_clear_terrain`: the end line is `-fieldend|move: Electric Terrain` and, without the "move: ", `-fieldend|Misty Terrain`), and gives Terrain Pulse the type of each of the four terrains. The Grassy Terrain and Psychic Terrain moves stay UNMODELED rows too (the terrain comes from the setters; not part of this step).
   - **A defect fixed on the way: the -ate boost of a Weather Ball.** The `-ate` abilities leave the moves of the `noModifyType` list of their `onModifyType` alone (`judgment`, `multiattack`, `naturalgift`, `revelationdance`, `technoblast`, `terrainpulse`, `weatherball`; `data/abilities.ts`), and only a type that the ability itself changed gets `typeChangerBoosted` and the 4915/4096. The engine skipped the type change for Weather Ball but still gave the boost when the weather's type equalled the ability's (a Refrigerate holder's Weather Ball in snow: Ice). `dfi_ate_excluded` (Weather Ball and Terrain Pulse) now guards both places. The regression battle is `g25_weather_ball_refrigerate` (Aurorus with Refrigerate, Abomasnow's Snow Warning); the mutation that takes the guard out turns `conformance_pool_data` red.
   - **A second defect, found by the campaign: Lightning Rod and the type of the move.** `getMoveTargets` runs its `RedirectTarget` event (`sim/pokemon.ts:829-831`) after `ModifyType` (`sim/battle-actions.ts:430-438`), so the redirection of Lightning Rod looks at the type the move has when it is used: a Terrain Pulse in Electric Terrain is Electric and is drawn to a Lightning Rod holder (Special Attack +1, `-activate|holder|ability: Lightning Rod`, the move line retargeted). The engine read the table type (Normal). `dfi_move_type_now` is the one function of the type (Struggle, the -ate abilities, Weather Ball, Terrain Pulse) and both the redirection and the hit steps call it; the four cases of the campaign are in the corpus (`fz_2501_{26,74,90,228}_prefix`: a mismatched target, the missing Special Attack stage, a later fainted holder and the invariant that the wrong target broke).
   - **Evidence**: eight recorded POOL battles (genders stated, seeds by predicate; the eighth, `g25_steel_roller_terrains` (seed `0x2500`), came with the re-sync onto G34: Electric Surge's terrain ended by Aggron's Steel Roller, Misty Terrain set by Sylveon and ended by the next one with its line without "move: ", the third one failing without a terrain): `g25_electric_surge_voltage` (Electric Surge sets the terrain at the start with its ability line, Rising Voltage from a grounded user at a grounded and at an airborne target, the move's fail when the terrain is up, Hypnosis refused to a grounded target with the Electric Terrain line and a flying one put to sleep, the end after five turns), `g25_electric_seed` (a seed holder that switches in while the terrain is up), `g25_misty_terrain` (Misty Terrain with its seed, Dragon Claw at a grounded target before the terrain and halved after it, a Dragon move at a Fairy, Will-O-Wisp refused, the end line), `g25_terrain_pulse_psychic_misty` (Psychic, then Fairy), `g25_terrain_pulse_electric` (Electric, super effective), `g25_misty_hurricane` (Hurricane's confusion at grounded targets in Misty Terrain: the secondary's roll succeeds and no confusion starts), `g25_weather_ball_refrigerate` (the defect). 163 pool battles and 880 stops (156 and 838 before: six per new battle, counted by hand from `tiebreak_ref.h`: 42 added, none removed), 82 marked moves (78 before), `duoforge.mechanics.electric_misty_terrain` (the terrain after every step against the field lines, both players, with the extension zero; the rows are derived from the traces by `test_trace_to_c.py`), the ability-family test (Terrain Pulse and Weather Ball are left alone by every `-ate` ability, three terrain setters), the generator tests (`TerrainFacts`, the seeds, the handler order). Mutation of the engine rules, 21 edits (the terrain's boost, Misty's factor and its grounded test, the doublings of Rising Voltage and Terrain Pulse, Terrain Pulse's type, the sleep refusal for the airborne and the paralysis instead of sleep, the all-status refusal and its -activate line, the failing of the same terrain, the length of a terrain, the seed's stat, the field detail, the end line, Electric Surge's terrain, the -ate guard of Weather Ball, Lightning Rod's type, the confusion refusal): 20 turn at least one of `conformance_pool_data`, `tiebreak_pool_data`, `pool_terrain` and the ability-family test red (the Lightning Rod one the corpus test), one stays green (below). A differential campaign (the two teams of the step against each other and teams A and B, 240 battles, seed 2501, POOL data kind, under the machine lock) gave 225 PASS and 15 DIVERGENCE of four signatures with the one cause above; fixed, the same campaign gave 240 of 240 PASS. Not exercised by a recorded battle, and so by no test but the reasoning above: Terrain Pulse's type for an airborne user (no learner is a Flying type; the mutation that drops the grounded test of its type stays green).
