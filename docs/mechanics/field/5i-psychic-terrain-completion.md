# 5i. G15, Psychic Terrain completion

- Area: field
- Step: G15
- Tests: duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data

5i. **G15, Psychic Terrain completion** (the two parts that blocked the most teams: Expanding Force 37 of 227, Psychic Seed 30). Pin (Champions mod, which overrides neither entry): **Expanding Force** `data/moves.ts:4943-4965`: Psychic, Special, 80 base power, accuracy 100, priority 0, flags `protect`, target `normal`; its own `onBasePower` (:4952-4957) is `chainModify(1.5)` and its `onModifyMove` (:4958-4962) sets `move.target = 'allAdjacentFoes'`, both only when `this.field.isTerrain('psychicterrain') && source.isGrounded()`. Order: `useMoveInner` (`sim/battle-actions.ts:431-443`) runs ModifyMove after `runMove`'s `getTarget` and BeforeMove, so the target is chosen and drawn as for a `normal` move (the request offers a target, an unavailable chosen foe is retargeted at random, a chosen ally is kept), and only then does the class change: `getRandomTarget` is called again (its draw only labels the move line, as for any spread move, and the converter drops it: `RANDOM_TARGET execute:allAdjacentFoes`), and `getMoveTargets` (`sim/pokemon.ts:791-810`) collects every adjacent foe, skipping the redirection of its default branch (Follow Me and Lightning Rod do not redirect the spread version, and do the single-target one). The spread modifier is 0.75 when more than one target is left (`sim/battle-actions.ts:551`, :1733-1737), so with one foe standing the move is a single-target hit with the same x1.5. Wide Guard (G7) and the protect check read `move.target` of the active move, so Wide Guard stops the spread version only. The x1.5 is the move's own BasePower handler, which `runEvent` puts first and sorts with the others by priority (0 for a handler that has none: after Helping Hand's 10, the items' 15 and the terrain's 6), the chain rounding once. `isGrounded` (`sim/pokemon.ts:2148-2160`): the engine reads the Flying type alone; Levitate, Eelevate, Air Balloon, Iron Ball, Gravity, Ingrain, Magnet Rise, Smack Down and Roost are UNMODELED rows (Telekinesis is not in the format), and no forme that learns Expanding Force is a Flying type, so a lifted user cannot occur (`test_pool_rows.py` checks both); the row of a user that is not grounded is therefore not a recordable case. **Psychic Seed** `data/items.ts:4903-4922` is Grassy Seed (`:2595-2614`) for Psychic Terrain and the Special Defense: `onSwitchInPriority: -1`, `onStart` (not `ignoringItem` and the terrain is up) and `onTerrainChange` (the terrain is up) call `useItem`, `boosts: { spd: 1 }`; the generator checks that text (`check_g15_facts`) and the engine reads one rule for both seeds (`dfi_terrain_seed`, `src/combat/field/terrain_seeds.h`): the entry abilities (priority 0, Psychic Surge included) run before the seeds of the same switch-in, a terrain started by the ability or the move runs every seed on the field in `eachEvent` order (a tie of equal-Speed holders is kept: the order of their lines), a seed that waits for the other terrain stays, and a Contrary holder gets -1. Electric Seed and Misty Seed stay UNMODELED (no Electric or Misty Terrain). The Psychic Terrain move itself is still an UNMODELED row (its condition has no column; Psychic Surge sets the terrain in every battle here). Tables: `EXPANDING_FORCE` is a new named handler id (`G15_HANDLERS` of the generator, after the weather ids and before UNMODELED, so the POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step) and Psychic Seed an `ENGINE_ROWS` item; both are marked. `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` pins that every modelled ability or item with a priority or Speed callback is Prankster, Unburden or Choice Scarf (an addition of the fainted-holder fix), and `test_pool_rows.py` that nothing that grounds or lifts a Pokemon is modelled. No new public value, no new event, no view change (the seed's lines are the Grassy Seed's: `-enditem` and `-boost` with `[from] item`). Evidence: eleven recorded POOL battles (`g15_ef_chain`: the spread in the terrain with the 0.75 modifier, the chain Twisted Spoon (15), Helping Hand (10), terrain (6), move (0), a Protect that stops one target of the spread, a resisted spread; `g15_ef_no_terrain`: the single-target move, not stopped by Wide Guard; `g15_ef_wide_guard`: the spread version stopped for both targets by both users, the next turn hit again; `g15_ef_follow_me`: Follow Me redirects the single-target move and not the spread one; `g15_ef_retarget_terrain` and `_plain`: the chosen target faints first, with one foe left the move is a single hit with or without the terrain; `g15_seed_surge`, `_terrain_starts`, `_tie` (the equal-Speed order of two seeds is drawn), `_contrary_unburden` and `_other_terrain`), the mutation checks (each of the following changes makes `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` fail and no other test: no x1.5, no spread class, Wide Guard reading the base class, the seed boosting the wrong stat, Psychic Seed not recognised, the seed not used at the switch-in; Follow Me reading the base class is an equivalent mutant: the only foe left is then the Follow Me user itself), `duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data`, a differential campaign (seed 151, 1500 battles over a Psychic Terrain team against itself, both reference teams and Team C: 1500 PASS, 5260 requests sampled for the domain check, all agreed) and the generator, row and fact tests.
