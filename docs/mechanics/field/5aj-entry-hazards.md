# 5aj. G37, the entry hazards

- Area: field
- Step: G37
- Tests: duoforge.mechanics.entry_hazards

5aj. **G37, the entry hazards** (Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web, and Toxic Debris; the tail's four layer fields of each side, rev 2, and `hazard_order`, rev 4; the owner's OK of 2026-10-03 for the four new public `SIDE` values). The four moves with the target class foeSide become modelled rows, Toxic Debris an engine row, and the switch-in handlers of the hazards run in the sorted order of the reference's `SwitchIn` event.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod overrides none of them; `tools/datagen/gen_closure.py` checks the whole texts, `G37_FACTS` and `G37_ABILITY_FACTS`). Stealth Rock `data/moves.ts:17814-17839`: `onSwitchIn` (unless Heavy-Duty Boots, which are Past in the format) damages `maxhp * 2 ** typeMod / 8`, the Rock effectiveness against the Pokemon's types clamped to [-6, 6], through `damage()` (floor, at least 1); grounded or not. Spikes `:17500-17525`: one to three layers (`onSideRestart` stops at 3), a grounded Pokemon takes `[0, 3, 4, 6][layers] * maxhp / 24`. Toxic Spikes `:19749-19788`: one or two layers; a grounded Pokemon: Poison type absorbs them (`-sideend|side|move: Toxic Spikes|[of] POKEMON`, the condition is gone), a Steel type takes nothing, else `trySetStatus('psn' or 'tox' with two layers, foe.active[0])` (no `-fail` or `-immune` line: the condition is the source effect, not a move; Flower Veil's `onAllySetStatus` blocks it silently). Sticky Web `:17935-17956`: a grounded Pokemon: `-activate|X|move: Sticky Web`, then `boost({spe: -1}, X, foe.active[0], the move)`: Clear Body, Flower Veil, Contrary, Defiant and Competitive react as to a move's own drop. Toxic Debris `data/abilities.ts:5104-5117` (`onDamagingHit`): a Physical hit adds a layer of Toxic Spikes to the attacker's side (the foe's side when an ally hit the holder) while it has fewer than two, `-activate|holder|ability: Toxic Debris` first, also for a holder the hit knocked out. The moves are `foeSide` status moves, accuracy true: `getMoveTargets` takes every foe position (`sim/pokemon.ts:796-807`), `tryMoveHit` runs `TryHitSide` (no Protect, no accuracy), and `getRandomTarget` names a random foe in the move line that the converter leaves out (the draw is `RANDOM_TARGET execute:foeSide`, dropped like a spread move's). A fifth layer, a second Stealth Rock or Sticky Web and a third Toxic Spikes fail (`-fail`, `[still]`).
   - **Switch-in order (the new part of the turn code).** `fieldEvent('SwitchIn')` collects the hazards of the entering Pokemon's side as handlers with the Pokemon as holder (`sim/battle.ts:502`), sorted by priority, speed (the shuffled order of `runSwitch`), sub-order (a side condition 4, before an ability's 7) and then `effectOrder`, the order in which the conditions were created (`:994-1000`, "they should activate in the order they were created"). The tail's `hazard_order` (rev 4: one byte per side, two bits per slot, a new kind appended, a further layer keeping its slot, an ended kind removed with the later ones moving down, a kind put up again last) is that order; `dfi_hazards_enter` runs the handlers in it, skipping one that an earlier handler removed (`:540-543`). A Pokemon with a hazard handler is a bearer for the draw of the speed tie of `runSwitch` (the harness counts the side's handlers, `tools/reference/ps_trace.js`, and the converter keeps the draw when two bearers tie, as for entry abilities). `fieldEvent` runs `faintMessages` after each handler (`:569-570`): a Pokemon that a hazard knocks out has its faint shown at once and no handler after it, neither another hazard nor its ability (found by the first campaign: Stealth Rock, then no Sticky Web line). After the runSwitch action the first Pokemon of the batch runs the `EmergencyExit` event when a hazard took it to half (`sim/battle.ts:2871-2874`).
   - **State and view.** The four layer fields and `hazard_order` (invariant: the first n slots are the kinds that are up, once each, the others 0). The view fills `stealth_rock`, `spikes`, `toxic_spikes` and `sticky_web` of both sides (supported bits 13, 30, 8 and 33); the order is not public.
   - **New public values (the owner's OK).** `DUOFORGE_SIDE_STEALTH_ROCK` 5, `_SPIKES` 6, `_TOXIC_SPIKES` 7 and `_STICKY_WEB` 8, the `amount` of `SIDE_START` and `SIDE_END` (a further layer of Spikes or Toxic Spikes is another `SIDE_START` of the same amount; a Toxic Spikes absorption is a `SIDE_END` with the absorbing Pokemon in `other`). No new cause: the damage is `CAUSE_MOVE` with the move's id (`[from] Stealth Rock` and `[from] Spikes` are the condition's own names, as Spiky Shield's), and Sticky Web's activation is an `ACTIVATE` with the same cause. The foeSide target class (15) and the four view fields existed.
   - **Tables.** No handler id: the move rows carry the side condition (the column holds the `SIDE` value) and the target class 15, so UNMODELED moves go down by four and the unmodelled abilities by one (Toxic Debris).
   - **Not in this step.** Hazard removal (Rapid Spin, Defog, Mortal Spin, Court Change, Tidy Up) and Ceaseless Edge and Stone Axe (a hazard after a hit, `onAfterHit`) stay unmarked, a later batch. Heavy-Duty Boots are Past, so no holder skips a hazard; Air Balloon (`isGrounded`), Magic Guard (it blocks `damage()` from a condition), Magic Bounce (`reflectable`) and Pressure (`mustpressure`) are unmarked, so no accepted battle has them: a step that marks one of them reads these lines. Corrosion (Glimmora's other ability) is unmarked too.
   - **Evidence.** Nine recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g37_*`, seeds by hand): `hazards_a` (the build-up of all four, the failures, flyers, a Steel type with the web and Defiant, a Water type with the rock, the spikes, the bad poison and Competitive), `toxic_spikes` (a flyer, bad poison, a Steel type, a Poison type absorbing and the layers put up again), `sticky_web` (Clear Body, White Herb, Competitive and a flyer), `toxic_debris` (a special hit, two Physical hits and a third that adds nothing; the attacker's switch-ins meet the layers and the rock), `order_a` (Spikes before Stealth Rock, Toxic Spikes before Sticky Web: the lines in that order), `order_b` (Toxic Spikes first, absorbed, put up again: they come last) and `hazard_faint` (a Pokemon that falls to the spikes: the faint at once, nothing after it), `exit` (Emergency Exit of a Pokemon that a hazard brings under half, after the switch) and `flower_veil` (Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web against Flower Veil: the foe is the source). The rows of `duoforge.mechanics.entry_hazards` (the layers, the creation order and the view of both viewers, after every step) are derived from the reference's own state (the harness records the side's hazards in creation order) and from the protocol lines (`tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py`).
   - **Converter.** `-sidestart` and `-sideend` of the four hazards, the damage causes, the move line of a foeSide move without a target, the `RANDOM_TARGET execute:foeSide` draw, and the boundary of a switch request: the harness records the actions still queued (`queue_len`), and a switch request with an empty queue is a REPLACEMENT also when the step has no `upkeep` (a replacement that a hazard knocked out at once; the old rule called it a PIVOT).
   - **Mutation checks.** 26 one-change mutants of `src/combat/turn.c`, the manifest and the observation (the rock fraction and type, the spikes amounts and the flyer, absorption, Steel and the bad poison, the web stages, flyer and line, the order and its slots, Toxic Debris category, side and layers, a fourth layer, the failed hazard, the entry bearer, Emergency Exit after the switch, the move line target, the Flower Veil source, the faint after each handler, the supported bits, the view): every one is caught by `conformance_pool_data`, `tiebreak_pool_data` and `entry_hazards`, the marking and view mutants also by `pool_tables` and `observation_ext`. The first run left three survivors; two led to the battles `exit` and `flower_veil`, the third (a Steel type poisoned) is the same behaviour, because the status code refuses Poison and Steel anyway.
   - **Campaigns.** `diff_driver.py random` under POOL with hazard teams: 1200 battles (500 and 700), 0 divergences and 0 unsupported; a kept set of 300 passes 300/300 and covers 2448 `SIDE_START`, 1062 Stealth Rock and 1277 Spikes damages, 285 Toxic Spikes statuses, 396 Sticky Web activations, 259 absorptions, 215 Toxic Debris and 169 hazard faints. The two divergences that the campaigns found (a Pokemon that a hazard faints has no later handler; a replacement that dies at once is a REPLACEMENT) are fixed and are in `hazard_faint` and the converter.
