# 5d. Sandstorm and Snowscape

- Area: field
- Step: -
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_weather

5d. **Sandstorm and Snowscape** (the weather step, the top of the M11 replay frequencies: Sand 6.45 percent and Snow 4.83 percent of the decisions). Sand Stream and Snow Warning join the weather-setter family (`DFI_FAMILY_WEATHER_SAND` 3 and `_SNOW` 4, the engine's `DFI_WEATHER_SAND` 3 and `_SNOW` 4; no Primal guard: the generator's pattern takes the optional guard of rain and sun only, and `pool_families.js` calls the pinned handlers with and without the orb), the moves Sandstorm and Snowscape become the two named handler ids `DFI_SPECIAL_SANDSTORM` and `_SNOWSCAPE` (the field `weather` has no column; each owns exactly `weather: 'Sandstorm'` resp. `'snowscape'`) and the type chart's `sandstorm: 3` becomes the immunity bit `DFI_IMMUNE_SAND` (32; Ground, Rock and Steel; the extended and closure canonical bytes mask it out), so the POOL fingerprints change (KP 18fcc0cc..., KPD f6a178b0... on the whole-pool tables). The generator reads the facts the engine hard-codes from the pin and fails on any other text (`check_weather_facts`: the conditions' duration 5, the 8 of the rock items, the stat boosts and their handler priority 10, the residual order 1, the damage `baseMaxhp / 16`, the upkeep and start lines; Snowscape has no `onWeather`; Weather Ball's types and doubling). Engine (`src/combat/turn.c`): the entry abilities and the moves set the weather for 5 turns (the same weather is not restarted: the ability does nothing, the move fails with `-fail` and `[still]`); Sandstorm damages every standing active Pokemon that is not Ground, Rock or Steel in `eachEvent` order (speed, a tie shuffled as a whole group, which the engine draws exactly when two of the tied Pokemon take damage, as the converter keeps the draws: `trace_to_c.drop_reason` for `each:Weather`, decided by the `[from] Sandstorm` damage lines of the step), `baseMaxhp / 16` (the cause `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_WEATHER` 16, id2 the weather value; generic for every weather that damages, in this format Sand only: Snow has no residual damage and Hail is isNonstandard "Past"), then the Update of eachEvent (Sitrus Berry), then the faints; this is the weather callback of the residual (order 1), before Grassy Terrain, Leftovers (5), poison (9) and burn (10). A Rock type has its Special Defense x1.5 in sand and an Ice type its Defense x1.5 in snow (`ModifySpD` and `ModifyDef` of priority 10, applied first); Weather Ball is a Rock move in sand and an Ice move in snow, doubled in every weather. The unmodelled sources of immunity or protection (Overcoat, Sand Force, Sand Rush, Sand Veil, Magic Guard) and of 8 turns (Smooth Rock, Icy Rock) stay unmarked, so no battle holds one (`duoforge.state.pool_weather` and `pool_families.js` check that). Marked: Sand Stream, Snow Warning, Sandstorm, Snowscape, by ten recorded battles (`w1_sand_stream`, `w2_sandstorm_move`, `w3_snow_warning`, `w4_snowscape_move`, `w5_sand_tie_four`, `_pairs` and `_mixed`, `w6_sand_residual_order`, `w7_sand_ko_sitrus`, and `w8_sand_soak`: Sandstorm and the Sand boost judge a Soaked Pokemon by its current type, step G11) and a differential campaign over a sand team and a snow team. View (decision 0018, supported bits 0 and 1): the new weather values are in the old observation's weather field; `duoforge.state.pool_weather` compares both viewers' weather and turns left with the `-weather` lines of the ten battles after every step (rows derived by `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py`) and the whole extension with its header. New public constants: `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_WEATHER` 16 (owner's OK); the weather values were declared by tier 0. The Python encoders 1 and 2 keep refusing weather 3 and 4 (ValueError); encoder 3 is the 0018 block.
