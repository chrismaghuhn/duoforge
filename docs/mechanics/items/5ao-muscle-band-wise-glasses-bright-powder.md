# 5ao. G49 (part), Muscle Band, Wise Glasses and Bright Powder

- Area: items
- Step: G49
- Tests: duoforge.items.power_and_accuracy_items, duoforge.items.bright_powder

5ao. **G49 (part), Muscle Band, Wise Glasses and Bright Powder** (the item rows of the duration-and-boost batch; provisional letter, the lead relabels at merge). No public value, no new state, no view change, no version bump. **Not part of this step: Damp Rock, Heat Rock, Smooth Rock, Icy Rock and Terrain Extender** (the duration rows). They change the weather and terrain turns to 8, the state's field counter `DFI_FIELD_TURNS_MAX` (5) and the bound of the invariant that reads it, and decision 0018 gives the public `weather_turns` and `terrain_turns` the range up to 8. But the Python live tracker (`python/duoforge_live/tracker.py`, `FIELD_TURNS = 5`) sets and reports the weather and terrain turns at 5 in its observation, so a rock weather would be reported with 5 turns: the owner must decide on that tracker change first (python/duoforge_live is not touched by this step). **Rows, all ENGINE_ROWS** (read by id, no family column: the pool's family columns are the type booster and the resist berry only). Muscle Band (`data/items.ts:4239-4251`) and Wise Glasses (`data/items.ts:7754-7766`): base power x4505/4096 for a Physical move (Muscle Band) or a Special move (Wise Glasses) of the holder, onBasePowerPriority 16, in the chain after Sharpness (19) and before the type boosters (15); the category is the table's (no marked move changes its category at the pin: Shell Side Arm, the one that does, is unmarked, and Tera Blast is not in the pool). Bright Powder (`data/items.ts:659-670`): the accuracy of a move against the holder x3686/4096, onModifyAccuracyPriority -2, in the same event as Wide Lens (priority -2, the attacker's side). The two of priority -2 have no order in the pin (the holders' Speed, a tie draws), so the engine computes both orders and refuses (E_UNSUPPORTED) a move for which they give different modified accuracies: with Compound Eyes (or Snow Cloak and Compound Eyes) before them the chains differ for some base accuracies (68 and 75 with Compound Eyes; 17, 51 and 85 with both), and the test pins those sets. A multi-hit move (the later hits, `multiaccuracy`) refuses a target with Bright Powder, as it refuses Wide Lens. The Champions mod overrides none of the three. The generator checks their pinned texts (`G49_ITEM_FACTS`, `check_g49_facts`) and `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` checks the category probe of the two boosters. The POOL table hash changes (`29c954ab...`) and the POOL fingerprints too (`KP 4a5c4c0b...`, `KPD 60939e2c...`); UNMODELED items 34 to 31. **Evidence** (seven recorded POOL battles, `data: pool`): `g49_wise_glasses` and `g49_wise_glasses_base` (Gholdengo's Shadow Ball at Milotic: 69 damage with the glasses, 63 without, same seed and plan; both Gholdengo, genderless); `g49_muscle_band` and `g49_muscle_band_base` (Kingambit (M)'s Kowtow Cleave at Milotic and Incineroar (M): the band boosts the physical hit, the Leftovers base does not); `g49_bright_powder` and `g49_bright_powder_base` (seed with Raichu (F)'s Thunderbolt drawn at 91: it misses Milotic (F) with the powder, accuracy 90, and hits it with Sitrus Berry; Gholdengo's Shadow Ball, accuracy 100, draws 91 and misses too); `g49_category_cross` (Gholdengo (genderless) holds Muscle Band and casts Shadow Ball, Kingambit (M) holds Wise Glasses and casts Kowtow Cleave: neither item boosts the other category). `g49_bright_powder_tie` (equal Speed of the two holders: the ModifyAccuracy event ties Wide Lens and Bright Powder; `trace_to_c.py` drops that draw under its own rule, `event:ModifyAccuracy` with exactly these two handlers, and refuses any other handler in that event (tie-context)). `tests/items/test_power_and_accuracy_items.c, tests/items/test_bright_powder.c` pins the marks, the handler columns, the modifiers and the order sets of the two priority -2 items over the base accuracies 1 to 100, and the two refusals, each with a control that the same battle with the powder removed is accepted: (a) Vivillon with Compound Eyes and Wide Lens, Sleep Powder (accuracy 75, orders 97 and 96) into a Bright Powder holder, a battle built through the step API; (b) Triple Axel's second hit into a Bright Powder holder (the recorded `g33_triple_axel` battle, its first hit's draw is below 81).

**Mutation checks of this step.** 15 mutants of `turn.c`: the thirteen that change a factor, a category, a holder, the powder, or a refusal are caught by a recorded battle or by `test_pool_g49` (a refusal removed, mutants 12 and 13, is caught by the two refusal tests above). Two mutants are **equivalent**, not survivors: (14) Muscle Band's chain placed after the type boosters (priority 15): the only change is its place relative to the type boosters, and one holder carries one item, so Muscle Band and a type booster never apply to the same move; (15) the powder-first order taken for the accuracy: where the engine accepts a move the two orders give the same accuracy, so the value does not change.
