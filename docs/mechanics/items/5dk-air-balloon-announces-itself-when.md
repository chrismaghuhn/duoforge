5dk. **Air Balloon announces itself when Trick, Switcheroo, Thief, Covet or Symbiosis gives it (issue 520; branch claude/520-balloon-after-trick; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** Pin: `Pokemon.setItem` runs the new item's Start at once (`sim/pokemon.ts:1885-1887`), and Air Balloon's onStart prints `-item|holder|Air Balloon` (`data/items.ts:191-195`). Trick and Switcheroo call setItem before their `-item ... [from] move:` line (`data/moves.ts` trick onHit, the target first, then the user); Thief and Covet call it on the user before their `-enditem`/`-item` lines; Symbiosis before its `-activate`. The engine printed the move's line without the announcement. Now each of the three sites (`dfi_trick`, `dfi_thief`, `dfi_symbiosis` in `src/combat/turn.c`) calls `dfi_item_start_shown` after it sets the item: ITEM_SHOWN for a living holder of Air Balloon, the event the switch-in announcement uses (step G67). Klutz, Embargo and Magic Room are not marked, so no holder ignores the item here; Gravity is marked since step 5dl, which also suppresses the line (the check in `dfi_item_start_shown`). **Evidence.** The recording `tests/reference/specs/fz520_switcheroo_air_balloon.json` (fz_1_97, cut after its step 8; seed and choices of the campaign, trace from the pinned Showdown b2cb775): Klefki's Switcheroo takes Arcanine's Air Balloon. On main it diverges at step 8 (event 3: the move's item line where the reference has the announcement); with the fix it passes. The second fuzz case, fz_1_8, has the same divergence at step 2 and passes it with the fix (its later divergence is the Outrage lock of issue 516, fixed on its own branch). The mutant is the old code, so it is the main run. **Gaps:** Thief, Covet and Symbiosis with an Air Balloon have no recording; the change there follows the same pin order. The live tracker (`python/duoforge_live/lines.py`) gets the bare `-item|holder|Air Balloon` before the giver's line, while the holder still has its old item; it folds that line (the giver's `[from] move:` line that follows changes the item, ITEM_CHANGE as before), so the spectator replay of the reference logs never stops there (`duoforge.python.replay`).
