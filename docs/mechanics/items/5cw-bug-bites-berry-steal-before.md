5cw. **Bug Bite's berry steal before the DamagingHit handlers (the order of the G64 item-end path; lane fix part, builder H24, branch chris/expansion-fix-bugbite-order).** The pin runs Bug Bite's `onHit` in `runMoveEffects`, right after the damage of the hit: `data/moves.ts:1920-1930` (takeItem, `-enditem ... [from] stealeat [move] Bug Bite`, then the Eat, only while `source.hp` is nonzero), at `sim/battle-actions.ts:1085-1086`. The self effects, the secondaries and the DamagingHit handlers come after it (`battle-actions.ts:1096`, `1099`, `1119-1121`); AfterHit comes after DamagingHit (`1123-1127`). Rough Skin is an `onDamagingHit` of order 1 (`data/abilities.ts:3938-3949`), so the reference prints the berry's item end before Rough Skin's damage on the attacker. `src/combat/turn.c` ran `dfi_bug_bite` after the DamagingHit block (the G64 block, which described its call as before the AfterHit of Knock Off): the order was inverted. The fix moves that block to the step-3 position, after the Steel Roller hook and before `selfSwitch`, the self effects and the secondaries (turn.c, the block marked "onHit of Bug Bite"). Its checks are unchanged (`source.hp`, the berry test, Sticky Hold, Unburden). **Affected marked moves**: Bug Bite (`DFI_MOVE_BUGBITE`) is the only marked move whose `onHit` changes the target's item or the attacker's HP before the DamagingHit handlers (the `source.hp` test makes the order observable). Knock Off, Thief and Covet are `onAfterHit` in the pin and already run after DamagingHit in the engine (AfterHit block), so they are not affected; Pluck and Incinerate are not marked. The marked `onHit` handlers of Alluring Voice, Anchor Shot, Aromatherapy and the rest change statuses, volatiles or stages, which the DamagingHit readers of the pin do not look at on the same hit. **Substitute and fainted targets** (the lead's conditions): `hit[i]` is false for a target that a Substitute absorbed, so the moved block steals nothing there, as the pin (no onHit for a Substitute hit); takeItem has no HP test in the pin (`sim/pokemon.ts:1851-1866`), and the engine keeps `hit[i]` for a target that the hit knocked out, so a fainted target loses its berry in both. Neither case is exercised by the two battles below; the claim rests on the code. **Evidence.** The two minimal cases fz_9007_1 and fz_9007_5 of run 9007 use C2 moves, which are not on main, so they cannot be legal POOL battles on this branch; the two battles committed in their place are `c2fix_a` (fz_9008_10, step 3: Bug Bite on Garchomp holding a Lum Berry, Rough Skin on the attacker) and `c2fix_b` (fz_9008_106, step 12: Bug Bite on Kangaskhan holding a Chople Berry), recorded from a campaign of run 9008 (teams M and N, main-marked moves only, 600 battles, `--keep-traces`) with the pinned Showdown (`ps_trace --check` matches). The campaign has 287 berry steals by Bug Bite (Lum 140, Chople 134, Sitrus 13). The fixed runner passes both battles; the mutant (origin/main's turn.c, the steal after DamagingHit) diverges on `c2fix_a` and passes `c2fix_b`, which has no DamagingHit reaction (the mutant is caught by one of the two). Pool battles 795 (793 before), tiebreak stops 4371 (4356 before). The quick CI of the fix commit (38112236330) is green. Sticky Hold blocks are not counted in these numbers.
