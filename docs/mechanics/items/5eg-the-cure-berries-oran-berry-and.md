- Step: I2
- Tests: duoforge.items.berry_family, duoforge.combat.item_family, duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.data.static, duoforge.state.pool_setup, duoforge.data.gen_closure_refusals, duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data

5eg. **I2, The cure berries, Oran Berry and the isBerry column** (Content lane, batch I2 of the berry family, builder i2, branch `chris/i2-berries` on main 69b04cba; the C0 inventory `docs/research/expansion/data/champions-100.json`, the tokens `item:cheriberry`, `item:chestoberry`, `item:pechaberry`, `item:rawstberry`, `item:aspearberry`, `item:persimberry`, `item:oranberry`; no new public value, draw site or residual kind). Pin b2cb775; the Champions mod has no entry for any of the seven, so every rule is the base `data/items.ts` (owner's rule for I2: the rows do not depend on Champions and serve Gen 9 OU as they are).

**The pin.**
- The cure berries (`data/items.ts`: Cheri `:836-856` par, Chesto `:877-897` slp, Pecha `:4497-4517` psn or tox, Rawst `:5068-5088` brn, Aspear `:292-312` frz, Persim `:4518-4536` the confusion volatile). `onUpdate` calls `eatItem()` while the holder has the berry's condition; `onEat` repeats the test and cures it (`cureStatus()`, Persim `removeVolatile('confusion')`). Unlike Lum Berry (`:3537-3560`) they have no `onAfterSetStatus`: the berry waits for the next `eachEvent('Update')` (`sim/battle-actions.ts:967` and `:1003`, the Champions hit loop's two Updates, `sim/battle.ts:2861` after an action, `:474` after the Weather upkeep, and the switch-out's at `sim/battle-actions.ts:83`).
- Oran Berry (`:4397-4418`) is Sitrus Berry (`:5744-5765`) with 10 HP for `baseMaxhp / 4`: `onUpdate` at `hp <= maxhp / 2`, `onTryEatItem` asks `runEvent('TryHeal', ..., 10)` (Heal Block's `onTryHeal` returns false, `data/moves.ts:8324-8332`), `onEat` `this.heal(10)`.
- `eatItem` (`sim/pokemon.ts:1768-1809`): `UseItem` and `TryEatItem` (a foe's Unnerve, `onFoeTryEatItem`), `-enditem|X|Berry|[eat]`, the `Eat` effect, then `AfterUseItem` (Unburden, Symbiosis). `cureStatus` (`:1674-1682`): `-curestatus|X|status|[msg]`, then `setStatus('')`, which ends the sleep counter, the toxic stage and the freeze turns with the status. The Update handlers are collected once per Pokemon (`sim/battle.ts` runEvent): an item a Pokemon receives during its own Update (Symbiosis after its berry) does not run in that Update.
- Bug Bite (`data/moves.ts:1911-1931`) takes every `isBerry` item and runs its `Eat` for the user, with no `TryEatItem`: a cure berry cures the user's own condition if it has it, Oran heals the user 10 HP (`this.heal`, Heal Block refuses, a full user shows nothing).

**State and rules.**
- Generator (`tools/datagen/tablegen/items.py`, format-independent: every pattern reads the base entry, and `derive_family` refuses any rule set's change of a member): two new item families, `CURE_BERRY` (3) with the parameter `DFI_FAMILY_CURE_SLP`, `PAR`, `PSN` (psn and tox), `BRN`, `FRZ` or `CONFUSION` (1 to 6), matched by `cure_berry_condition`, and `HEAL_BERRY` (4) with the parameter the HP healed, matched by `heal_berry_amount` (Sitrus's three handlers with a literal amount; Sitrus itself, `baseMaxhp / 4`, does not match and stays the closure row by id). The new column `dfi_pool_item_berry` is the base entry's `isBerry` for every item (28 berries, `item_is_berry`; a rule set may not change it); it is the very last part of the canonical pool bytes. `check_5eg_facts` demands the texts of eatItem, cureStatus and Bug Bite (`I2_SIM_FACTS`) and that the seven ids are read nowhere else but Oran in Anger Shell's and Berserk's healingItems lists (both unmarked) and in `RESTORATIVE_BERRIES` (the Endless Battle Clause's staleness; Reg M-C has no such clause, the singles kind refuses from turn 101). The family members have the handler NONE: the unmodelled items go from 18 to 11. `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` probes the pinned handlers: each cure berry is eaten for exactly its condition and cures exactly it, Oran's three handlers give 10, and the isBerry column is the dex's `isBerry` for every row.
- Engine (`src/combat/items/families.h`, pure): `dfi_item_is_berry`, `dfi_cure_berry_status`, `dfi_berry_cures` (Lum: every status and the confusion; a cure berry: its one condition) and `dfi_hp_berry_heal` (Sitrus a quarter, a heal berry its parameter). `src/combat/turn.c`:
  - `dfi_use_item_core`: the eaten flag of `ITEM_END` for every isBerry item (was a hard-coded list of Sitrus, Lum and the resist berries).
  - Lum's path is the family path: `dfi_eat_cure_berry(r, flat, item)` (Unnerve, the eaten line, `dfi_berry_cure`, Symbiosis) for Lum (its AfterSetStatus and Update) and the cure berries (Update); `dfi_berry_cure` (was `dfi_lum_cure`) cures what `dfi_berry_cures` says.
  - `dfi_update`: the holders of a cure or heal berry are bearers of the Update (whether they act or not, for the each-tie draw; `EACH_HANDLERS` of `tools/reference/trace_to_c.py` has the same seven ids); Sitrus's clause is the HP berry clause (Heal Block and Unnerve, then the eaten line, the heal, Symbiosis); the Lum clause is the cure clause, with the item the holder had when its Update started.
  - `dfi_bug_bite`: the isBerry column decides what is taken; an HP berry heals the user, Lum or a cure berry cures it (`dfi_berry_cure`), a resist berry does nothing, and a berry whose Eat the engine does not model (Leppa Berry) is refused (E_UNSUPPORTED) before anything happens.
- The public data API (`duoforge_data_item_static`, `src/state/data_query.c`) names only the type families: the seven rows are reported with no family, as before (lead and HS decision 2026-10-24; making them public is HS's decision).

**Interactions** (marked on main unless said): Unnerve refuses the eating (`dfi_unnerved`; the berry waits for the next Update); Heal Block (Psychic Noise) refuses Oran's eating; Symbiosis passes the ally's item after the eat; Bug Bite steals and eats every berry; Knock Off, Thief, Covet, Trick and Switcheroo move berries through `item_now`, and a received berry is eaten at its holder's next Update; Sticky Hold keeps one; Unburden after the eat. Not marked, so no battle meets them (`duoforge.items.berry_family` checks the marks): Ripen, Cheek Pouch, Harvest, Gluttony, Cud Chew, Klutz, Berserk, Pickpocket, Magician, Magic Room, Fling, Pluck, Stuff Cheeks, Teatime, Belch, Recycle; Anger Shell, As One, Embargo, Natural Gift and Incinerate are not in the pool. The abilities that cure the same conditions in their own onUpdate (Insomnia, Vital Spirit, Immunity, Own Tempo, Sweet Veil) are unmarked. **Leppa Berry is not marked**: its onEat line `-activate|X|item: Leppa Berry|Move|[consumed]` would be a new ACTIVATE form and the viewer's PP fold a new rule (an HS decision, the builder's notes in `scratchpad/i2/leppa_analysis.md`).
