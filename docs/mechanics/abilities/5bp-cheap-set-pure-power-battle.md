# 5bp. G75, the cheap set: Pure Power, Battle Armor, Scope Lens, and the Mega Stones Medichamite and Clefablite

- Area: abilities
- Step: G75
- Tests: duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.state.pool_setup, duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data, duoforge.data.api, duoforge.state.pool_g57

5bp. **G75, the cheap set: Pure Power, Battle Armor, Scope Lens, and the Mega Stones Medichamite and Clefablite** (lane B, batch 4; no public value, no new state, no new draw site, no new pool column, no flags3 bit, no view change, no version bump). Pure Power, Battle Armor and Scope Lens are engine rows (`ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator, handler NONE) that `src/combat/turn.c` reads by id; Medichamite and Clefablite are marked on the Mega-by-stone model of 5p. None is overridden by the Champions mod.
  - **Pure Power** (`data/abilities.ts:3608-3617`, onModifyAtkPriority 5, chainModify(2)): the same text as Huge Power (`:1886-1896`). x2 for a physical move of its holder in the ModifyAtk chain of `turn.c` (the Huge Power line, with the same physical gate). Medicham and Medicham-Mega carry it; Mold Breaker is not marked.
  - **Battle Armor** (`data/abilities.ts:355-361`, onCriticalHit false, breakable): the same text as Shell Armor. The critical roll is taken and then cancelled by the target's CriticalHit event (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1645-1646`), so no `-crit` line. Perrserker and Falinks carry it; the cancel is in `turn.c` next to Shell Armor's.
  - **Scope Lens** (`data/items.ts:5554-5565`, onModifyCritRatio +1): one more term of the holder's critical-ratio sum in `turn.c`. The sum is move ratio + Dragon Cheer stage + Super Luck + Scope Lens, clamped to 0..4 after the sum (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1623-1633`), then the roll and the CriticalHit event (`:1637-1647`). Dragon Cheer (`data/moves.ts:4056-4086`, +1, or +2 for a Dragon-type target at start) and Super Luck (`data/abilities.ts:4703-4711`, +1) were already summed (G65, G72b).
  - **Crit battles** (the draw bound: 1 always critical, 2 is 1/2, 8 is 1/8): `g75_scope_lens_cheer` (Absol with Scope Lens and Super Luck; Milotic's Dragon Cheer on turn 1; Slash on turn 2: 2+1+1+1 = 5, clamped to 4, bound 1, critical); `g75_crit_cheer_no_scope` (the same without Scope Lens: sum 4, bound 1); `g75_scope_lens_only` (Scope Lens without cheer: sum 4, bound 1); `g75_crit_control` (Super Luck alone: sum 3, bound 2); `g75_scope_lens_sucker_punch` (Justified, Scope Lens, no Super Luck, no cheer: Sucker Punch of critical ratio 1, sum 2, bound 8).
  - **Mega Stones.** Medichamite (`data/items.ts:3865-3876`) gives Medicham-Mega (Pure Power; `data/pokedex.ts:5911-5925`; base stats 60/100/85/80/85/100). Medicham keeps Pure Power and Telepathy (H). Clefablite (`data/items.ts:1088-1099`) gives Clefable-Mega (Magic Bounce, Fairy/Flying; base stats 95/80/93/135/110/70; `data/pokedex.ts:1045-1060`, Champions tier `data/mods/champions/formats-data.ts:276`). Clefable's hidden ability Unaware (`data/pokedex.ts:1028-1044`) is marked by G65, so the pair is reachable; its Cute Charm and Magic Guard are not marked and their sets are refused.
  - **Not marked, and why.** Starminite (`data/items.ts:6021-6032`): Starmie-Mega has Huge Power, but Starmie's Illuminate, Natural Cure and Analytic (`data/pokedex.ts:2730-2746`) are unmarked, so no team reaches the Mega. Altarianite (`data/items.ts:238-249`): Altaria-Mega has Pixilate (marked, the ATE family), but Altaria's Natural Cure and Cloud Nine (`data/pokedex.ts:6284-6298`) are unmarked. Sharpedonite (`data/items.ts:5621-5632`): Sharpedo-Mega has Strong Jaw (`data/abilities.ts:4661-4672`, x1.5 for `move.flags.bite`), which is UNMODELLED: a bite flag needs a pool column, which is a lead decision, so it is deferred. Five stones of `data/formats/pools/reg-mc.json` have no pool row (Mewtwonite X, Diancite, Latiasite, Latiosite, Zeraorite); Heatranite is Past. They are left out.

## Support

**Pure Power, Battle Armor and Scope Lens, and the Mega Stones Medichamite and Clefablite (step G75, decision 0015 entry 5bp)**: Pure Power is Attack x2 for a physical move of its holder (as Huge Power); Battle Armor cancels a critical hit after its draw (as Shell Armor); Scope Lens is critical ratio +1 on the holder's side of the sum (before the clamp to 0..4). Engine rows (no public value, no new state, no new draw site). Medicham-Mega (Pure Power) and Clefable-Mega (Magic Bounce, with Clefable's hidden Unaware) are reachable; Starminite, Altarianite and Sharpedonite stay unmarked (their base abilities, or Strong Jaw's bite flag, are not in the pool yet). Evidence: eleven recorded POOL battles (`g75_*`); a 900-battle campaign (899 PASS, 0 divergence, one refused step of an existing rule); 14 of 14 mutants caught.
