5bd. **G53, Pressure and the foe's derived PP** (decision 0030 section 1 to 3, lane B overnight; the letter 5bd is the lead's). Marked: the ability **Pressure** (`data/abilities.ts:3437-3449`, onStart and onDeductPP; the Champions mod has no entry; `check_g34_facts` pins both texts), an engine row read by id (`dfi_pressure_extra`, `dfi_pressure_holder`, `dfi_entry_ability`). **Public values** (the owner's decision 0030): `DUOFORGE_MOVE_STATIC_FLAG_MUST_PRESSURE` = 0x800, the pin's `flags.mustpressure` written by the generator (Imprison, Spikes, Stealth Rock and Toxic Spikes of the pool; Snatch and Tera Blast are not in it), and the meaning of `DUOFORGE_PP_DERIVED` (pp_max minus the PP the viewer saw spent). No event kind, cause, view bit, API, state, or version bump. **Engine**: a foe's move costs one extra PP per standing Pressure target (`useMoveInner`, `sim/battle-actions.ts:473-484`, after the move's own PP and the redirection, before TryMove, clamped at 0 as `deductPP`; the pressure targets are `getMoveTargets`' targets, none for a foeSide move unless mustpressure, every standing foe for a field or mustpressure move; a locked turn has none, `sim/battle-actions.ts:289`). **Viewer**: `dfi_events_fold_knowledge` counts 1 plus the extra per foe use, from the viewer's own Pressure timeline (`dfi_own_pressure`: the standing holders of its side, their Pressure from the sheet, Trace's copy, a Mega Evolution, and the switch-ins of the step). **Attributed, not refused** (the lead's ruling of 2026-10-09, decision 0030 section 1): the state deducts the extra exactly; the viewer counts it only where the target is shown or follows from the move's class. A `[still]` line blanks the target (`attrLastMove`, `sim/battle.ts:3123-3138`), and a two-turn charge names none on its charge turn, so their single-target extra is not attributed (1 counts). Nothing is refused. **Evidence**: fifteen recorded POOL battles (`tests/reference/specs/g53_*.json`): single target into Pressure (`g53_single_target`), spread into two Pressure foes with one protected (`g53_spread_protect`), field Trick Room with the clamp at 0 (`g53_field_trick_room`), mustpressure Spikes and Imprison (`g53_mustpressure`), a Pressure holder fainted before a field move (`g53_fainted_field`) and in an Earthquake (`g53_fainted_holder`), an ally holder (`g53_pressure_ally`), foeSide Sticky Web (`g53_sticky_web`), a Trace copy (`g53_trace_pressure`, `g53_trace_switch`), a switch-in (`g53_switch_pressure`, `g53_switch_in_pressure`), Expanding Force under Psychic Terrain (`g53_expanding_force`), a Mega Evolution that ends Pressure (`g53_mega_pressure`). `tests/test_pool_g53.c` (`duoforge.state.pool_g53`): at every boundary of every g53 battle, for both players, the foe's derived PP equals the owner's exact PP (the derived value is checked against the engine's own state, not against a number typed in); the charge of a two-turn move into a Pressure foe is accepted and deducted (a Solar Beam costs 2 PP, its control at an ally costs 1), the `[still]` battle `g53_still_pressure` has its Sucker Punch exact at 6 PP and attributed at 7 (the gap is the hidden extra, pinned for each step), and its control `g53_trace_pressure` has a shown target with the two equal. **Mutation checks**: 28 mutants (15 of the engine, 13 of the viewer fold), each rebuilt and run against `duoforge.state.pool_g53` and `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data`: After the ruling three of them (the refusal paths) no longer exist; of the 25 that remain, 23 are caught and 2 survive, both equivalent: the fold's foeSide branch (a foeSide move's line names no target, so the single-target rule gives 0 either way), and `alive = true` at a switch-in (a switched-in member always stands). The two that were open are closed white-box: a faint without a damage line (`check_fold_fainted_holder`, the fold of a constructed event list) and a locked turn whose target is a Pressure holder (`check_locked_turn_free`, the step API; the locked move gets no extra). **Differential campaign** (`tools/cloud/aws_fuzz/campaigns/g53-pressure/`, `diff_driver.py random`, seed 53, 300 battles, pairings DE, ED, DD, EE): PASS 300, UNSUPPORTED 0, DIVERGENCE 0 under the attribution rule. Before the rule, the engine's foe view counted the hidden extra: DIVERGENCE 120, all in steps with a `[still]` line; with a refusal, UNSUPPORTED 120 (the same battles). The ruling replaced the refusal by the attribution rule, and the reference side (`trace_to_c.attribute_pp`, an independent implementation from the events) gives the same attributable PP as the engine. The conformance record carries it as `pp_foe` next to the exact `pp`.

## Support

**Pressure (step G53, decision 0030 and decision 0015 item 5bd)**: a foe's Pressure costs the user one more PP per standing Pressure target of the move (every standing foe for a field, spread or mustpressure move; none for a foeSide move that is not mustpressure, nor for an ally). The state is exact. The viewer attributes the foe's extra only where the target is shown or follows from the move's class (`DUOFORGE_PP_DERIVED`, pp_max minus the PP the viewer can attribute): a `[still]` line blanks its target (sim/battle.ts:3123-3138) and counts 1 only, so the foe's view can be higher than the owner's exact PP by that extra. The mustpressure flag is `DUOFORGE_MOVE_STATIC_FLAG_MUST_PRESSURE` (0x800). Recorded as `g53_*` battles (decision 0015 item 5bd).
