# 5ba. G45, six abilities of the simple kind: Steadfast, Moxie, Weak Armor, Telepathy, Volt Absorb and Punk Rock

- Area: abilities
- Step: G45
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g45

5ba. **G45, six abilities of the simple kind: Steadfast, Moxie, Weak Armor, Telepathy, Volt Absorb and Punk Rock** (the next free letter after G43 5am and G49 5an; no public value, no new event, no view change, no version bump, no new draw site, no battle state). Pressure is **not** in this step (decision B of the lead, 2026-10-09): its PP charge is not in any protocol line, so the opponent's derived PP would differ from the reference; it is parked for a later step that derives the charge from public lines (the observation fold). All six are engine rows (`ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator, handler NONE) that `src/combat/turn.c` reads by id, and they are marked in `src/data/support_manifest.c`:
   - **Steadfast** (`data/abilities.ts:4549-4557`, onFlinch: `boost({spe: 1})`, the holder its own source): after the `[cant] flinch` line of a flinched move, `-ability|holder|Steadfast|boost`, then `-boost`; the ability's primary change (a change of 0 shows nothing).
   - **Moxie** (`:2749-2759`, onSourceAfterFaint: `boost({atk: length}, source)` when the last faint's effect is a Move). `length` is the number of faints that the faintMessages call shows (`sim/battle.ts:2537-2601`); AfterFaint runs after the faint lines and the win check, so a finished battle gets nothing. **Run-scoped scratch, not state:** the engine keeps the cause of the last queued faint in two fields of `dfi_run` (`last_faint_by`, `last_faint_move`), set by the hit loop when a Move's direct damage queues a faint. They are set and read inside one step's run, between the faint announcement and AfterFaint (or the process of the same action). Every action's epilogue runs `dfi_process_faints` before a step can end, a PIVOT boundary included, which resets `faint_count`; so the fields are dead at a step boundary. They are never saved in the battle, the codec or the digest.
   - **Weak Armor** (`:5448-5458`, onDamagingHit: a Physical move, every hit of a multi-hit move: `boost({def: -1, spe: 2}, target, target)`, the holder its own source). It sits in the DamagingHit loop after Justified and before the attacker's Poison Touch; a fainted holder gets nothing.
   - **Telepathy** (`:4931-4942`, onTryHit, breakable: a non-Status move of an ally, target not the user: `-activate|holder|ability: Telepathy`, the hit is stopped for that target). A spread move still hits the others.
   - **Volt Absorb** (`:5340-5353`, onTryHit, breakable: an Electric move of another Pokemon, a Status move included): `this.heal(baseMaxhp / 4)` whose source is the user (`-heal|holder|hp|[from] ability: Volt Absorb|[of] user`), and when the heal does nothing (full HP or Heal Block) `-immune|holder|[from] ability: Volt Absorb`; the move is stopped for that target either way. It is the TryHit loop's branch after Good as Gold's, the Lightning Rod path.
   - **Punk Rock** (`:3589-3602`, breakable): onBasePowerPriority 7, `chainModify([5325, 4096])` for a sound move of the holder, placed after Helping Hand (10) and the items (15), before the terrains (6); onSourceModifyDamage, `chainModify(0.5)` for a sound move against the holder, a target's ability in the ModifyDamage chain (one of Solid Rock, Multiscale, Aura Guard and Punk Rock per hit, so the chain still has six modifiers at most).
   - **Breakable.** Telepathy, Volt Absorb and Punk Rock are breakable. No marked ability ignores that flag: Mold Breaker is unmarked (`tests/test_pool_g45.c` pins it), Teravolt, Turboblaze and Mycelium Might are not in the pool, and a move with `ignoreAbility` is UNMODELED. So no interaction is implemented and none is refused.
   - **Evidence**: six recorded POOL battles (genders stated in each paste): `g45_steadfast` (Persian (F)'s Fake Out flinches Machamp (M): `cant|flinch`, `-ability|Steadfast|boost`, `-boost|spe|1`); `g45_moxie_double` (Krookodile (M)'s Earthquake knocks out Pikachu (M) and Raichu (F) at once: two faints, then `-ability|Moxie|boost` and `-boost|atk|2`); `g45_weak_armor` (Garchomp (M)'s Earthquake on Ceruledge (M): `-ability|Weak Armor|boost`, `-unboost|def|1`, `-boost|spe|2`); `g45_telepathy` (Garchomp (M)'s Earthquake reaches Medicham (M), stopped by Telepathy, the two foes take it); `g45_volt_absorb` (Raichu (F)'s Thunderbolt at Jolteon (M) at full HP is `-immune`; after Garchomp's Earthquake the Thunderbolt heals it, `[from] ability: Volt Absorb|[of] Raichu`); `g45_punk_rock` (Hyper Voice of Toxtricity (M) and Toxtricity (F), both with Punk Rock; damage in both directions, checked by the conformance comparison).
   - **Rework evidence** (five more POOL battles, recorded after the lead's rework request; the specs in `tests/reference/specs/`, the traces in `tests/reference/traces/`): `g45_weak_armor_special` (Gholdengo (genderless) Shadow Ball at Ceruledge (M), three times: no Weak Armor line and no stage change on the holder); `g45_telepathy_status` (Gholdengo's Shadow Ball is a foe move and hits Medicham (M), the Telepathy holder; Pikachu (M)'s Charm, a Status move from the ally, is not stopped); `g45_volt_absorb_heal_block` (Farigiraf (F)'s Psychic Noise on Jolteon (M), then Raichu (F)'s Thunderbolt: `-immune` with `[from] ability: Volt Absorb`, not a heal, because Heal Block is on); `g45_punk_rock_attacker` (Toxtricity (M)'s Hyper Voice, a sound move of the holder, against two foes without Punk Rock); `g45_moxie_win` (Krookodile (M)'s Earthquake knocks out two Electric foes on turn 1, Moxie +2; one more on turn 3, Moxie +1; the last foe on turn 4 ends the battle with no Moxie line, because the win comes before AfterFaint).
   - **Tests**: `tests/test_pool_g45.c` (`duoforge.state.pool_g45`): the marks and the handler column, the Mold Breaker family unmarked, and the state after the recorded battles: Steadfast's Speed stage, Moxie's Attack stage and the two faints, Weak Armor's stages, Telepathy's HP, Volt Absorb's full HP and its heal (a quarter, then Leftovers a sixteenth). The conformance tests compare every event of the six battles with Showdown.
   - **Mutation checks**: **24 of 28 caught** (the edits of `turn.c` built and run against `duoforge.state.pool_g45` and the pool conformance tests, each restored after, on the merged tree with the eleven battles). Caught: Steadfast dropped and +2 Speed; Moxie dropped, +1, twice the length, without the announce path; Weak Armor dropped, Defense -2, Speed +1, and for special moves too; Telepathy dropped, for foes too, for Status moves too, and the line without the stop; Volt Absorb dropped, a third, without -immune, with Heal Block ignored, and the heal without its source; Punk Rock's BasePower dropped, x1.5, its weakening dropped, x0.75, and the attacker weakened instead.
     Survivors, with the reason. **S3** Steadfast in SELF mode: equivalent (the two differ only at a change of 0, which shows nothing). **M4** Moxie without the process path: equivalent for every reachable battle (a move's direct faints are announced in its hit loop; argued, not proven). **M6** Moxie with the win check dropped: equivalent in this engine. After a finished battle `dfi_boost` returns at its `foes_left == 0` guard before any stage change, so the win check and that guard are redundant; `g45_moxie_win` shows no Moxie line after the win, and with the guard in place the mutant cannot change it. **V6** Volt Absorb absorbing Status Electric moves: not catchable, no marked Electric status move targets a Pokemon (Thunder Wave is unmarked, so no battle can contain one). Reported, not hidden.

## Support

**The simple abilities (step G45)**: Steadfast (a flinch that stops a move raises Speed by 1), Moxie (a Move's knock-out raises Attack by the number of faints it caused at once), Weak Armor (each Physical hit lowers Defense by 1 and raises Speed by 2), Telepathy (an ally's non-Status move is stopped), Volt Absorb (an Electric move heals a quarter of the HP or is stopped) and Punk Rock (sound moves: x1.3 for the holder, x0.5 against it) are marked: **IMPLEMENTED + TESTED** against recorded reference battles (decision 0015 step 5ba).
