# 5ah. G39, small batch: thirteen abilities and four moves

- Area: abilities
- Step: G39
- Tests: duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data

5ah. **G39, small batch: thirteen abilities and four moves** (the last batch of the expansion, rows that need no new state, from the "batchable now" list of E's audit: Charm, Fake Tears, Sacred Sword and Super Fang; Hyper Cutter, Regenerator, Solar Power, Scrappy, Infiltrator, Queenly Majesty, Damp, Sturdy, Snow Cloak, Sand Veil, Static, Justified and Limber). **No public value, no version bump, no view change, no state change.** Tables: `SUPER_FANG` is one new named handler id (`G39_HANDLERS`, after the four ids of step G29: 60; UNMODELED moves to 61), Sacred Sword reuses `DARKEST_LARIAT` (the same two ignore flags), `DFI_BOOST_ROLE_PRIMARY_TARGET` = 6 is a new role of the generated boost column (a status move of one adjacent target whose primary boosts go to that target: Charm, Fake Tears and twelve more rows that the column now models, which stay unmarked), and the thirteen abilities are `ENGINE_ROWS`. The draw site `DFI_SITE_STATIC` = 19 is new (`DFI_SITE_COUNT` 20; internal, not public). The POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step; UNMODELED moves 252 to 236, abilities 154 to 141 (`tools/datagen/pool_families.js` agrees).
   - **The pin.** The generator checks the whole texts that the engine hard-codes, whitespace aside: the four moves (`G39_FACTS`) and the thirteen ability entries (`G39_ABILITY_FACTS`; Regenerator's is the Champions mod's own entry with its `[silent]` heal, `G32_ENTRY_FACTS`), and `pool_families.js` mirrors the engine rows and checks that Sacred Sword is a copy of Darkest Lariat's rules.
   - **Abilities.** Hyper Cutter: a foe's drop of Attack is refused with `-fail ... unboost atk [from] ability: Hyper Cutter` (not for a move's secondary, not for the holder's own drops). Scrappy: Normal and Fighting moves hit Ghost types, and Intimidate does not lower the Attack of its holder (it joins Inner Focus's guard). Infiltrator: the holder's moves skip the screens. Queenly Majesty shares Armor Tail's TryMove block (priority moves at the holder or its allies fail). Sturdy: a hit at full HP leaves 1 HP, before Focus Sash (also confusion damage); its OHKO immunity is inert because no OHKO move is marked (`damp_sturdy_rows` pins it). Damp is inert: every move that its callbacks stop (Explosion, Mind Blown, Misty Explosion, Self-Destruct, Aftermath) is unmarked (guard test). Snow Cloak and Sand Veil: the evasion rider x3277/4096 for the target in snow or in sand, in G34's accuracy chain per target (after Compound Eyes, Wide Lens last); Sand Veil also makes its holder immune to the sand's damage. Static: a contact hit paralyses the attacker 3 times in 10 (site 19, `randomChance(3, 10)`, called next to Flame Body). Justified: a Dark move that hits raises Attack by one stage (after Stamina and Thermal Exchange). Limber: no paralysis from any source (`-immune` for a move's primary status, silent for a secondary; a Flower Veil holder stays E_UNSUPPORTED), and Trace refuses to copy it onto a paralysed holder. Solar Power: Special Attack x1.5 in sun and upkeep damage of 1/8 of the maximum HP at the end of each turn of sun (`[of]` is the holder), in the weather's each-order. Regenerator: switching out heals floor(max HP / 3) before the switch, as the Champions `[silent]` heal; the engine emits it as a HEAL event with the ability cause (the opponent's HP display follows the event) and the converter keeps that one `[silent]` line. **The Python tracker needed no fold**: `python.replay`, `python.live` and the equivalence tests pass with the `g39_regenerator` battle, and nothing in `python/` changed.
   - **Moves.** Charm and Fake Tears: `dfi_boost` with the primary effect at the target. Super Fang: `dfi_get_damage` returns max(1, floor(current HP / 2)) with no draws (the accuracy of 90 is the normal check). Sacred Sword: Darkest Lariat's handler.
   - **Evidence**: ten recorded POOL battles (`g39_*`; seeds in their specs): `g39_hyper_cutter_charm` (Intimidate and Charm refused at Hyper Cutter, Fake Tears, Coaching's raise), `g39_scrappy_sword` (Sacred Sword at a Ghost), `g39_scrappy_intimidate` (a Scrappy holder keeps its Attack; Earthquake at a flier), `g39_sturdy`, `g39_infiltrator_queenly`, `g39_static_limber` (Static's roll, Limber's refusals), `g39_sand_veil`, `g39_snow_cloak`, `g39_solar_power`, `g39_regenerator` (the heal at about 41 percent). 265 pool battles and 1474 stops after the re-sync onto G25 (255 and 1417 on main: 57 stops for the ten battles, taken from the run).
   - **Mutations**: 29 single-rule edits (`g39_mutate.py` of the session) of `src/combat/turn.c` (each ability's rule broken in a different way: Hyper Cutter never blocks or also stops a raise, Scrappy for Intimidate, for Ghost types, for every immunity, Limber, Static's roll, status and contact test, Justified for every move or by two stages, Sturdy at any HP, never, or after the Sash, Sand Veil and Snow Cloak accuracy, the 0.9 factor and Sand Veil's sand damage, Solar Power's boost, damage and weather, Regenerator never, half or after the switch, Queenly Majesty, Infiltrator, Sacred Sword, Super Fang, Charm), each built and run: all 29 go red: `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` red (most also `tiebreak_pool_data`; Sacred Sword also the corpus). Two survivors of the first run drove battle changes: Static for a move without contact (B4; the Static battle now has a Flamethrower at the holder) and, earlier, Justified for a non-Dark hit, Regenerator at low HP, Coaching beside Hyper Cutter and Earthquake at a flier under Scrappy.
   - **Campaign**: `camp39a`, seed 3901, 400 random battles over fifteen pairings of three new teams (N, O, P with Milotic's Life Dew and Recover as full-HP heals, and the existing A and B) that carry the thirteen abilities and the four moves: 366 PASS, 0 DIVERGENCE, 0 UNSUPPORTED, 0 REF_ERROR, 0 CAP, 34 ORACLE_GAP (all `tie-context event:TryMove`: the converter refuses a speed tie between two TryMove handlers of the reference, for example Queenly Majesty and another priority block; the oracle gap is in the converter, not a difference of the engine, and is outside this step). Domain check at rate 0.1: 1590 of 1708 sampled requests agreed, 0 dropped.
   - **What stays unmarked.** Mold Breaker (which would break Hyper Cutter, Queenly Majesty, Damp, Sturdy, Snow Cloak, Sand Veil and Limber), the OHKO moves (Sturdy), and every row of the "needs state" list; the expansion ends with this step by the owner's decision of 2026-10-03.
