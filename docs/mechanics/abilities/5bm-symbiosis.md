# 5bm. G69, Symbiosis

- Area: abilities
- Step: G69
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g69, duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.state.pool_setup, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data

5bm. **G69, Symbiosis** (lane B batch 3; the Cute Charm half is split off to a later step, see `_scratch/cute_charm_design.md` of the branch, not in this entry; no new public value, no new state, no new draw site, no view change, no version bump). Marked: the ability **Symbiosis** (`data/abilities.ts:4837-4856`; the Champions mod has no entry of its own). The pin, in order: `pokemon.switchFlag` returns; `source.takeItem()` (the holder must be alive and hold an item, its own Mega Stone refused by the TakeItem check of the holder: `dfi_item_takeable`); the item's TakeItem with the **user** as its second argument (a Mega Stone of the user's species is refused, `dfi_stone_refused_for`, the check Trick already uses); `pokemon.setItem(item)` on the user (alive, on the field), whose Start runs at once. A refusal changes nothing and prints nothing. On success the pin prints `-activate|holder|ability: Symbiosis|Item|[of] user` and nothing else (no `-enditem`, no `-item`). **Engine**: `dfi_symbiosis` (`src/combat/turn.c`) is the ally's AfterUseItem. **Knowledge** (`src/combat/events.c`, the fold of each viewer's knowledge of the foe): the Symbiosis ACTIVATE sets the holder's `ITEM_CONSUMED` (its item is gone, as an ITEM_END would say) and clears it for the user (its new item, as ITEM_START does); without that the observation invariant of `src/state/invariants.c` rejects the state after the pass. **Support**: Symbiosis is marked in `src/data/support_manifest.c`. It runs after `dfi_use_item_of` (useItem: Focus Sash, Red Card, Eject Button, White Herb, the seeds, the resist berries, Mental Herb) and after the two berries eaten at Update (Sitrus, after its heal; Lum, after its cure), because eatItem runs its Eat effect before AfterUseItem. **Bug Bite never runs AfterUseItem** (`data/moves.ts:1920-1930` runs only the Eat singleEvent and EatItem of the stolen berry), so the eater gets no Symbiosis (and no Unburden either, an existing fact of G64). The event is ACTIVATE (35) with the cause ABILITY, id2 Symbiosis + 1, position the holder, other the user, and **id the item + 1** (the one field of ACTIVATE that was 0 before; documented in `include/duoforge/duoforge.h` at ACTIVATE, no new value). The state is the POOL tail's `item_now`: the holder's becomes DFI_TAIL_ITEM_NONE and the user's the item (`dfi_set_held`, so a Choice Scarf's lock rule applies). **Refusals (explicit, never a guess)**: a user that holds an item at the pass (defensive: unreachable, every user has used its item up); a White Herb or a terrain seed that starts on the user at once (`dfi_start_uses_item`). The second is the one new control flow: the handler that decides it (`dfi_symbiosis`) is reached from the item-use functions, which return nothing (dfi_use_item, the berries of dfi_update and dfi_lum_berry, the damage path), so it cannot return the status at its own point without changing about a dozen callers. It sets `dfi_run.deferred = E_UNSUPPORTED` before any change of its own, and the turn returns it at its end (`dfi_turn_run`, `dfi_turn_start`). The caller runs the turn on a working copy (`src/state/request.c`), so a non-OK turn commits nothing: the battle the caller holds is unchanged, and the step returns E_UNSUPPORTED (test: `check_seed_refusal` in `tests/test_pool_g69.c`, a Psychic Seed holder whose pass meets Psychic Terrain at the step: the step returns E_UNSUPPORTED and the battle is byte-for-byte its snapshot). **Not modelled, by the pin's own text or without effect**: `pendingStaleness` of `setItem` (only Trick and Switcheroo mark it external in the pin's test; no engine staleness exists; no observable effect); Sticky Hold on the holder: **no refusal**, because a Symbiosis holder has Symbiosis as its only ability, so no Sticky Hold can stand there and its TakeItem meets none (the lead confirmed this; a refusal would be dead code). **Eject Button**: the engine sets the switch flag before its useItem (`dfi_turn_run`, the Eject Button branch) exactly as the pin does (`items.ts:1692-1694`); `g69_symbiosis_eject` shows it: no pass. **Trick's receiver check**: Trick already has it (`dfi_stone_refused_for`, used by `dfi_trick` for both sides), so Symbiosis shares that predicate; no Trick change. **Evidence**: four recorded POOL battles (`g69_*`, six members a side, genders stated; the first four of each side are the brought ones; `g69_symbiosis_sash`: Sneasler (M) holds a Focus Sash at full HP and survives a Brave Bird with 1 HP, the Sash is used through useItem (`-enditem`), and Floette-Eternal (F)'s Leftovers pass to it before the damage line, which is the path the mutant M11 of the useItem hook needs): `g69_symbiosis_sitrus` (Milotic (F) eats its Sitrus Berry at 43 percent, the heal comes first, then Floette-Eternal (F)'s Leftovers pass to Milotic; the residual heal of the Leftovers follows), `g69_symbiosis_eject` (Milotic (F) with Eject Button is hit and ejects; no pass, the Leftovers stay), `g69_symbiosis_stone` (Lucario (M) eats its Sitrus Berry; Floette-Eternal (F) holds Lucarionite, Lucario's own stone: refused, no line). Tests: `tests/test_pool_g69.c` (`duoforge.state.pool_g69`): the pass in one step, the holder none after it, the user the Leftovers; no change in the other two battles. **Campaign**: 300 random battles, seed 9690000, the G (Floette-Eternal, Symbiosis) and H teams against each other, A and B (`tools/cloud/aws_fuzz/campaigns/g69-symbiosis/`): PASS 300, the domain check 1469 requests with no change; 188 of the battles have the Floette-Eternal team, and in the kept traces 40 Symbiosis activations occur in 39 battles (164 Sitrus Berry eats in all). **Mutation** (the first count, with the three battles; the Focus Sash battle then kills M11, see below): 11 mutants, each one edit in `turn.c` or `events.c`, checked by ctest of the three G69 tests (`state.pool_g69`, `reference.conformance_pool_data`, `reference.tiebreak_pool_data`; the unmutated baseline is green): 10 caught (the switch-flag guard, the receiver stone refusal, the ACTIVATE's other, the holder's item not cleared, the event id, the holder's ability, the knowledge clear, the deferred refusal returning OK, the start-item refusal, the Sitrus order), 1 survivor, then killed: the `dfi_use_item_of` hook (the useItem paths other than the Sitrus berry at Update) survived the three battles, and is caught by `g69_symbiosis_sash` (the Focus Sash path). Red Card, White Herb and the seeds of the useItem family are not recorded (no legal battle here shows them), and only the Focus Sash path is. **Pins**: pool hash and fingerprints in the regeneration (`test_pool_tables.c`, `state_v3_model.py`, `test_pool_setup.c`, `tests/CMakeLists.txt`); DF_POOL_DATA_BATTLES 646 (642 before); `test_tiebreak.c` 646 battles and 3620 stops (3592 before; G69 adds 28). The Pokemon Showdown checkout is read only and was only read.
