# 5bg. G57, Magic Bounce and the Mega Stones Absolite and Sablenite

- Area: abilities
- Step: G57
- Tests: duoforge.abilities.magic_bounce

5bg. **G57, Magic Bounce and the Mega Stones Absolite and Sablenite** (the public meaning of one combination: a MOVE event with cause ABILITY and id2 = Magic Bounce + 1 is the bounced move line, `other` = the original user, also for a hazard; no new kind, cause or view bit; no version bump).
  - **Rule.** Magic Bounce (`data/abilities.ts:2437-2464`, priority 1 TryHit after Protect's 3, and `onAllyTryHitSide` for the foeSide hazards) bounces the twenty marked reflectable moves (`dfi_pool_move_reflectable`, generated from the pin's `flags.reflectable`, checked against the pinned Dex by `tests/reference/reflect_ref.h`) back at the user. The bounced run (`dfi_bounce`, `src/combat/turn.c`) runs as `useMove`: no BeforeMove, no PP, no pressure drain, no Choice lock (hasBounced), no Prankster immunity (pranksterBoosted false), and the holder's move result becomes the bounced result (the original's is false).
  - **Refusals (E_UNSUPPORTED, each with a test in `tests/abilities/test_magic_bounce.c`):** two holders for a hazard (the speed order of the TryHitSide handlers, with a shuffle on a tie); a Follow Me or Rage Powder on the source's side (the RedirectTarget of the bounced move); an Armor Tail on the source's side with a Prankster-boosted original (the bounced copy's priority; the recorded campaign battle fz_5700_279 is this case: the pin stops it with a `cant ... Armor Tail` line); a Psychic Terrain with a Prankster copy is not refused in practice (the terrain's TryHit stops the original at a grounded holder before Magic Bounce; the bounced check is unreachable with the pool's grounded holders). Spread reflectable moves are refused (none is marked).
  - **Refactor.** The move body's hazard branch, Protect loop, TryHit steps, accuracy loop and status tail are helpers (`dfi_hazard_move`, `dfi_protect_targets`, `dfi_try_hit_abilities`, `dfi_accuracy_targets`, `dfi_status_effects`); the current converter reproduces origin/main's closure, Team C and 409 earlier pool conformance headers byte-identically (checked on origin/main's traces); the committed pool header grows by the seven new battles, whose shared-array offsets shift.
  - **Opponent view.** `dfi_events_fold_knowledge` (`src/combat/events.c`) does not count a bounced move line as a PP use (the pin deducts none); found by the `g57_mb_yawn` conformance battle.
  - **Marks.** Magic Bounce (ability), Absolite and Sablenite (items). Clefablite stays unmarked: Clefable's abilities (Cute Charm, Magic Guard, Unaware) are unmarked. Absol has two Mega pairs (Absolite to Absol-Mega, Absolite Z to Absol-Mega-Z); the test checks each stone to its own Mega. Espeon's and Hatterene's hidden ability becomes legal, and Trace can copy Magic Bounce (no test of that copy yet).
  - **Evidence.** Seven recorded POOL battles with genders in the specs (`g57_mb_whirlwind`, `_taunt`, `_stealth_rock`, `_parting_shot`, `_paralysed_bouncer`, `_sablenite`, `_yawn`), each with a bounce that the trace shows; conformance 416 pool battles.
  - **Mutation.** Sixteen mutants (`_scratch/mutants_g57.py`; M2 is a no-op placeholder, so fifteen are real): eleven caught by the tests (M1, M3, M6, M7, M8, M9, M10, M13, M14, M15, M16). Survivors: M4 (the bounce counts as a move action of the holder; only Fake Out reads it, and no battle has it), M5 (a Prankster holder: dead, a bouncer cannot have Prankster), M11 (a Choice Scarf bouncer locked: no Scarf bouncer in a battle), M12 (the source's Protect in the bounced run: unreachable, the source is the move's user).
  - **Campaign.** 300 random POOL battles, seed 5700, teams D (`tools/cloud/aws_fuzz/campaigns/g57-magic-bounce/D.txt`) with A and B: PASS 299, UNSUPPORTED 1 (the Armor Tail refusal above), DIVERGENCE 0, ORACLE_GAP 0.
  - **Not in this step.** Trace copy of Magic Bounce; a sleeping or Scarf bouncer; Follow Me's own refusal battle (only the white-box test); the Gastro Acid and Worry Seed suppression of Magic Bounce (unmarked).

## Support

**Magic Bounce and the Mega Stones Absolite and Sablenite (step G57, decision 0015 entry 5bg)**: Magic Bounce bounces the twenty marked reflectable moves (Roar, Whirlwind, Hypnosis, Parting Shot, Soak, Encore, Will-O-Wisp, Disable, Sleep Powder, Stun Spore, Poison Powder, Toxic, the four hazards, Taunt, Yawn, Charm, Fake Tears) back at their user: a single target after Protect, a foeSide hazard through its holder on the foes' side; two holders, a Follow Me on the source's side, an Armor Tail with a Prankster copy, and a spread reflectable move are refused (E_UNSUPPORTED); the bounced move line is `[from] ability: Magic Bounce` (the MOVE event, cause ABILITY) and uses no PP; Absolite and Sablenite are marked, Clefablite is not; seven recorded POOL battles (`g57_*`, `duoforge.abilities.magic_bounce`).
