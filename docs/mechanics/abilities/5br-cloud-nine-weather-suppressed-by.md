- Area: abilities

- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g79, duoforge.state.pool_weather, duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data

5br. **G79, Cloud Nine: the weather suppressed by a standing holder** (Altaria and Drampa, the two pool formes with Cloud Nine; no public value, no new state, no new event, no new draw site, no view change, no version bump). Cloud Nine is an engine row (`ENGINE_ROWS`, handler NONE) read by id: `dfi_weather_suppressed` (a holder that is not fainted, `hp != 0` or a faint still queued, `sim/field.ts:106-116` with `!pokemon.fainted` at :109; the faint is shown at `sim/battle.ts:2556-2564`). The weather that the engine reads is one predicate, `dfi_field_weather` (`Field.effectiveWeather`, `sim/field.ts:101-104`), and the move reading of Mega Sol on top, `dfi_move_weather` (`Pokemon#effectiveWeather`, `sim/pokemon.ts:2190-2212`). Under suppression: the field's weather effects are skipped (`battle.ts:618`, `:891`: the Weather handlers, but not FieldStart, FieldResidual or FieldEnd), the residual damage and heal are not run (sand, Rain Dish, Solar Power), the 'Update' of an `eachEvent('Weather')` runs only for rain and sun (the sand and snow residuals call it only while the weather is effective, `conditions.ts` sandstorm and snowscape, `battle.ts:472`), the countdown and the `-weather [upkeep]` line continue, and the setters (raw compare, `sim/field.ts:45`) and the entry setters are unchanged. The switch-in line `-ability|holder|Cloud Nine` is the existing `DUOFORGE_EVENT_ABILITY` (34u) in the switch-in order, with Cloud Nine a bearer of the speed tie (`dfi_has_switch_in`); a copy or a swap starts it without a line. Refusals (E_UNSUPPORTED, lead decisions): a Mega Sol holder's Solar Beam, Weather Ball, Thunder, Hurricane and Electro Shot while a Cloud Nine holder suppresses the weather (`dfi_mega_sol_refused`; the `-activate Mega Sol` line of an effective field that is not sun is not modelled), and a Trace that copies Cloud Nine (`dfi_trace`). Skill Swap is modelled (the suppression follows the current ability). The pinned texts are `G79_ABILITY_FACTS` and `G79_FIELD_FACTS` (checked by `check_g79_facts`, whole lines, whitespace collapsed); the Champions mod overrides none of them.
   - **Evidence**: ten recorded POOL battles (`g79_*`; genders in each paste): `g79_cloud_nine_sun` (Drought's sun under Altaria: Sunny Day fails on the raw sun, Solar Beam charges, the countdown runs), `g79_cloud_nine_rain` (Drizzle's rain under Altaria: Rain Dance fails on the raw rain), `g79_cloud_nine_sand_sitrus` (Sand Stream's sandstorm under Altaria with a Sitrus Berry: no sandstorm damage, the berry eats), `g79_cloud_nine_roar_sand` (Roar forces the holder out and the sandstorm is effective in the same turn, its damage shows), `g79_cloud_nine_snow_both` (Snow Warning under Drampa and Altaria on both sides: Aurora Veil fails), `g79_cloud_nine_skill_swap` (Skill Swap gives Cloud Nine to Gardevoir, the sun stays suppressed), `g79_cloud_nine_faint_sun` (the holder falls to a Shadow Ball and the slower Solar Beam takes no charge after the faint, the sun is effective again). `g79_cloud_nine_sand_burn_sitrus` (Gardevoir's burn residual after a suppressed sandstorm step, and the Sitrus Berry eaten after it) and `g79_cloud_nine_speed_tie` (Altaria and Gardevoir enter in a SwitchIn speed tie, a draw of two bearers, and Trace copies Incineroar, not Cloud Nine), and `g79_cloud_nine_spread_pending` (Basculegion's spread Blizzard faints Altaria, Cloud Nine, on the first target; the second target Torkoal is then frozen by the secondary while the sun is still suppressed by the fainted, not yet shown holder, `sim/battle-actions.ts:347` runs `faintMessages` after the whole move).
