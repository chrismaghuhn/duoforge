# 5bb. G47, the status and volatile cures: Synchronize, Oblivious, Lum Berry and Mental Herb; a fix of the G36 Toxic row

- Area: abilities
- Step: G47
- Tests: duoforge.interactions.status_cures

5bb. **G47, the status and volatile cures: Synchronize, Oblivious, Lum Berry and Mental Herb; a fix of the G36 Toxic row** (no public value, no new state, no new draw site, no view change, no version bump; the Champions mod overrides none of the four). Marked: the abilities Synchronize (`data/abilities.ts:4857-4871`) and Oblivious (`data/abilities.ts:3008-3040`) and the items Lum Berry (`data/items.ts:3537-3560`) and Mental Herb (`data/items.ts:3889-3926`); all four are engine rows read by id, with the status origins of `dfi_try_status` (OTHER, MOVE, SYNC, HAZARD: what the effect that sets a status is to its messages, `src/combat/turn.c`). **Synchronize** passes a psn, tox, brn or par that another Pokemon gave the holder back to that Pokemon, after `-activate|holder|ability: Synchronize`, through its trySetStatus with the Synchronize effect: the source's immunities and fail lines are those of a move's own status, Flower Veil's block stays silent for it (`data/abilities.ts:1438`), its start line has no `[from]`, and a source that already has a status fails. Not for slp or frz, not from Toxic Spikes, not to the holder itself; the source's own Synchronize answers in turn. The paths that set a status from a source all go through it: move statuses, secondaries, Dire Claw's pick, Poison Touch, Flame Body, Static. **Lum Berry**: eaten at any status set on the holder (AfterSetStatus, priority -1, after Synchronize's 0) and at Update when the holder has a status or is confused (`-enditem|X|Lum Berry|[eat]`, then the cure with `[msg]` and the end of the confusion), refused by a foe's Unnerve (TryEatItem). The confusion half is reached through Hurricane (marked; its 30 percent secondary is a confusion). **Mental Herb**: at Update, used when its holder has a taunt, an encore, a disable or a heal block, and then those are removed in the pin's order (taunt, encore, disable, heal block); attract and torment are not modelled (no volatile of either exists). Encore on a holder of Mental Herb keeps the target's queued move (Champions `encore`, `data/mods/champions/moves.ts:330`), replacing the former E_UNSUPPORTED. **Oblivious**: Taunt is -immune to its holder; Intimidate's Attack drop is -fail with `[from] ability: Oblivious`; a Trace copy of Oblivious onto a taunted Pokemon is refused (E_UNSUPPORTED; the copy comes at entry, where no taunt stands). Its Attract and Captivate halves are not reached (unmarked moves, no Attract volatile). Breakable: no Mold Breaker is marked. **The G36 Toxic row, fixed in this step**: a tox status now resets the toxic stage when it starts (`tox` onStart, `data/conditions.ts:141-150`), and a Lum Berry cure ends the stage with the status (the state invariant allows a stage only on a badly poisoned Pokemon). The switch-in order of two entering badly poisoned Pokemon of one Speed draws the tie: the tox status has an onSwitchIn handler (`data/conditions.ts:151`, the stage back to 0), which `dfi_has_switch_in` now counts. The converter's switch-order rule already counted the reference's own handler total, so it needed no change; it and the engine now agree. The gap predates G47 (the tox status is G36's) and was found by the campaign below (seed 9800000, battle 96 of pairing DD). Numbers changed: unmodelled items 34 to 32 (then 29 with main's G49), unmodelled abilities 137 to 135; the POOL table hash is `3205ab99...` (`gen_closure.py --pool --check`), the model's `POOL_TABLE_HASH` (`tools/state_model/state_v3_model.py`) carries the same value, the POOL fingerprints are those of the model (`FP_KP` 9e89c7ff..., `FP_KPD` 031689ae...), and the pool-data conformance count is 397. **Evidence**: twenty recorded POOL battles: `g47_synchronize_*` (nine, and `g47_synchronize_flower_veil`), `g47_lum_berry`, `g47_lum_unnerve`, `g47_lum_confusion`, `g47_herb_taunt`, `g47_herb_encore`, `g47_herb_disable`, `g47_herb_heal_block`, `g47_oblivious`, `g47_switch_tox_tie` (a double replacement after two faints: the two poisoned Houndoom enter in one switch-in sort), `g47_toxic_reset` (the Lum Berry cure, then the poison starts again); `tests/interactions/test_status_cures.c` (the marks, and the state after each rule's step, including the toxic stage 1 after the re-poison); pool-data conformance; `checkG47` (stub calls of the pinned handlers). **Mutants**, one each in `src/combat/turn.c`, rebuilt: round one (six): Synchronize's rule dropped, Lum Berry's Unnerve refusal dropped, Encore's Mental Herb guard dropped, Oblivious's Taunt branch dropped, Synchronize's origin read as a move's (caught by conformance only), Lum Berry's confusion cure dropped; all six caught by at least one suite. Round two (five): Flower Veil's silence for Synchronize (caught by conformance; the G47 state test sees no line), the toxic stage reset at a status start (survives both suites: an equivalent mutant, since the stage is zero whenever the status is not tox, by the invariant and the cure clear), the toxic stage clear on a Lum Berry cure (caught by both: the invariant fails), the switch-in toxic term (caught by both), and Mental Herb's removal order of taunt and heal block (survives both: no battle puts two of the four volatiles on one holder before an Update, so the order is not observed). Round two caught 3 of 5, the two survivors are explained above. **Campaign** (`tools/cloud/aws_fuzz/campaigns/g47-cures`, 300 random battles, seed 9800000, 15 pairings): before the fix 299 PASS and 1 DIVERGENCE (`fz_9800000_96`); after the fix 300 PASS. **Python**: `duoforge.python.replay` and `duoforge.python.live` pass on the merged tree (main's #274 is in). **Open**: none in this step.
