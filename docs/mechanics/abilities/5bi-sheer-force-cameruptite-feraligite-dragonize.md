- Area: abilities
- Step: G61
- Tests: duoforge.data.pool_tables, duoforge.state.pool_setup

5bi. **G61, Sheer Force, Cameruptite and Feraligite, and Dragonize as an ATE member** (lane B; branch chris/expansion-g61-sheer-force, merged with origin/main 2be0afe3). The pin decides the strip: `data/abilities.ts:4202-4221` deletes `secondaries` and `self` only for a move that has secondaries and is not `hasSheerForceBoost`, sets `hasSheerForce`, and gives x5325/4096 to such moves and to Electro Shot (`hasSheerForceBoost`, `data/moves.ts:4656`). Self-only effects (Close Combat, Overheat, the recharge moves) are not stripped. The engine has one predicate, `dfi_sf_strips_move` in `src/combat/sheer_force.h`, on the existing columns `sec_chance` and `special` (Stone Axe, Ceaseless Edge, Ice Fang, Tri Attack). The generator checks it against the pin for every pool move, marked or not, in both directions (`tools/datagen/pool_families.js` `checkG61`: 102 stripped pool moves, one boost-only). Gated in the turn code: the generic secondaries, the placeholder rolls and hazards of Stone Axe and Ceaseless Edge, the rolls of Ice Fang and Tri Attack, the target's Emergency Exit (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:578`), and the holder's Life Orb recoil and Emergency Exit (`sim/battle-actions.ts:536`). Not gated, as the pin has it: Red Card, Eject Button, the thaw of Scald (`scripts.ts:595-600`) and recoil. **Dragonize** (Feraligatr-Mega) matches the -ate pattern exactly (4915/4096, the same noModifyType list); family ATE, parameter Dragon. Cameruptite and Feraligite are stone rows. Marked: Sheer Force, Dragonize, Cameruptite, Feraligite. Unmarked: Magma Armor, Anger Point, Shell Bell. No new public value, event, state or draw site. **Evidence**: 12 recorded POOL battles (`g61_*`): Camerupt Mega Flamethrower and Overheat; Feraligatr Life Orb (Ice Fang, then Hydro Pump with recoil); Feraligatr Mega Hyper Beam; Red Card; Eject Button; Golisopod with Emergency Exit (the stripped Flamethrower takes it to 18 percent with no switch, the control at half switches); Trace copying Sheer Force; Brave Bird recoil; Kleavor Stone Axe with and without Sheer Force (Stealth Rock only without); Rampardos Blizzard spread. **Campaign**: 300 random battles, seed 6100000, the three G61 teams against each other, A and B, PASS 300; the domain check 1646 requests, no divergence. **Mutation**: 21 mutants run (M05 and M17 are not run: no rule edit). 17 caught (M04 and M08 at compile time, an unused parameter under -Werror); 4 survive: M09 (the multiplier moved to the end of the base-power chain: the order changes the rounded result only for three-modifier combinations such as Sheer Force, Helping Hand and Expanding Force, which no battle has), M13 (Tri Attack: no Sheer Force learner in the Champions learnsets), M21 (recoil of the stripped Flare Blitz and Volt Tackle: no Sheer Force learner), M18 (the thaw path: reachable through Steelix's Scorching Sands, but no battle thaws a frozen target with it; a follow-up battle needs a foe's freeze roll). The multiplier pin is `pool_g61`'s direct check (`DFI_SHEER_FORCE_MODIFIER` 5325, 100 becomes 130). **Rule change with evidence**: the G46 guard in `tests/test_pool_tables.c` expected Sheer Force to stay unmarked until a battle shows Red Card against a Sheer Force attacker; that battle is `g61_sf_red_card`, so the guard asserts the marked state. **Pins**: pool hash `bb258dbf0a95162eef36ec04393f562b67390654896af83025193442b1b1ff2f` (`test_pool_tables.c`, `state_v3_model.py`); POOL fingerprints KP `5cf73db5…`, KPD `5621cb06…` (`test_pool_setup.c`); provenance of `pool_tables.c` `6c2ab9db…` (`tests/CMakeLists.txt`); 565 pool battles; tiebreak stops 3248; unmodelled abilities 121.
