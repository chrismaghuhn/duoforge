# 5s. G22, six abilities

- Area: abilities
- Step: G22
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_weather, duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data, duoforge.abilities.swift_swim_fainted_holder, duoforge.abilities.inner_focus_intimidate_cap

5s. **G22, six abilities** (Sand Rush 14 of the 227 survey teams, Cursed Body 13, Inner Focus 12, Swift Swim, Liquid Voice, Slush Rush and Chlorophyll: the cheap ability batch of the blocker list; the batch rule of 2026-10-03: small mechanics in one PR). The turn code runs all six by id, so they are `ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator (handler column NONE, the POOL fingerprints change with the table hash; no family, no new handler id, no public value, no state, no view change). Cursed Body stays UNMODELED and is out of this batch: see the last point.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod has no entry of any of the six: `data/mods/champions/abilities.ts` is hashed by the generator and has none of these ids). **Sand Rush** `data/abilities.ts:3974-3987`: `onModifySpe` is `chainModify(2)` while `field.isWeather('sandstorm')`, and `onImmunity` returns false for the type `'sandstorm'`, so its holder takes no Sandstorm damage (`runStatusImmunity`, `sim/battle.ts:2116`: a Weather effect on a target that fails the immunity does no damage; the Sand Rush holders of the pool are Excadrill and Lycanroc, which the types already protect, and Houndstone, a Ghost, which only the ability protects). **Swift Swim** `:4808-4818` (`pokemon.effectiveWeather()` is `raindance` or `primordialsea`), **Slush Rush** `:4347-4357` (`isWeather(['hail', 'snowscape'])`) and **Chlorophyll** `:512-522` (`sunnyday` or `desolateland`) are the same `chainModify(2)`. Hail is not in the format and the Primal weathers are not states of the engine, so each reads exactly one state weather (rain, snow, sun; sand for Sand Rush). `Pokemon.effectiveWeather` (`sim/pokemon.ts:2190-2210`) ignores sun and rain for an Utility Umbrella holder (the item is not in the pool) and `Field.effectiveWeather` returns nothing under Cloud Nine or Air Lock (Cloud Nine is unmarked, Air Lock has no row; `tests/test_pool_weather.c` and `pool_families.js` pin both). **Inner Focus** `:2153-2167`, `flags: {breakable: 1}`: `onTryAddVolatile` returns null for `flinch`, so the volatile is not added and nothing is shown (the secondary's roll is drawn before, one `SECONDARY` draw per hit target as for any secondary); `onTryBoost` deletes `boost.atk` when `effect.name === 'Intimidate'` and shows `-fail|holder|unboost|atk|[from] ability: Inner Focus|[of] holder`. `Battle.boost` (`sim/battle.ts:2020-2088`) caps the change first (`getCappedBoost`: a drop at -6 is 0 and is no `boost.atk`) and runs TryBoost before the loop, so the engine reads the capped delta; Contrary reverses before the cap. Intimidate (`:2193-2212`) names itself as the effect, also when Trace copied it. No other effect is stopped (Parting Shot lowers Attack and shows `-unboost`). **Liquid Voice** `:2416-2427`: `onModifyType` (priority -1) makes a move with `flags.sound` Water (not for a Dynamaxed user). The turn code reads the generated `flags2` sound bit, so the whole pool's sound moves are covered by the one rule (25 moves have the flag; the modelled ones are Hyper Voice, Snarl, Psychic Noise, Parting Shot and the sound-flag status moves that exist as rows); the changed type reaches the immunity, STAB, weather and resist-berry steps through `move_type` (data/mods/champions has no `onModifyType` of its own).
   - **Order of the Speed abilities.** `dfi_speed_key` multiplies the chain with the others (Tailwind, Choice Scarf, Unburden: every factor is an exact multiple of 4096, so the order of the chain is free and one rounding follows; paralysis comes last). Only a standing holder counts: a fainted one has its raw Speed, the rule of `dfi_move_priority` (#126; `ignoringAbility` is true for a Pokemon that is not active). `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` `ENGINE_ORDER` now names the four abilities as the modelled rows with a Speed callback, so a fifth cannot enter without this function being read. The speed of the weather changes from the action that sets it (Battle.updateSpeed after each action, as for Tailwind); the residual order (`dfi_residual_events`) reads `speed_seen` and so the same key.
   - **Event.** The Inner Focus line is a `DUOFORGE_EVENT_FAIL` with `cause` ABILITY, `id2` the ability + 1 and `other` the holder (the converter, `tools/reference/trace_to_c.py`, maps exactly `-fail|X|unboost|atk|[from] ability: Inner Focus|[of] X` and refuses every other `unboost` line). No new constant: a documented use of fields that the event already has (`include/duoforge/duoforge.h`).
   - **Flower Veil.** Both Flower Veil and Inner Focus delete an Intimidate drop through TryBoost, and which runs first depends on the Speed of the two holders (a tie is a shuffle). They meet only on a Grass type with Inner Focus on a side with Flower Veil: no Inner Focus holder of the pool is a Grass type (`pool_families.js` `checkG22`).
   - **Tables.** The six abilities lose their feature list and take handler NONE: the unmodelled abilities go from 179 to 173; the POOL table hash, the generated file hashes and the two fingerprints are repinned (decision 0015 section 2).
   - **Evidence.** Eight recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated, seeds picked where a roll mattered): `g22_swift_swim` (no rain: Raichu first; the rain comes in with Politoed, Basculegion moves before it in the same turn; Kingambit's Sucker Punch knocks Basculegion out and its queued action, priced at its raw Speed, ties Politoed's: a `SPEED_TIE` draw of the queue site), `g22_sand_rush` (Houndstone sets the sandstorm, outpaces a 130-Speed Raichu with Shadow Ball, and neither Sand Rush holder takes sand damage while Raichu and Milotic do), `g22_slush_rush` (Beartic after Milotic on the turn of its Snowscape, before it on the next), `g22_chlorophyll` (Drought comes in with a switch: Venusaur, 100 raw, and Whimsicott, 136, move before the 178-Speed Raichu that outpaced them without the sun; Tailwind on top later, x4), `g22_foreign_weather` (the four abilities in the sun and the snow that are not theirs: nothing doubles), `g22_speed_tie_snow` (Beartic's 140 in snow ties Sneasler's raw 140: the queue ties and the two Leftovers heals of the residual are shuffles, checked against the residual list of #154), `g22_inner_focus` (Intimidate at the start and at a switch-in: `-fail` for Dragonite, `-unboost` for Raichu; Fake Out and a 30 percent Rock Slide flinch Raichu and never Dragonite; Parting Shot lowers its Attack) and `g22_liquid_voice` (Psychic Noise hits a Dark type, Hyper Voice a Ghost, both as Water with the rain; Moonblast stays Fairy). `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` replays all of them with the reference's draws (every HP, line and draw), `duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool_data` with the reference's tiebreak; `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py` derives the effect of each ability from the protocol lines alone, and `duoforge.abilities.swift_swim_fainted_holder` checks the tie of the fainted Swift Swim holder's action (the tape without that entry fails exactly there) and `duoforge.abilities.inner_focus_intimidate_cap` the Intimidate drop that the cap already took to nothing (a hand-made state: no battle reaches -6 Attack on an Inner Focus holder). Mutation checks, the campaign and the counts are in the PR text.
   - **Cursed Body is not in this batch.** `data/abilities.ts:784-797`: `onDamagingHit` (not at a Max or future move or Struggle, not while the attacker is Disabled) with `randomChance(3, 10)` adds the `disable` volatile (`data/moves.ts:3648-3716`) to the attacker: 5 turns (4 when the attacker has not moved or is the active Pokemon), `-start|X|Disable|Move|[from] ability: Cursed Body|[of] holder`, the onDisableMove lock of the request, `cant|X|Disable|Move`, the end in the residual at order 17 and `-end`. That is new state use (`disable_slot` and `disable_turns` of the tail), a request lock, a view field and at least two new public values (a volatile id and a cause for the `cant`), and a hook in the DamagingHit loop, so it is a PR of its own (owner rule of 2026-10-03: new state or a new public value goes in a separate PR).
