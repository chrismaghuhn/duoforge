# 5di. Mega Sol cancels the sandstorm's Special Defense and the snow's Defense for its holder's hits (issue 521; branch claude/521-mega-sol-sand; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).

5di. **Mega Sol cancels the sandstorm's Special Defense and the snow's Defense for its holder's hits (issue 521; branch claude/521-mega-sol-sand; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** Pin: Sandstorm's onModifySpD and Snow's onModifyDef read `pokemon.effectiveWeather()` of the defender with the weather condition as the effect (`data/conditions.ts:640-645` and `:706-711`); `Pokemon.effectiveWeather` (`sim/pokemon.ts:2190-2198`) answers sun while the active Pokemon has Mega Sol and the effect is a move, a weather or Mega Sol. So during the Mega Sol holder's move the Rock defender gets no 1.5x Special Defense in a sandstorm, and the Ice defender no 1.5x Defense in snow. The engine read the field weather there. Now the Defense chain of `dfi_get_damage` (`src/combat/turn.c`) reads `dfi_move_weather(r, a, true)`, the same reading the engine already uses for Weather Ball and the other effectiveWeather sites. **Evidence.** The recording `tests/reference/specs/fz521_mega_sol_sand.json` (fz_2_42 of the campaign, cut after its step 2; seed and choices of the campaign, trace from the pinned Showdown b2cb775): Meganium-Mega hits its ally Tyranitar with Energy Ball in Tyranitar's sandstorm (175 to 31, after Sludge Bomb took 11 and poisoned it), then Scald knocks Tyranitar out. On main it diverges at step 1 (Tyranitar keeps 10 HP; `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` and `tiebreak_pool_data` fail); with the fix both pass. The mutant is the old line, so it is the main run. **Gap:** the snow branch has the same change and no recording (no fuzz case and no recorded battle has a Mega Sol holder's physical hit on an Ice defender in snow); a mutant of only that branch is not caught. Every other reference battle is unchanged: the pool set grows by this one (991 battles; the format pin's pool line is updated for it), the conformance and tiebreak headers only by its counts.
