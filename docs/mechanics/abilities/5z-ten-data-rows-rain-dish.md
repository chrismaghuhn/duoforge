# 5z. G35, ten data rows, Rain Dish and Friend Guard

- Area: abilities
- Step: G35
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g35

5z. **G35, ten data rows, Rain Dish and Friend Guard** (the next batch of E's audit of the rev 4 blockers, `tail-rev4-proposal.md` section 6, after G30, G32, G33 and G34: rows that need no state, by blocked teams; Friend Guard alone is on 1056 of the Reg M-B teams of the 2026-10-03 survey). Twelve rows: the moves Thunder Punch, X-Scissor, Lumina Crash, Overdrive, Scorching Sands, Leaf Blade, Boomburst, Sludge Wave, Volt Tackle and Discharge, and the abilities Rain Dish and Friend Guard. No new public value, no new state, no view change, no new handler id.
   - **Deferred: Gigaton Hammer.** It was in the plan (a `cantusetwice` move: the pin refuses the same move twice in a row, and an Encore'd second use prints only a `-hint` line, which the converter and the Python reader already skip). It is not in the step because its only learner, Tinkaton, has Mold Breaker, Own Tempo and Pickpocket as legal abilities and none of them is marked, so no setup can use it, no battle can be recorded and an engine rule for it would be untested. `duoforge.state.pool_g35` fails the day one of the three is marked: Gigaton Hammer is then the next row (the rule: the second flags byte has the free bit 128, the request disables the slot whose `last_move` is the move).
   - **The pin.** Moves (`data/moves.ts`, the Champions mod overrides none of them): the data rows are the generic columns (power, accuracy, flags, the secondary kinds: Lumina Crash's 100 percent Special Defense -2, Scorching Sands' 30 percent burn with `thawsTarget`, Volt Tackle's recoil of a third, 10 percent paralysis for Thunder Punch and Volt Tackle, 30 percent for Discharge, 10 percent poison for Sludge Wave, critical ratio 2 for Leaf Blade, the target classes `allAdjacentFoes` (Overdrive) and `allAdjacent` (Boomburst, Sludge Wave, Discharge)); the flags that other rules read were added by G34 (`punch`: Thunder Punch for Iron Fist; `slicing`: X-Scissor and Leaf Blade for Sharpness; `sound`: Overdrive and Boomburst for Soundproof and Throat Chop). Abilities (`data/abilities.ts`): Friend Guard `:1533-1544`, `onAnyModifyDamage`, breakable: when the target is not the holder and is the holder's ally, `chainModify(0.75)`; Rain Dish `:3759-3770`, `onWeather`: in `raindance` (and `primordialsea`, which the format lacks) `this.heal(target.baseMaxhp / 16)`. The generator checks both texts (`G35_ABILITY_FACTS`, whole, the Champions mod overriding neither) and both abilities are `ENGINE_ROWS` (handler column NONE: the turn code reads them by id); `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` mirrors the list. The POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step; no handler id moves (UNMODELED stays 47).
   - **Friend Guard in the ModifyDamage chain** (`turn.c`, `dfi_get_damage`). The handler is `onAny`: every active Pokemon that has not fainted runs it for every hit, so the weakening applies when the target's partner stands and holds the ability (the holder itself is not weakened; the attacker's own partner is a holder too when the attacker hits its ally). It is one more modifier, 3072/4096 (`chainModify(0.75)`), after Solid Rock and Multiscale. Like those it has no order of its own: the pin sorts the handlers by their holders' speed, then by sub-order, and this build does not compute that order but requires every order of the modifiers of one hit to chain to one value (`dfi_mods_commute`, G34). One hit now has up to six modifiers (Life Orb or Expert Belt, a resist berry, a screen, Glaive Rush's volatile, Solid Rock or Multiscale, Friend Guard), so `DFI_MODIFY_DAMAGE_MAX` is 6 and the subsets that one hit can have are 143: **85 commute and 58 do not (G34 alone: 53 of 71, 18)**. A hit with a combination that does not commute is E_UNSUPPORTED, as before; `duoforge.state.pool_g35` compares `dfi_mods_commute` with a brute-force walk of all 143 subsets and plays the refusal itself (an Expert Belt on the attacker of `g35_friend_guard`, with Multiscale and Friend Guard on its target, step 1). The bad sets with Friend Guard but without Expert Belt or Glaive Rush all have four modifiers.
   - **Rain Dish** (`turn.c`, `dfi_rain_dish`). In the weather upkeep under rain, after the upkeep line and in eachEvent's order, every active Pokemon that has not fainted and holds Rain Dish heals `floor(max HP / 16)`, at least 1, as `-heal ... [from] ability: Rain Dish` (no `[of]`): nothing is printed at full HP, and Heal Block refuses it (the common heal path). Two holders at one Speed share the shuffle of the Weather event, which draws whether or not either is damaged (the pin sorts every active Pokemon); the converter keeps the draw only in rain with two holders and drops it in any other weather (a holder has the handler under every weather and it does nothing there) and with one holder. Under Sandstorm a holder's extra handler is ignored by the Sandstorm tie rule.
   - **Converter** (`tools/reference/trace_to_c.py`). `friendguard` 3072 joins `modify_damage_values`; a ModifyDamage tie with Friend Guard twice (a holder on each side, or two on one side: the handler applies only to the holder's allies other than itself, so at most one of them applies to a hit) is dropped as one value in every order; `raindish` is an each-event handler of its holder alone (`EACH_HANDLERS`). Unit tests: `test_friend_guard_ties_step_g35`, `test_rain_dish_weather_ties_step_g35`.
   - **Evidence.** Nine recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g35_*`, seeds by predicate): `blades_punches` (`0x3502`: X-Scissor, Leaf Blade with a critical hit and Thunder Punch of Gallade, whose Sharpness boosts the slicing moves and not the punch, Thunder Punch with Iron Fist and Scorching Sands of Infernape; Steel resists the blades), `sands_thaw` (`0x351a`: Gallade's Ice Punch freezes Milotic and Torkoal's Scorching Sands, a Ground move, thaws it), `lumina_crash` (`0x3500`: -2 Special Defense answered by Competitive, Speed Boost), `toxtricity_sound` (`0x3500`: Overdrive at the foes and Boomburst at everyone, Soundproof of a foe and of the ally immune), `toxtricity_spread` (`0x3500`: Sludge Wave and Discharge hit the ally; Steel is immune to the poison hit, Ground to Discharge), `volt_tackle` (`0x3500`: recoil of a third of the damage, none against an immune target), `rain_dish` (`0x3500`: two Blastoise of equal Speed on the two sides heal in the rain that Pelipper set, the tie is drawn at each upkeep, the heals stop when the rain ends), `friend_guard` (`0x3502`: Maushold's partner Dragonite takes 0.75 with Multiscale and a Life Orb attacker in the chain, Maushold itself takes none), `friend_guard_faint` (`0x3500`: the holder is knocked out and a later hit in the same turn on its partner is not weakened). **Mutation checks.** 12 edits of `turn.c`, each built and run against the pool conformance, tiebreak, terrain, family, corpus and `pool_g35` tests: Friend Guard never applied, 0.5, protecting its holder, held by the attacker's partner, a fainted holder still weakening, missing from the commutation list, the commutation test off; Rain Dish in no weather, an eighth, no ability cause, no tie draw. **All 12 are red** (the fainted holder survived the first run, which asked for `friend_guard_faint`; the commutation-test-off edit is caught by `pool_g35` only).
   - **Campaign.** Three differential runs against the pin with two new teams (`L`: Maushold with Friend Guard beside Dragonite with Multiscale, an Expert Belt and a Life Orb among the attackers, Rain Dish and Life Dew, Volt Tackle and Discharge, the blades; `M`: Toxtricity with the sound and spread moves, a second Rain Dish holder, Scorching Sands, Recover and Life Dew at full HP and below, Iron Fist), paired with each other and with the registry teams A and B: seeds 3501 (300 battles), 3501 again after the converter fix and 3502 (400). **The first run found 11 ORACLE_GAPs of one signature** (`modifydamage-tie friendguard`: the two holders of the mirror pairing tied in one hit; fixed in the converter above, not the engine); **after the fix, 700 battles: PASS 700, 0 divergences; in all three runs 0 refusals** (a refusal is the UNSUPPORTED bucket); the domain check agreed in 1183 of 1183 and 1529 of 1529 requests of the two later runs. The teams include a full-HP heal (Life Dew, Recover) for the path that G32's loop got wrong (#180).
   - **Refusal rate of the chain** (asked for by the coordinator: above about 0.5 percent of battles the exact-order analysis of G34's text would belong in this step). Measured: 0 refusals in the 1000 battles of the three runs, whose teams were built to meet the refusal (Friend Guard, Multiscale and an Expert Belt on one side), about 21,000 steps, so below 0.3 percent of battles with 95 percent confidence even for them; estimated for the surveyed teams (`g35_fgrate` over the 43,258 parsed teams of the survey, 1510 with Friend Guard: the partner's ability and berry, a screen in the team, a Life Orb or Expert Belt on the attacker by the survey's item shares, a super effective hit one time in four): about 0.002 percent of battles, because the bad sets with Friend Guard need an Expert Belt (0.4 percent of the surveyed Pokemon) or a Multiscale or Solid Rock partner (30 of 7550 candidates) together with a berry, a screen or a Life Orb. The exact-order analysis is therefore not part of G35.
   - **Remaining refusals.** The 58 combinations of the chain (listed by `pool_g35`'s brute force), and what G34 left: two Eject Button holders at one Speed in one move, an ally under Heal Block that Life Dew would heal. Refusals met in the campaign: none.
