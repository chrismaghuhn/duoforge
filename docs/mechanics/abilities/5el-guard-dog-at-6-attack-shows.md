5el. **Guard Dog at +6 Attack shows the 0 change** (Content lane, Opus builder, branch `chris/fix-guard-dog-plus6`, issue 684, found by the review of the issue 640 fix; no new public value, draw site or residual kind). Pin: `data/abilities.ts:1727-1744` (`guarddog.onTryBoost`: `this.boost({ atk: 1 }, target, target, null, false, true)`), `sim/battle.ts:2020-2088` (`boost`: a change of 0 by an ability shows `msg|target|stat|0` when `isSecondary || isSelf`); the Champions mod overrides neither.
   - **The pin.** Guard Dog deletes Intimidate's Attack drop and raises the holder's Attack by 1 with a nested boost whose sixth argument, `isSelf`, is true. Its effect is Guard Dog (`effect ||= this.effect`). At +6 the capped change is 0: no `-ability` line (that line comes only with a real change), and `-boost|holder|atk|0` shows.
   - **The engine before.** `dfi_boost` (`src/combat/turn.c`, the Guard Dog branch) ran the nested boost as `DFI_BOOST_PRIMARY`. For an ability, the 0-change line shows only in a non-primary mode, so nothing showed: one protocol line missing, a silent divergence.
   - **The engine now.** The nested boost is `DFI_BOOST_SELF`. In `dfi_boost` the mode is read in three places only: the Clear Body, Flower Veil and Hyper Cutter family lines test `MOVE` and `SECONDARY`, the `-ability` announcement tests `SECONDARY`, and the 0-change line tests "not primary" for an ability. So `SELF` changes the 0-change line and nothing else; the `-ability` line and a real +1 are as before.
   - **Reach.** Mabosstiff learns no move that raises its own Attack in the Champions learnsets (`data/mods/champions/learnsets.ts`). Skill Swap gives Guard Dog to a Pokemon that has Swords Dance: Gallade learns both.
- **Evidence.** The reference battle `guard_dog_plus6_intimidate` (pool, seed ending `68a4`, recorded with `ps_trace.js`; `--check` matches): Gallade (M) takes Guard Dog from its ally Mabosstiff (M) by Skill Swap and goes to +6 Attack with three Swords Dances. Gyarados then replaces Chimecho, and its Intimidate gives Gallade `-boost|p1a: Gallade|atk|0` with no `-ability` line; Mabosstiff, now with Steadfast, takes `-unboost atk 1`. On main f9a026c3, `diff_driver.py replay` makes it a DIVERGENCE at step 5 (event 2: the reference's BOOST of Gallade with amount 0 is missing, the engine goes on to Mabosstiff's UNBOOST). With the fix it is a PASS. `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` replays the battle; its pins were captured for the new battle only (the pool bound is not raised: it is a lower bound, HS rule of 2026-10-24). `duoforge.abilities.guard_dog_plus6` (white-box, both POOL kinds) runs step 5 on the reference tape at +6 (one `-boost atk 0`, no `-ability` line) and, as its neighbour, at +5 (the `-ability` line and `-boost atk 1`); Mabosstiff loses 1 Attack in both. Mutant (one lock job, green baseline, then restored and rebuilt): the nested boost back to `DFI_BOOST_PRIMARY` is killed by `conformance_pool_data` (this battle only, step 5) and by the white-box test (no `-boost atk 0` at +6).
