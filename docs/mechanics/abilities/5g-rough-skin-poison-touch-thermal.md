5g. **G14, Rough Skin, Poison Touch and Thermal Exchange** (three abilities that the turn code runs by id: they are `ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator, so their handler column is NONE and the POOL fingerprints change with the table hash; no family, no public value: the damage and the status use the existing `CAUSE_ABILITY` with the holder or the attacker as `other`, and the boost the Stamina path). Pin (the Champions mod has no override of any of them): **Rough Skin** `data/abilities.ts:3938-3950`: `onDamagingHitOrder: 1`, so it runs before Rocky Helmet (order 2, `data/items.ts:5295-5309`) and before every unordered handler (Stamina, Thermal Exchange, the attacker's Poison Touch); `checkMoveMakesContact` (`sim/battle.ts:1289-1298`: the contact flag, unless the attacker holds Protective Pads, which the pool does not have; Long Reach, which would delete the flag from its holder's moves, is unmarked) then `damage(source.baseMaxhp / 8, source, target)`: truncated, at least 1 (`spreadDamage`, `sim/battle.ts:2091-2150`; `Pokemon.damage`), the line `-damage|attacker|hp|[from] ability: Rough Skin|[of] holder`, also when the hit knocked the holder out, and the attacker's Emergency Exit sees it like Rocky Helmet's. Iron Barbs has the same entry (`data/abilities.ts:2224-2235`) but is not in the legal pool, so it has no row. **Poison Touch** `data/abilities.ts:3370-3383`: `onSourceDamagingHit` (the attacker's handler, appended after the target's own handlers, `sim/battle.ts:1035-1063`, the sort `compareLeftToRightOrder` is stable): for a contact move (`checkMoveMakesContact(move, target, source)`) `randomChance(3, 10)` (one `random(10)`, a new draw site `POISON_TOUCH` 16 of the tape, named by `ps_trace.js` through the effect and the event: no committed trace had one, the harness version stays 14) and then `trySetStatus('psn', source)`: `[-status] psn [from] ability: Poison Touch [of] attacker` (`data/conditions.ts` psn `onStart`); the roll is drawn also for a Poison or Steel type and for a target that is down (the handler runs first, the status fails after); Shield Dust and Covert Cloak, which the pin lets block it, are not marked; Sheer Force does not touch it (it is not a secondary). **Thermal Exchange** `data/abilities.ts:4990-5018`: `onDamagingHit`: a Fire move (`move.type`, after an -ate or Weather Ball change) raises the holder's Attack by 1 (`-ability|holder|Thermal Exchange|boost`, `-boost`: the Stamina path), also for each target of a spread Heat Wave; `onSetStatus` refuses every burn (a move's own burn shows `-immune|holder|[from] ability: Thermal Exchange`, a secondary such as Flare Blitz's or an item's burn shows nothing, `sim/pokemon.ts:1724` and `data/abilities.ts:5003-5010`; the secondary's roll is drawn all the same); its `onUpdate` cure of a burn that a holder already has is unreachable (a member starts without a status and the ability blocks every burn).
