5bk. **G65, the simple modifier abilities: Unaware, Marvel Scale, Water Bubble, Reckless and Super Luck** (lane B, batch 3; no public value, no new state, no new event, no new volatile, no new draw site, no new pool column, no flags3 bit, no view change, no version bump). The five are engine rows (`ENGINE_ROWS` of the generator, handler NONE, family NONE) that `src/combat/turn.c` reads by id. The pinned texts are `G65_ABILITY_FACTS` (whole callbacks, whitespace collapsed), checked by `check_g34_facts`; `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` probes them (`G65_PROBES`, and the Water Bubble x2 in `ENGINE_STAT_MODIFIERS`). None is overridden by the Champions mod. Mold Breaker is not marked, so no breakable ability is ignored.
  - **Unaware** (`data/abilities.ts:5214-5234`, breakable). The zeroed stages follow whose stat is READ (`sim/pokemon.ts:581`, `sim/battle-actions.ts:716-720, 917-926, 1702-1703`), not whose stage it is. When the target holds it, the user's attack-side reads (Attack, Defense, Special Attack) are zeroed, including Foul Play, whose read is the target's own Attack. When the user holds it, the target's defence-side reads (Defense, Special Defense) and its evasion are zeroed. A self-target (a confusion hit) is not touched. The accuracy check of `dfi_accuracy_check` reads the same way (the Keen Eye pattern).
  - **Marvel Scale** (`abilities.ts:2534-2545`, priority 6, breakable): x1.5 on the holder's Defence while it has any status, in the ModifyDef event, so Psyshock's Defence is covered and a special hit is not. The weather's 1.5 and this one are one chained modifier (`sim/battle.ts:2382` applies the product once).
  - **Water Bubble** (`abilities.ts:5377-5417`, breakable): x2 for its Water moves of either category (ModifyAtk and ModifySpA), x0.5 for a Fire move against its holder (onSource, priority 5, as Thick Fat), no burn (`onSetStatus`, the -immune line for a move's own status only, `primary`), and the Trace refusal below.
  - **Reckless** (`abilities.ts:3799-3811`, priority 23, not breakable): x4915/4096 for a move with `move.recoil`, i.e. the move data's recoil fraction (`recoil[1] != 0`). Struggle has none (`struggleRecoil` is a flag, `moves.ts:18228`). The crash moves (`hasCrashDamage`: Axe Kick, High Jump Kick, Supercell Slam) are UNMODELLED; the generator refuses a modelled one while Reckless has no crash clause (`pool_families.js` `checkG65`, `RECKLESS_CRASH_CLAUSE = false`).
  - **Super Luck** (`abilities.ts:4703-4711`, not breakable): critRatio + 1 before the clamp to 0..4 (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1623-1633`). The draw is the existing CRIT site. A 0-ratio move cannot occur: `sim/dex-moves.ts:483` makes a data critRatio of 0 into 1.
  - **Trace** (`dfi_trace`): copying Water Bubble onto a burned holder is E_UNSUPPORTED, as Limber is refused, because its onUpdate cure is not modelled (`abilities.ts:5400-5405`).
  - **Unmodified reads** (`getStat(..., true)`, no ModifyBoost): `moves.ts:10367` (Light That Burns the Sky), `:13346` (Photon Geyser), `:16230` (Shell Side Arm), `abilities.ts:1022` (Download). Only Shell Side Arm is a pool row, and it is UNMODELLED; the other three are not pool rows. `tests/abilities/test_modifier_abilities.c, tests/abilities/test_reckless.c, tests/abilities/test_trace_water_bubble.c` pins both facts.
  - **Evidence** (`tests/reference/specs/g65_*`, nine recorded POOL battles; six-member teams, the four brought by team preview; the Champions learnsets; Item and Species Clause on each side). `g65_unaware_foul_play` and its control (Intimidate lowers Clefable's Attack, Kingambit's Foul Play at it: the holder's own -1 stage is ignored; the control is Milotic with Marvel Scale). `g65_unaware_accuracy`: Milotic (Coil: Attack, Defense, accuracy +1) hits Clefable with Double-Edge (Attack ignored) and Muddy Water, which misses Clefable at a draw of 90 (the threshold is 85, not 113), and Clefable hits Milotic with Double-Edge (its Defense +1 ignored). `g65_marvel_scale` and its control: Will-O-Wisp burns Milotic, then Kowtow Cleave does less damage to it. `g65_water_bubble`: Liquidation is doubled (Water x2 in the ModifyAtk chain), Flare Blitz does half (Fire x0.5 at the holder), Will-O-Wisp is refused with the -immune line, and the burn of the Flare Blitz is refused silently (its roll succeeds, so the refusal is what the battle shows). `g65_super_luck`: Night Slash and Sucker Punch take the raised ratios (CRIT draws). `g65_reckless` and its control: Brave Bird of Reckless Staraptor does 115 against 96 for the Intimidate control; Close Combat, which is not boosted, is the same in both. **Not battle-covered**: the holder-as-user evasion (the Keen Eye sibling line in `dfi_accuracy_check`): no marked move raises evasion (Double Team and Minimize are unmodelled), so it is pinned by the code path only. Water Absorb's control is not possible: Araquanid's only legal abilities are Water Bubble and Water Absorb, and Water Absorb is unmarked.

## Support

**Unaware, Marvel Scale, Water Bubble, Reckless and Super Luck (step G65, decision 0015 entry 5bk)**: Unaware zeroes the stages that the pin reads (the attack-side stages of the user when the target holds it, the defence-side stages and the evasion of the target when the user holds it, the accuracy of the user, Foul Play included); Marvel Scale is Defense x1.5 with a status (Psyshock too); Water Bubble is Fire x0.5 at the holder, Water x2 for its own Water moves, and no burn (a Trace copy onto a burned holder is refused); Reckless is x1.2 for a move with a recoil (Struggle and the crash moves are not); Super Luck is critical ratio +1. Engine rows (no public value, no new state). Mold Breaker is not marked, so no breakable ability is ignored. Unmodified-stat reads of the pin: only Shell Side Arm is a pool row and it is unmodelled. Evidence: nine recorded POOL battles (`g65_*`); the holder-as-user evasion line is not battle-covered (no marked move raises evasion); the 300-battle campaign waits for the 17:30 slot.
