5al. **G48, four moves on the fields that tail rev 4 already has (Rage Fist, Stone Axe, Ceaseless Edge, Population Bomb; Quick Guard dropped)**. No new public value, no event kind, no state field, no view change: `hits_taken` and the hazard fields of G37 are the state. The batch takes the rows that need nothing beyond those fields; the row that needs a public value is dropped.
   - **Dropped: Quick Guard** (data/moves.ts:14489-14520). Its block line `-activate|X|move: Quick Guard` needs a new BLOCKED detail (`DUOFORGE_BLOCK_QUICK_GUARD`), a public value that the lead has not approved; the side condition, the stall counter (Wide Guard's `addVolatile('stall')`) and the view bit `DUOFORGE_SIDE_GUARD_QUICK_GUARD` already exist, so the row waits for that value alone.
   - **Rage Fist** (data/moves.ts:14583-14596; the Champions mod only inherits it, data/mods/champions/moves.ts:793-796, and resets the counter in Pokemon#clearVolatile, data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:123-170). Power `min(350, 50 + 50 x timesAttacked)` of the user. The pin counts the damaging hits of a move on its target after the move (scripts.ts:565, sim/battle-actions.ts:994, `hit - 1`); the engine counts each one at the damage of that hit, under the POOL kinds only, saturating at 6 (which is already 350), in the hit loop of `dfi_run_move`. A switch-out and a faint clear the position (`dfi_tail_clear_occupant`, which is the pin's clearVolatile). Learner: Annihilape with Inner Focus or Defiant (both marked).
   - **Stone Axe and Ceaseless Edge** (data/moves.ts:18069-18095 and 2220-2246). `onAfterHit` runs for every damaged target, after DamagingHit and before the attacker's Emergency Exit, with no test of the user's HP (data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:411-414): `source.side.foeSidesWithConditions()` gets Stealth Rock (Stone Axe) or one Spikes layer (Ceaseless Edge) through the G37 add, which puts a layer or does nothing and prints nothing when the side is full. Sheer Force is unmarked, so a holder is refused. The `onAfterSubDamage` of both is for a Substitute, which is not marked. Learners: Kleavor (Swarm) and Samurott-Hisui (Torrent), both marked.
   - **Population Bomb** (data/moves.ts:13613-13626; no Champions entry). `multihit: 10, multiaccuracy: true`, accuracy 90, so the later-hit proof of G33 (`dfi_multiaccuracy_proven(90)`) covers it.
