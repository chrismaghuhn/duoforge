5dv. **A lock holder put to sleep before its locked turn ends its lock through the duration (issue 606; found by lane A's lock-family check; branch chris/lockfam-sleep-fix on main 19e8d47a, after the residual runner of restructure step 4, #621; the prepared material of `chris/lockfam-sleep-prep` 935f909c merged in; design `docs/research/expansion/lockfam-sleep.md`).** At the pin the Residual event counts a handler's duration down before its callback (`sim/battle.ts:515-522`): a `lockedmove` (`data/conditions.ts:253-285`; Outrage, Thrash, Petal Dance and Raging Fury share it) that was not restarted this turn is at duration 1 there, so the countdown reaches 0 and ends it with `onEnd` (`:278-281`): the fatigue confusion when `trueDuration <= 1` (`|-start|X|confusion|[fatigue]`, `:165-175`, with its CONFUSION_TURNS draw), and its `onResidual` (the silent delete of a sleeping holder, `:257-263`) does not run that turn. With the refusals of step G56 the only way a lock stands unrestarted at the residual is a holder whose move a sleep stopped (`|cant|X|slp`). The engine removed such a lock silently (`dfi_residual_lock`), one draw short when the count was 1 (`tape 4 of 5`).

The fix (HS decision, variant (b) of the design's section 3.4: the fact lives in the state, so a PIVOT pause between the holder's `cant` and the residual keeps it, and no refusal is added):

- **The bit.** `DFI_SINGLE_TURN_LOCK_UNUSED` = 8, bit 3 of the tail's `single_turn` byte (`src/state/battle_internal.h:296-316`, the field comment `:410`; the mask `DFI_TAIL_SINGLE_TURN_MASK` 7 -> 15, bits 4-7 free; `position_flags` has no free bit any more, all eight are taken). It is an **internal state bit, not a public value**: no event, cause, volatile, POSITION_EXT bit or view field, so no number of lane A's block is used (POSITION_EXT 0x01000000 stays free). It is encoded with the byte it lives in (`DFI_ENC_TAIL_POS_SINGLE_TURN_OFF` 28, rev 5 schema 0x0503): the layout, the schema and the codec version do not change, exactly as for 5de's bit 2; the decoder accepts bit 3 under the invariant below. No table changes: the POOL fingerprints and `POOL_TABLE_HASH` are unchanged (`duoforge.state.pool_setup` passes as it stands).
- **Hidden, not in the observation.** The bit is not publicly derivable: its owner knows its own lock (its request and `lockedmove` in its observation) and sees the `cant` line, so it learns nothing from it; the foe cannot know whether the lock still runs at all (`dfi_maybe_lockedmove`, `src/state/knowledge.h`: the count is drawn and its fatigue can end silently), so exposing the bit would leak the lock. It stands only at a PIVOT pause, where `duoforge_battle_public` refuses anyway. The observation, its extension, the request and the factored domain are unchanged, and with them the observation digest of decision 0082 (built from those and the projected lines): `duoforge.state.pool_lockfam_sleep` compares all four, byte for byte and for both players, at each of the three pauses with and without the bit. The privileged state digest (`duoforge_battle_digest`, the hash of the encoding) does see it, as it sees every hidden value.
- **Set** at the stopped use (`src/combat/flow/turn.h:592-619`): after the unchanged refusal of a holder that is not asleep, a standing holder with `lock_turns != 0` gets the bit, at `mover` after the Ally Switch remap of 5dn(h). The tail position moves whole on a swap (`dfi_swap_position_state`), so the bit follows the Pokemon, and it ends with the occupant (`dfi_tail_clear_occupant`) and with the lock (`dfi_lock_remove`, `src/combat/turn.c:2091-2105`).
- **Read** by the lock's handler in phase 6 (the no-order phase) of `dfi_residual_events_run`, in the pin's two phases (`dfi_residual_lock`, `src/combat/turn.c:9201-9225`): the duration phase ends a marked lock with `dfi_lock_end` (onEnd; a Misty Terrain or Own Tempo stops the confusion inside `dfi_add_volatile` as at the normal end) and returns, so the callback does not run; otherwise the callback phase is unchanged (the silent delete of a holder put to sleep after its restarted use). The place in the order is unchanged (no order, sub-order 2, Speed order in the no-order phase), so the fatigue line and its draw come where the silent removal was. The bit is read, not the status: a holder cured after its `cant` is ended too.
- **Invariant and state model** (`src/state/invariants.c:784-785`, `:978-991`; `tools/state_model/state_v3_model.py:79-83`, `:1067`, `:1160-1168`): the bit only with a running lock (`TAIL_POSITION`), only at a PIVOT or TERMINAL boundary (every other boundary is after a residual or before the first turn), and with no queued move of its position in the rest of the turn (its use is behind it; review hardening).
- **Phase 6 around the lock (review of the fix).** The lock's two phases make two shortcuts of the no-order phase visible, both now modelled or refused (`src/combat/turn.c:10002-10007`, `:10063-10087`): (a) at a battle end inside phase 6 the equal-Speed refusal also counts a lock holder (`had[]`): whether its end (a marked lock's fatigue line and draw) or its `onResidual` (and the faint point after it) ran before the battle-ending faint point of an equal-Speed Pokemon is the shuffle, so the step is refused (E_UNSUPPORTED); (b) a lock that is not marked runs its `onResidual`, and the `faintMessages` after it is a faint point (`sim/battle.ts:565-566`), as for a counter that keeps: a faint queued into phase 6 (a Perish count's end, which `continue`s with no faint point) is processed there, and a battle that ends there leaves the slower holders' handlers unrun (a slower marked holder: no fatigue, its lock and its bit stand at TERMINAL); a holder with both a lock and a counter that keeps is refused at its own battle-ending faint point (which of its two faint points came first). The faint point of (b) was missing on main too (latent there: only the counters of slower Pokemon could differ).
- **Unchanged:** the fatigue at a use that does not restart the lock (onAfterMove, `flow/turn.h`, and its White Herb refusals), the countdown at a restarted use, the silent delete of a holder put to sleep after its restarted use, the Ally Switch epilogue of 5dn(h), and the refusals of a stopped use by a flinch, a full paralysis, a freeze or a confusion self-hit (lifting them is its own change). Comments: `run_internal.h` (`lock_restarted`), the stopped-use comment, `tests/moves/test_pool_g56.c` (its sleeping holder, count 2, is now ended with `onEnd`, no confusion; checks unchanged).
