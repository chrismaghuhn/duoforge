- Area: moves

5m. **G21, a second data-only batch** (27 moves; after G13). The coverage count of the 226 survey teams of the registry (`docs/research/expansion/data/registry_blockers.json`, the coordinator's count on main 0.31.0) lists these as blockers; each was checked against the pin and the Champions mod (`data/mods/champions/moves.ts`), and a move qualifies only if every part of it runs on a column or handler that exists. The rows were modelled by the generator before (special NONE, no `unmodelled` marker in `pool_tables.c`), so **the tables, the generator and the POOL fingerprints do not change**: the step marks the moves in the manifest and records battles.
   - **Marked, with what each uses** (pin lines of `data/moves.ts`): Sludge Bomb 16920-16936 and Gunk Shot 8017-8033 (a 30 percent poison secondary; Steel is immune), Dragon Claw 4087-4099 (plain; the Champions mod adds the `slicing` flag, which no marked ability reads), Night Slash 12739-12752 and Slash 16837-16850 (critical hit ratio 2), Air Slash 250-266, Icicle Crash 9437-9453, Waterfall 20488-20504 and Dark Pulse 3338-3354 (a flinch secondary; Air Slash and Dark Pulse target `any`), Crunch 3206-3224 and Bug Buzz 1935-1953 (a stat drop secondary), Mystical Fire 12509-12527 (Special Attack -1 at 100 percent), Struggle Bug 18233-18251 (spread, the same drop on both foes), Nuzzle 12841-12857 (paralysis at 100 percent), Fire Blast 5321-5337 (burn 10 percent), Power Whip 13893-13905, Overheat 13110-13127, Leaf Storm 10150-10167 and Armor Cannon 524-541 (self drops after the hit), Dragon Dance 4100-4117, Quiver Dance 14532-14550 and Agility 219-235 (boosts of the user), Will-O-Wisp 20887-20901 (a primary burn at 85 percent, which Thermal Exchange and a Fire type refuse), Accelerock 34-46, Bullet Punch 1991-2003 and Mach Punch 10645-10657 (priority +1) and Drain Punch 4236-4249 (drain 1/2). Flags that the engine does not read (`bullet`, `pulse`, `punch`, `bite`, `sound`, `slicing`, `dance`, `distance`, `contact` where no marked reader exists) are ignored by the existing rule: no marked ability or item reads them, so nothing is hidden.
   - **Not marked, and why** (each stays UNMODELED in the table with its feature): Shell Smash (found by the first recorded battle, not by reading: the pin lists its boosts Defense, Special Defense, Attack, Special Attack, Speed (`data/moves.ts` 16255-16275) and the protocol shows the two drops first, while the engine applies a boost vector in stat order; a per-move order is new logic, so the move was taken back out of the step); Earthquake (`allAdjacent` is not an engine target class: the ally hit, the spread and Wide Guard on that class are new code); Ancient Power (a secondary that boosts the user: the generator has no kind for it); Blizzard (`onModifyMove`: accuracy in snow); Dual Wingbeat and Twin Beam (`multihit`); Feint (`breaksProtect`); Life Dew (target `allies`); Acrobatics (`basePowerCallback` on the item now); Terrain Pulse (callbacks); Clanging Scales (`selfBoost`); Expert Belt (an item that scales damage by type effectiveness: no handler); Sleep Powder (modelled, but the Grass type's powder immunity is engine logic that does not exist); Meteor Assault (G17's class, but its only learner, Sirfetch'd, has no supported ability, so no accepted battle can show it); Matcha Gotcha (spread drain with a thaw and a burn: unrecorded, no battle shows a spread drain).
   - **Evidence**: 8 recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g21_*`, genders stated, seeds by predicate): `g21_poison_agility_nuzzle`, `g21_dragon_dance_claw`, `g21_quiver_bug_fire`, `g21_waterfall_pulse_crunch`, `g21_armarouge_wisp_thermal` (Thermal Exchange refuses the burn and takes +1 Attack from the Fire hits, Incineroar is immune to it, a switched-in Milotic is burned), `g21_priority_punches`, `g21_mach_punch_power_whip` and `g21_crit_slashes`; every marked move is used in one of them (`duoforge.reference.converter_api`). The recorded battles are 133 (125 before) with 720 stops (672 before: six per new battle, none of an ended battle, counted by hand from `tiebreak_ref.h`); 75 moves are marked (48 before). The converter gains one alias for the protocol name of Lycanroc-Dusk (`Lycanroc`, `BASE_SPECIES_NAME`), as for the other formes with a base species. Mutation of the pool rows, 27 edits (a poison or flinch or drop chance, an accuracy, a power, a critical ratio, a priority, a target class, a drain, a boost or drop of one stat, a boost role): all 27 turn `conformance_pool_data` red (22 also `tiebreak_pool_data`). A first Power Whip edit of its accuracy by five points stayed green (the recorded hits and misses do not fall between 85 and 90), so the check was made on its power instead. A short differential campaign (teams of the step against each other and teams A and B, 240 battles, seed 2101) gave 239 PASS and one ORACLE_GAP (`thermal-exchange-burn`, fz_2101_3): a defect of the converter, not of the engine. The precondition of step G14 (a Thermal Exchange holder is never burned) was read on the state before the step, which still shows the Pokemon that a holder replaces when it switches in during the step (a burned Grimmsnarl in that case). It is now read on the state after the step, where the holder stands (a burned holder there is still an error), the unit test `test_thermal_exchange_is_no_holder_of_an_update_tie` has both cases, `diff_random.step_report` passes the state after the step as the converter does, and the battle is kept in the corpus (`fz_2101_3_prefix`, 13 of 18 steps). The same campaign then gave 240 of 240 PASS.
