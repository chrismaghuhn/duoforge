5af. **G29, the item-transfer moves Trick, Switcheroo, Thief and Covet** (Trick blocks 8 of the 227 survey teams). Four handler ids (`TRICK` 56, `SWITCHEROO` 57, `THIEF` 58, `COVET` 59; UNMODELED moves to 60), so the pool tables, their hash (850cc218...) and the POOL fingerprints (KP 68962f87..., KPD 01d22794...) change once; the four rows are marked; view bit 11 stays exact (the item that a move gave is `item_now` too).
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod changes none of the four): `data/moves.ts:19865-19911` Trick, `:18644-18690` Switcheroo, `:19302-19330` Thief, `:3099-3123` Covet; `sim/pokemon.ts:1851-1889` takeItem and setItem; `sim/battle.ts:571-650` singleEvent (the handler's arguments are the relayed item, the event's target, its source); `data/abilities.ts:4622-4635` Sticky Hold, `:5240-5242` Unburden; `data/items.ts:27` the Mega Stones' onTakeItem. The generator checks the whole text of each callback.
   - **Trick and Switcheroo.** `onTryImmunity` (the hit step before accuracy) fails a target with Sticky Hold: `-immune|target`, no reason. `onHit` takes the target's item (the user is the taker), then the user's own; a refusal of either (a Mega Stone held by its own species), two empty hands, or the second TakeItem check of an item with the other Pokemon as its receiver (a Mega Stone is refused for a receiver of the species it belongs to; the handler's second argument is the receiver) restores both items and fails: `-fail|user` with `[still]`. Then `-activate|user|move: Trick|[of] target` (Switcheroo prints Trick too), and for the target, then for the user, `setItem` and `-item|X|Item|[from] move: M`, or, when the other one had nothing, the silent `-enditem|X|Item|[silent]|[from] move: M` of the item that left. Unburden's volatile is set whenever the TakeItem handlers ran for an Unburden holder that holds an item, also when the move then fails.
   - **Thief and Covet.** `onAfterHit` (at Knock Off's point, also for a user that has fainted since: the Champions mod does not ask): nothing if the user holds an item; the target's `takeItem(user)` (a live Sticky Hold target shows `-activate|X|ability: Sticky Hold` and keeps the item; a Mega Stone is not taken from its own species); the second TakeItem check with the user as receiver; `setItem(user)`, which refuses a user that has fainted (the item goes back, the target's TakeItem handlers have run). Thief shows the silent `-enditem|target|Item|[silent]|[from] move: Thief|[of] user` and `-item|user|Item|[from] move: Thief|[of] target`; Covet only the second line, with its own move.
   - **State and rules.** The held item is the tail's `item_now` (G16): an item id + 1 for an item that a move gave (the consumed flag of the receiver is cleared), 255 for none. A Choice lock ends with the item (a Choice item's onStart removes it; a Scarf that arrives locks at the Pokemon's next move as always). Under the POOL kinds a member without a sheet item may have used an item that a move gave it (the tail's `item_now` names it), and the Unburden volatile may stand with an item held (a swap that gave an item back, or one that failed after the TakeItem handlers ran): `tests/items/test_item_transfer.c` (`check_kind_rules`) runs one state under TEAM_C_DEV, where both stay refused, and under POOL_DEV. **A transfer that would make the receiver use the item on the spot** (setItem's onStart: a White Herb with a lowered stat, a terrain seed under its terrain) **is `E_UNSUPPORTED`**, decided before anything changes; every other onStart of the marked items does nothing here.
   - **Events (owner's OK pending for the new value).** `DUOFORGE_EVENT_ITEM_START` = 42: position the receiver, `id2` the item + 1, cause MOVE with `id` the move, `other` the Pokemon it came from when the line says `[of]`. The silent `-enditem` of Trick, Switcheroo and Thief is ITEM_END with the cause ITEM_TAKEN (no other silent line is an event); `-activate|X|move: Trick` is the ACTIVATE that every move's `-activate` already is. The converter reads exactly these shapes and refuses any other `-item` or `[from] move:` `-enditem` line. The old `item_used` of the player view is defined on its own commit (owner's OK pending): the sheet item is gone and nothing is held.
   - **Evidence.** Four recorded battles under the POOL kind: `g29_trick_scarf` (Leftovers and a Choice Scarf change hands, the Scarf locks its new holder), `g29_empty_hands` (Switcheroo and Trick to and from empty hands, Unburden), `g29_thief_covet` (Covet takes a Sitrus Berry; Thief does nothing while the user holds an item; Sticky Hold blocks it twice; a Mega Stone is not taken; a Rocky Helmet is taken after it hurts the thief), `g29_trick_fails` (a stone refused for its receiver's species, a Mega Stone refused for its holder, Sticky Hold and Good as Gold `-immune`, two empty hands). `duoforge.items.item_transfer` reads the tail and both viewers' extension after every step against rows that `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py` derives from the protocol lines.
