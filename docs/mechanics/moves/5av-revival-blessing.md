5av. **G52, Revival Blessing** (decision 0025 item 6 and the revive part of items 8 to 10; `data/moves.ts:15110-15136`, `sim/side.ts` 925-985 and 1119-1132, `sim/battle.ts` 2781-2797, 2862-2891). One handler id, `REVIVAL_BLESSING` (72, after Double Shock's 71; UNMODELED moves to 73); the move is marked. `onTryHit` fails with a plain `-fail` and `[still]` (the no-target line) when the user's side has no fainted brought member. Otherwise the user's slot is flagged (switch flag 7, `DFI_SWITCH_REVIVE_BLESSING`) and a PIVOT is made with no check of a living reserve: the revive names a brought fainted member (a fainted active ally too: its instaswitch). A pass is offered when the flagged slots exceed the living reserves, and a revive takes one of min(flagged, living reserves) forced switches while it is accepted regardless (`chooseSwitch`'s Revival Blessing branch). The revive is `sethp(maxhp / 2)`, status cleared, the REVIVE event (public value 43, PERCENT for the foe, EXACT for the owner). The revive's pivot is answered in the step after the move line; the converter keeps each side's Revival Blessing user across steps. **Not in this step**: decision 0023's revived foe HP (the world builder's uniform draw over the public percent is not the revived value; open: the refusal the coordinator proposed (a public record with a foe REVIVE is E_UNSUPPORTED) needs the revive to be a public fact the record can hold; the record keeps no revive history and the knowledge of a revived member looks like any heal, so it needs a public marker, a layout change, which is not in this PR), and the Regenerator instaswitch, which is refused with E_UNSUPPORTED (no recorded battle reaches it). ## Support

**Revival Blessing (step G52, decision 0015 item 5av)**: the move fails with a plain `-fail` when the side has no fainted brought member; otherwise the user's slot is flagged and, at a pivot, the side revives a brought fainted member at half its maximum HP (the fainted active ally too: an instaswitch). A slot with no living reserve may pass. Five recorded POOL battles (`g52_*`, `duoforge.interactions.revival_blessing_view`, `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data`). Not supported: a revived Regenerator holder's instaswitch (refused with E_UNSUPPORTED), the revived foe's HP in the determinization (decision 0023, open), and the instaswitch and the earlier-fainted revive, which have no recorded battle yet.
