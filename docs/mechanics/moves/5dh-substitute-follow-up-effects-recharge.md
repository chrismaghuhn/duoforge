5dh. **Substitute follow-up effects: recharge, Ice Fang's rolls, Volt Switch's pivot (issues 517, 518, 519; branch claude/517-519-substitute-effects; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** The common cause: when a Substitute takes a hit, the pin's target is null, not false (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:349-352`: HIT_SUBSTITUTE becomes `damage[i] = true`, `targets[i] = null`), and every later step that skips only false targets runs for it: runMoveEffects (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1186-1313`, its selfSwitch block at `:1290-1296` and the flag at `:1311-1312`), selfDrops (`:1317-1335`) and secondaries (`:1336-1350`, one random(100) per secondary; moveHit of a null target does nothing). The engine's hit loop marks such a target `hit[i] = false, sub_off[i] = true`, and each handler must count `sub_off` itself; three did not. **Fixes** (`src/combat/turn.c`, one commit each): (517) the recharge of Hyper Beam and Giga Impact counts `sub_off`, so the user gets mustrecharge and the next request is the recharge; (518) Ice Fang draws both SECONDARY rolls for a `sub_off` target and applies neither effect; (519) a self-switch move (U-turn, Volt Switch, Flip Turn) sets the switch flag after a `sub_off` hit. **Evidence.** Three recordings from the fuzzing campaign, cut after the step that shows the effect (`tests/reference/specs/fz517_hyper_beam_sub.json`, `fz518_ice_fang_sub.json`, `fz519_volt_switch_sub.json`; seeds and choices of the campaign, traces from the pinned Showdown b2cb775): on main each one diverges (517 at step 1: offers 0xf, reference 0x20, the `-mustrecharge` event missing; 518 at step 2 and 519 at step 8: E_INVARIANT on the tape), with the fix each passes. Mutants, each the revert of one fix: each is killed by its own recording and by none of the other two. Every other reference battle is unchanged: the pool set grows by these three and the three of the extension (996 battles; the format pin's pool line is updated for them), the conformance and tiebreak headers only by their counts. **Extension of 518 (the final fuzzing report: Outrage, Tri Attack and Ceaseless Edge as well).** The same cause in four more places of `src/combat/turn.c`: the lockedmove lock of Outrage counts `sub_off` (selfDrops, so the lock starts and its count, LOCK_TURNS, is drawn); Tri Attack draws its SECONDARY roll for a `sub_off` target and runs no pick there (its onHit is the secondary's, and moveHit of a null target does nothing); the empty secondary of Stone Axe and Ceaseless Edge draws its roll for a `sub_off` target; and their onAfterSubDamage (`data/moves.ts` stoneaxe and ceaselessedge, called by the Substitute at `:18368` after the recoil and the drain and before the holder's AfterSubDamage) lays the hazard on the user's foe side when the user stands and has no Sheer Force (`dfi_substitute_takes`, before the Air Balloon pop). Recordings: `fz518_outrage_sub` (fz_116_36 of the final run, cut after step 1: Tauros breaks Lycanroc's Substitute with Outrage, the pin draws LOCK_TURNS), `sub518_tri_attack` (seed 0x5180: Hydreigon's Tri Attack into Milotic's Substitute, the roll is 9, below 20, and the pin draws no pick) and `sub518_ceaseless_edge` (seed 0x5192: Samurott-Hisui's Ceaseless Edge into Milotic's Substitute, `-sidestart|p2|Spikes` after the `-activate`, then the roll). Mutants, each one change reverted, each killed by its own recording in `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data`: the lock, the Tri Attack roll, the pick drawn for the null target, the Stone Axe roll, the hazard. The pivot cases of the final report (U-turn fz_144_13, Flip Turn fz_179_72, Volt Switch fz_338_75) pass with the 519 fix; the first two are recorded as well, cut after step 3 (`fz519_uturn_sub`: Golisopod's U-turn breaks Chesnaught's Substitute at step 1 and the switch request follows at step 2; `fz519_flip_turn_sub`: Sharpedo's Flip Turn breaks Tauros's Substitute), and on main the first diverges at step 1 (turn 2, reference 1) and the second stops at step 1 with E_INVARIANT. **Not changed here (same cause, inferred from the pin, no recording yet):** Glaive Rush's self volatile, Double Shock's self onHit, and the clear of the terrain by Steel Roller (onHit) and Ice Spinner (onAfterSubDamage); each handler tests `hit[i]` only.
