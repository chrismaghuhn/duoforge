# 5aw. G56, Part 2: Outrage as a lockedmove (marked; Thrash and Petal Dance are not marked).

- Area: moves
- Step: G56
- Tests: duoforge.moves.outrage_lock

5aw. **G56, Part 2: Outrage as a lockedmove (marked; Thrash and Petal Dance are not marked).** The pin (`data/conditions.ts:253-285`): `lockedmove` has duration 2; its `onStart` draws `trueDuration = random(2, 4)` (2 or 3); its `onResidual` counts `trueDuration` down every residual and deletes the volatile silently when the holder is asleep; its `onRestart` keeps duration 2 only while `trueDuration >= 2` (a successful hit by the self effect); its `onAfterMove` removes the volatile at duration 1, after the use whatever its result; its `onEnd` adds confusion only when `trueDuration <= 1`. Engine representation (tail rev 4, `lock_turns` = `trueDuration`, 0 when there is no lock; the slot's `locked_move` = the move slot + 1, the body's half of the pairing of section 7): the count draw is `DFI_SITE_LOCK_TURNS` (2 to 4, site 21, the converter's `LOCK_TURNS`), and the count goes down at the use, not in the residual: a restarting use decrements it in its AfterMove, so a replacement's pause (an Eject Button, a pivot) cannot strand the restart in a call that does not have it. A use that does not restart the lock ends it there (`dfi_lock_end`: confusion at `trueDuration <= 1`). The request offers the locked move alone, with no target; the lock is cleared silently by a switch-out, a faint and a sleep at the residual; the locked move uses no PP. Refused with `E_UNSUPPORTED`, decided before anything changes: a locked user whose move is stopped before its use (a flinch, a freeze, a full paralysis; a sleep is not refused, its lock is deleted at the residual), and a White Herb holder with a stat down when a lock ends at this use (the herb's AnyAfterMove and the confusion would both print; their order is not modelled). Thrash and Petal Dance share the handler (`LOCKED_MOVE`) but stay unmarked: no recorded battle yet. Evidence: seven recorded battles under POOL (`g56_*`): `g56_outrage_protect` (Protect blocks the first use: no lock), `g56_outrage_full` (a full lock of three uses that ends in confusion), `g56_outrage_cut` (a lock cut by a Protect with no confusion, its count above one), `g56_outrage_disrupt` (cut by a Protect, then confusion), `g56_outrage_confusion` (a Protect-blocked locked use ends in confusion, the battle has an Eject Button replacement during the lock), `g56_outrage_switch` (a lock dragged out by Roar, cleared with no confusion) and `g56_outrage_faint` (a faint on the turn the lock would start, from Rocky Helmet). Gap, stated: no battle faints a locked user during an active lock (the foe's Sucker Punch never KOs the lead in the roughly 1,000 trials of the searches); the refusal paths above are unit-tested (`duoforge.moves.outrage_lock`), not replayed. Random campaign (seed 56, 300 battles over four pairings of the two prepared teams, Showdown as the judge): PASS 297, UNSUPPORTED 3 (explicit engine refusals), ORACLE_GAP 0, no DIVERGENCE. The ties of a lockedmove with the silent ends of stall and Protect (59 in the campaign, PASS) are dropped by the converter's named rule `lock_counter_tie` (`tools/reference/trace_to_c.py`): the reference shuffles them, but neither order shows in a line, a draw or a state that the other reads, and the engine's list keeps the lock at the pin's group (no order, sub-order 2, as every condition). The three UNSUPPORTED battles are one shape: a confused lock user whose confusion self-hit stops its move, so the lock reaches the residual unused (the pin ends it there, `fieldEvent` at duration 1); the engine cannot tell a used lock from an unused one at the residual (the count goes down at the use), so it refuses that step (E_UNSUPPORTED, `pool_g56` covers it with a seeded roll). Mutation check: ten anchored mutants of the engine, with the G56 tests as the oracle; all ten are caught, the last (a sleeping holder keeps its lock into the residual) by the case of `duoforge.moves.outrage_lock` added for it.
