5dp. **The Outrage lock of issue 544: 30 cases, four causes (branch claude/project-thread-fxsdy2; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** One commit each. (a) **No Pressure on a lockedmove turn** (fz_178_90, 23 cases): the locked turn's source is the lockedmove condition, not a move with PP, so useMoveInner charges no Pressure extra (`sim/battle-actions.ts:289`, `:473-484`); the engine skipped it only on a two-turn move's locked turn. (b) **A Choice lock under a lockedmove** (fz_117_58, 3 cases): the reference keeps the Choice lock's slot as the locked slot while Outrage runs; the observation and the conformance comparison of the state hid it. Decision 0083 (merged to main first) made the same change for both viewers; this entry adds only the recording. (c) **The owner's Pressure timeline and drags** (fz_356_30): a member dragged in (Red Card, Roar, Dragon Tail) replaces the Pressure holder as a switch does (`dfi_own_event`, `src/combat/events.c`), so the foe's next move is not charged the holder's extra in the owner's view; this closes the gap that 5dn left open. (d) **The converter's Pressure tracker and re-entering Megas** (fz_308_16, 2 cases): a member that Mega Evolved comes back as the Mega, whose ability is not Pressure (Absol-Mega-Z: Sharpness); `FoePressure` took the sheet's Pressure on every switch-in. The engine was right there. Main fixed the tracker in the same way first; this entry adds only the recording. The issue's summary (the lock ends when the target faints, PP at the lock's end) was the fuzzing's guess; the cases show these four causes. **Evidence.** Four recordings (`tests/reference/specs/fz544_*.json`, whole battles; seeds and choices of the campaign, traces from the pinned Showdown b2cb775). Of the 30 cases 29 pass the runner (fz_480_26 already passed before these fixes); one (fz_117_80) stops with E_UNSUPPORTED at a refusal already documented (a locked move that a confusion self-hit stops, 5dn). Mutants, each fix reverted alone, over the four recordings: each is killed by its own recording and by no other (checked before (b) and (d) landed on main). Every other reference battle is unchanged (`trace_to_c.py --check`, `tiebreak_ref.js`); the pool set grows by these four.
