5ar. **G50, Double Shock** (decision 0025 items 1, 2 and 5; `data/moves.ts:3945-3969`, the Champions punch flag at `data/mods/champions/moves.ts:256-259`). Marked for Pawmot (Electric and Fighting, Iron Fist) only, the one usable Pawmot; every other shape is refused with `E_UNSUPPORTED` (tests in `tests/moves/test_double_shock.c`). The lead approved the public values: `DUOFORGE_TYPE_NONE` = 255 (the typeless first slot, `???`) and the `TYPE_CHANGE` amount = the second type id + 1 (0 = one type, which Soak keeps). Internal: `soak_type` may hold `DFI_TAIL_TYPE2_TYPELESS` (255) only with a `type2` of 1 to 18 (a type id + 1; `type2` 0 or 255 is refused; invariant TAIL_MEMBER); `dfi_types_of` reads the typeless slot as `DFI_CLOSURE_NONE`, which every reader skips; the view maps it to `type_now` = {0, type2 + 1}.
   - **The pin**: Double Shock fails without the Electric type (`-fail` and `[still]`); after a hit the user's types are `???` and the second type (`-start|X|typechange|???/Fighting|[from] move: Double Shock`, the event TYPE_CHANGE with detail 255 and amount 6). A switch-out resets both, Soak (`setType`, `sim/pokemon.ts:2109`) replaces both with Water and clears the second type, and a Ground move hits the typeless Pawmot neutrally (Fighting, not Electric).
   - **No target**: Showdown runs TryMove before the no-targets test (`sim/battle-actions.ts:486-513`) and its target is not null when the foe in slot 0 has fainted, so a Double Shock with nothing to hit fails with `-fail` and `[still]` when the user lacks Electric. The campaign below found it: every one of its six divergences was this case, and `dfi_no_target_or_try` now runs the gate first (pinned by `g50_double_shock_no_target`). **Known pin-order question (not changed in this item, a later check):** Showdown runs TryMove before the no-targets test (`sim/battle-actions.ts:486-513`), but the engine returns the no-target result first for every move with nothing to hit; only Double Shock's gate was moved ahead of it. Other moves whose TryMove gate can fail (Fake Out's first-turn rule, Sucker Punch's target move, and any other onTry fail) would print the no-target line where the pin prints the gate's own `-fail`. No recorded battle of this item covers those moves, and this item does not change them.
    - **Tables**: after G44's four handlers (ids 67 to 70, merged from main, after G48's 63 to 66), DOUBLE_SHOCK = 71 and UNMODELED = 72; the tables are regenerated by `gen_closure.py --pool`.
   - **Evidence**: six recorded POOL battles (`g50_double_shock_fail`, `_switch`, `_soak`, `_ground`, `_soak_after`: Double Shock, then Soak on the typeless Pawmot, and `_no_target`), genders stated; with G48's eleven, 375 pool battles in the conformance and tiebreak data (2185 stops); `duoforge.moves.double_shock` checks the soak type and second type after every step of the first five, the two refused shapes, the view and a byte-equal codec round trip with soak type 255.
   - **Mutation checks**: eleven one-change mutants of `src/combat/turn.c`, `src/state/invariants.c` and `src/state/observation.c` (`g50_mutate.py`): the typeless STAB slot, the Electric gate, the self effect, the amount and the detail of TYPE_CHANGE, the view's two slots, the invariant, the special whitelist, Soak's clearing of the second type, and the no-target gate. Eleven of eleven are caught, by `duoforge.moves.double_shock`, `duoforge.state.pool_tail`, `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` or both. M01 to M10 were run before the no-target fix and are not run again after it (the fix does not change their edited lines); M02 and M11 were run after it.
   - **Campaign**: `diff_driver.py random`, seed 5050, 300 battles, pairings XY, YX, XX, YY, Pawmot teams (X: Pawmot with Iron Fist against Y: Garchomp, Samurott-Hisui, Milotic, Gholdengo, Primarina, Sneasler). The first run gave 294 PASS and 6 DIVERGENCE (all the no-target Double Shock above); after the fix the same seed gives 300 PASS, with 1639 of 1639 sampled requests offered by Showdown.
