5ax. **G58, Phantom Force** (a two-turn move, semi-invulnerable on its charge turn, breaks Protect on a hit; no new public value, no new event kind, no new state field or tail byte, no view change, no version bump). Pin (`data/moves.ts:13307-13335`): Ghost, Physical, 90, accuracy 100, flags contact, charge, mirror, metronome, nosleeptalk, noassist, failinstruct, no protect flag, `breaksProtect: true`; `onTryMove` removes its own volatile on the locked turn (`return`), else `-prepare`, the ChargeMove event (Power Herb's, unmarked), `addVolatile('twoturnmove')` and `return null`; the condition `phantomforce` has duration 2 and `onInvulnerability: false` (no exception for any move). `twoturnmove` (`data/conditions.ts:287-323`) adds the move's volatile, `[still]`, PrepareHit, and ends with its move's volatile. The Invulnerability step is the first hit step (`sim/battle-actions.ts:556, 621-643`), the breaksProtect step is after the accuracy check (`sim/battle-actions.ts:755-777`, removes the target's protect volatiles and the side's Wide Guard, Quick Guard, Crafty Shield and Mat Block, `-activate ... move: X [broken]`, the stall counter deleted). Handler `PHANTOM_FORCE` (82; UNMODELED moves to 83), the table row marked in the support manifest. **The charge** is Electro Shot's path (`charge_turns`, the locked move and target, `[still]`, `-prepare`), with no weather to skip. **The semi-invulnerability is derived, not stored**: a position is semi-invulnerable when it is charging Phantom Force and either the charge turn is running (`charge_turns` is 2) or the locked turn has not reached its own action yet (`charge_turns` is 1 and the user's MOVE record is still in the queue, which the state codec already encodes); a fainted user has no charge. **The Invulnerability step** (`dfi_invulnerable_to`, first in the hit loop, before Wide Guard, Quick Guard and Protect): a semi-invulnerable target is a `-miss` (the `[miss]` on a single target's line, no accuracy draw), unless No Guard is on the user or on the target (`data/abilities.ts:2970-2974`, `onAnyInvulnerability` returns 0, which is not false) or the move is a Toxic of a Poison-type user (`sim/battle-actions.ts:627-628`). Coaching (an ally-targeted status move) takes the same step before Good as Gold. Spread moves miss only the charging target (`-miss`, no `[miss]` on the line). **breaksProtect** reuses Feint's `dfi_break_protect` (after the accuracy check, for targets still hit); it now also resets the target's `protect_kind` with the Protect volatile (the invariant of tail rev 3, which a Spiky Shield break would otherwise leave behind; Feint shares the fix). **This is a fix to an existing row, Feint (G28, `data/moves.ts:5173-5187`)**: a Spiky Shield that Feint breaks used to leave `protect_kind` set with the volatile down, which the tail invariant rejects; Feint now resets it too. The Feint battle that shows it is planned as `g58_feint_spiky_break` (recorded after the campaign). The converter reads `-activate ... [broken]` as Feint's line (the same event). **Not in this step**: Power Herb (no row in the pool tables; its ChargeMove skip is not modelled), Lock-On (`onSourceInvulnerability`, UNMODELED), Metronome, Mirror Move, Sleep Talk, Instruct and Assist (their moves are unmodelled), Dig, Fly, Dive, Bounce and Shadow Force (not in the pool's marks). **Evidence**: eleven recorded POOL battles (`g58_*`, `tests/reference/specs` and `traces`, genders stated; Golurk is genderless): `g58_phantom_charge_hit` (Shadow Ball misses the charging user on the charge turn, a locked hit on the next turn), `g58_phantom_double_charge` (two charging users: the faster one's move misses the still-charging foe, which then hits the one that already acted), `g58_phantom_noguard_target` (Iron Head hits the charging No Guard Golurk), `g58_phantom_noguard_user` (Golurk's Earthquake hits the charging Ceruledge), `g58_phantom_toxic_poison_user` (Gengar's Toxic lands on the charging Annihilape), `g58_phantom_protect_break` (Quick Guard on the target's side is broken, `[broken]`), `g58_phantom_ally_protect` (the Protect of the user's own partner is broken), `g58_phantom_coaching_miss` (Machamp's Coaching misses the charging partner), `g58_phantom_spread_earthquake` (the spread move misses the charging ally and hits both foes), `g58_phantom_choice_scarf` (the item's lock carries the charge into the locked turn), `g58_phantom_flinch_charge` (Iron Head flinches the No Guard charger and cancels its charge). Each team has six members and unique items per side. Tests: `duoforge.moves.phantom_force` (the row, the handler, the flags and the support mark), `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` (496 battles, 7022 checks, 0 failures), `duoforge.reference.tiebreak_pool` (833 stops, 0 failures), the converter tests (89, OK). **Mutation checks**: 14 mutants of `turn.c` (one edit each, `g58_mutate`: the invulnerability switched off, the charge turn or the queue check changed, No Guard on the user or the target, the Toxic exception, breaksProtect, the charge line, the `-prepare`, the `[miss]`, Coaching's step, the charge itself, the result of the miss, the `protect_kind` reset), each run against `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` and `duoforge.moves.phantom_force`: 12 of 14 caught.
