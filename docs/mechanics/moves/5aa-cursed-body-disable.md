# 5aa. G27, Cursed Body and Disable

- Area: moves
- Step: G27
- Tests: duoforge.interactions.cursed_body_disable

5aa. **G27, Cursed Body and Disable** (the first users of the tail's `disable_slot` and `disable_turns`, rev 2 and 3 as they are; decision 0018 tier 3, Disable 0.29 percent of the decisions). Disable keeps a handler id of its own (`DISABLE`: its `onTryHit` and a condition whose state the tail holds), the turn code implements it, and Cursed Body is an engine row of the generator (it runs by id from the damage code). Both are marked, with the view bit 21.
   - **The pin.** Disable (`data/moves.ts:3648-3716`), Normal, Status, PP 20, accuracy 100, flags protect, reflectable, mirror, bypasssub; the Champions mod changes only the condition's `onBeforeMove` (`data/mods/champions/moves.ts:228-238`: a move with the `cantusetwice` flag is not stopped; no marked move has it). `onTryHit` (:3658-3662) fails the move (`-fail` and `[still]`) for a target without a last move or whose last move is Struggle; it is the move's own handler, run in `spreadMoveHit`, so after the accuracy (the draw comes first: recorded in `g27_disable_b`). The condition (:3663-3711): `duration: 5`; `onStart` takes one turn off (4) when the target has not taken its turn (`queue.willMove`) or when the effect is a move's own effect in progress (Cursed Body: the attacker is the active Pokemon), refuses (the move fails) a target without a last move or whose last move has no PP left, prints `-start|X|Disable|MOVE` (with `[from] ability: Cursed Body` and `[of] holder` for the ability) and stores the move id; `addVolatile` refuses a second one (no `onRestart`) and a fainted Pokemon; `onResidualOrder: 17` with `onEnd` printing `-end|X|Disable` (the duration handler is in the sorted Residual list between Encore's order 16 and Heal Block's 20); `onBeforeMovePriority: 7` (after the flinch's 8, before Throat Chop's and Heal Block's 6) stops the barred move with `cant|X|Disable|MOVE` and no PP used; `onDisableMove` bars the one slot of that move. Cursed Body (`data/abilities.ts:784-797`): `onDamagingHit`, no order (after Rough Skin's 1 and Rocky Helmet's 2), nothing when the attacker is already disabled (checked before the roll, so no draw), nothing for Struggle, a max move or a future move; otherwise `randomChance(3, 10)` (one draw per hit holder) and on a success `source.addVolatile('disable', holder)` for the attacker's last move, which is the move that just hit.
   - **State.** The tail's per-position `disable_slot` (move slot + 1, 1 to 4) and `disable_turns` (1 to 5), zero together, cleared with the occupant (a switch-out or a faint ends it with no line): no layout change. The request leaves the barred slot out; with no move left (the barred move was the only usable one: a Choice lock or an Encore on it) the slot gets Struggle, as for any disabled move. Encore keeps its own rules (it can start on a barred move; the request then offers Struggle).
   - **Rules of the turn.** The Disable move: Protect first, then the accuracy, then in the status loop its onTryHit failure (no last move, or Struggle) and `dfi_disable_start` (a start that refuses fails the move with `-fail`). Cursed Body: `dfi_cursed_body` in the DamagingHit loop of each standing damaged target, after Thermal Exchange's place and before the attacker's Poison Touch; a holder that the hit knocked out still makes its attacker roll (its onDamagingHit runs before the faint is processed: `-start|...|Disable|MOVE|[from] ability: Cursed Body` shows before the faint line, recorded in `g27_cursed_body_a`), and a fainted attacker still rolls (the roll comes first). Two holders hit by one spread move roll in the order of the move's target list, with no tie draw: DamagingHit is one of the events that `runEvent` sorts with `compareLeftToRightOrder` (`sim/battle.ts:789-790`: order, then priority, then the target's index; `:421-426`), not with `speedSort`, and the damaged targets enter as one array (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1109-1121`); Cursed Body has no order, so the second holder follows the first, and it finds the attacker already disabled when the first roll succeeded (no roll, no draw). Recorded with two holders of different Speed (the slower in slot a) and with two of equal Speed (`g27_cursed_body_spread_*`). The residual: Disable (order 17) and Heal Block (order 20) are entries of the one sorted handler list of #154 (no separate pass), at their own orders, and callbacks exactly when they end now; the engine's sort draws a group of equal handlers when two of its entries are callbacks, so the draw order follows the reference by construction (the side conditions' tie, order 26, comes after) and the tie draw is the reference's own (the converter keeps it as it is; before, it was rewritten to the order of the positions, which a displacing selection sort does not guarantee). The insertion-order refusal (#154) stays: the turn's counters (Protect, stall, flinch, a charge, Helping Hand, Follow Me) are added by this turn's action and so come after a Disable or Heal Block that ends now, which the exact test knows; every other pair of volatiles of one Pokemon is of unknown order.
   - **New public values (the blanket OK of 2026-10-03, names and numbers agreed with the coordinator).** `DUOFORGE_VOLATILE_DISABLE` = 4 (the detail of `VOLATILE_START` and `VOLATILE_END`; START carries the barred move in `id`, and for Cursed Body the cause `ABILITY` with the ability in `id2` and the holder in `other`) and `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_DISABLE` = 19 (`CANT`, the barred move in `id`), in their own commit; and the internal draw site `CURSED_BODY` = 17 (random(10) < 3, named by `ps_trace.js` from the effect and the event).
   - **View.** Decision 0018: `disable_slot` of the position, public (`-start|X|Disable|MOVE`: the slot of MOVE on the open sheet), supported bit 21, both viewers; the turns are never shown.
   - **Tables.** The special id DISABLE (47, the last of the handlers, after G34's 46 that main had when this step was merged) is new, so UNMODELED moves to 48 (the generator checks the move's `onTryHit` and both condition texts, the pinned and the Champions one), and Cursed Body becomes an engine row (the handler column of its row is NONE); `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` runs the pinned callbacks of both (the failures, the durations, the lines, the order, the bar and the roll).
   - **Evidence.** Eleven recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g27_*`; three of them `g27_cursed_body_spread_a`, `_b` and `_tie`: two Cursed Body holders on one side hit by one Heat Wave, both rolling in slot order, the first success leaving the second without a roll, and equal Speed with no `SPEED_TIE` draw): `g27_cursed_body_a` (Cursed Body rolls, a start with `[from] ability: Cursed Body`, a holder that the hit knocks out still disabling its attacker), `g27_disable_b` (the move on a target that has moved, five turns, and on one that has not, four; the failure with no last move and after the accuracy draw; a second Disable fails; the `cant` line of a queued barred move), `g27_disable_lock` (Disable with Encore and a Choice lock: the request offers Struggle), `g27_disable_switch` (an already disabled attacker hits a holder: no roll; a switch-out clears the bar without a line), `g27_disable_pair_a` and `_b` (two Disables ending in one residual at equal Speed, both orders of the shuffle), `g27_disable_pp` (Disable after the last PP of the target's move, and on a target whose last move is Struggle: `-fail`) and `g27_disable_heal_block` (Disable and Heal Block ending in one residual: Disable first). 247 pool battles in all after the merge with main (167 before it), 94 view rows checked for both viewers (`duoforge.interactions.cursed_body_disable`: the tail and the view field after every step), 1369 tiebreak stops (916 before it).
   - **Mutation checks.** Twenty-six edits (`g27_mut.py`), each killed by the conformance battles, the tiebreak stops or `duoforge.interactions.cursed_body_disable`, except three: the redundant `last == Struggle` test of Disable's own failure (the start refuses a last move of 5 anyway: an equivalent edit), Disable's order 17 moved to 21 (Heal Block's pass is fixed after Disable's, and no other handler lies between: an equivalent edit), and a fainted attacker that Cursed Body disables (no recorded battle has a Rocky Helmet or Rough Skin kill with a successful roll, so the refusal of a fainted occupant by `dfi_disable_start` is read from the pin, `addVolatile`, not recorded).
   - **Campaigns.** Three differential campaigns under the machine lock, teams of Gengar, Banette and Froslass (Cursed Body, Disable) against a spread-move team and each other (seeds 27001, 27002 and 27003, 800 battles): 800 PASS, 0 DIVERGENCE, 0 UNSUPPORTED. The first version of the step refused a spread hit on two Cursed Body holders (`E_UNSUPPORTED`, 47 of 400 battles, 11.75 percent) because it assumed the holders' order was a Speed order; the pin's order is the target's index (`compareLeftToRightOrder`), the refusal is gone, and the same two seeds of 400 battles now all pass. The 800 passing battles hold 1628 Disable starts (883 by Cursed Body), 730 `-end` lines, 119 `cant` lines of a barred move, 564 failed Disable moves and 3165 Cursed Body draws. After the residual change (the sorted list) two more campaigns (seeds 27101 and 27102, 1000 battles): 1000 PASS, 0 DIVERGENCE, 0 UNSUPPORTED, with 2186 Disable starts (1198 by Cursed Body), 970 ends, 166 `cant` lines, 741 failed Disable moves and 3975 Cursed Body draws. Mutation checks after it: 26 edits, all killed but the two equivalent or unrecorded ones above (Disable's order 17 moved to 21 or 15 is now killed too).
