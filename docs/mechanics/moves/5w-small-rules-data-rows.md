# 5w. G34, small rules and data rows

- Area: moves
- Step: G34
- Tests: duoforge.interactions.small_rules_rows, duoforge.interactions.modify_damage_chain_order

5w. **G34, small rules and data rows** (a batch of E's audit of the rev 4 blockers, `tail-rev4-proposal.md` section 6: rows that need a small rule or none). Fifteen rows: the moves Steel Roller, Clangorous Soul, Brick Break, Fiery Dance, Psycho Cut, Iron Defense and Electroweb, the abilities Compound Eyes, Iron Fist, Sharpness, Solid Rock, Technician, Multiscale and Gale Wings, and the item Wide Lens. No new public value, no new state, no view change.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod overrides none of them but Clangorous Soul, whose override has `accuracy: true`; the generator reads the override, `G34_FACTS`, `G34_ABILITY_FACTS` and `G34_ITEM_FACTS` check the pinned text of every callback it relies on). Moves (`data/moves.ts`): Steel Roller :17893-17913 (`onTry` fails without a terrain, `onHit` clears it), Clangorous Soul, Brick Break :1822-1840 (`onTryHit` breaks the three screens, the text of Psychic Fangs), Fiery Dance, Psycho Cut, Iron Defense, Electroweb (data). Abilities (`data/abilities.ts`): Compound Eyes :666-677 (ModifyAccuracy, priority -1, 5325), Iron Fist :2236-2248 (BasePower 23, 4915, punch flag), Sharpness :4174-4186 (BasePower 19, 6144, slicing flag), Solid Rock :4414-4425 (SourceModifyDamage, x0.75 when typeMod > 0), Technician :4916-4930 (BasePower 30, 6144 for 60 or less), Multiscale :2760-2771 (SourceModifyDamage, x0.5 at full HP), Gale Wings :1588-1596 (+1 priority for a Flying move at full HP). Wide Lens `data/items.ts:7713-7727` (ModifyAccuracy, priority -2, 4505).
   - **Tables.** Three handler ids: `STEEL_ROLLER` 44, `CLANGOROUS_SOUL` 45 and `BRICK_BREAK` 46, after the eight of G32 (UNMODELED moves to 47); the seven abilities and Wide Lens are `ENGINE_ROWS`; the second flags byte gets the internal bits `PUNCH` (32) and `SLICING` (64) (`FLAGS2_BITS`, `DFI_MOVE_FLAG2_PUNCH` and `_SLICING`). **The engine reads only these internal bits**, never the static dex features of decision 0020 (the cmake check `static_columns_unread.cmake` still holds); `tests/interactions/test_small_rules_rows.c, tests/interactions/test_modify_damage_chain_order.c` pins that the two internal bits equal the set of the public static column. Fiery Dance's 50 percent Special Attack boost is the secondary kind `SECONDARY_SELF_BOOST` of G28 (Ancient Power's), as data. The POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step.
   - **Base power, accuracy, priority** (`turn.c`). Technician is judged at the pin's point: the first handler (priority 30) on the move's own power after its callback, with the chain still 4096; then the -ate abilities and Iron Fist (23), Fairy Aura (20), Sharpness (19), the type booster (15), Helping Hand (10), the terrains (6). Compound Eyes and Wide Lens chain into one accuracy modifier before the stage calculation, and a move that never misses is untouched. Gale Wings is in `dfi_move_priority`: +1 for a Flying move of a holder at full HP, judged on the type the move has before ModifyType.
   - **Steel Roller** fails without a terrain (`-fail`, [still]); after the damage of the hit the terrain ends (`-fieldend`, then the TerrainChange event of the seeds), once per move, before the self switch and the secondaries. The engine has Grassy and Psychic Terrain today, so those are what it ends; **G25 (Electric and Misty Terrain, pending the owner) extends it** to those, in `dfi_clear_terrain`.
   - **Clangorous Soul** fails at one third of the HP or less (`hp * 100 <= hp_max * 33`) and with a maximum HP of 1, otherwise raises the five stats and costs the user a third of its maximum HP, rounded down, at least 1 (a plain damage event after the boost), then the status hit end. **Brick Break** shares Psychic Fangs' block: after the accuracy check, so a miss or a Protect keeps the screens.
   - **Solid Rock and Multiscale join the ModifyDamage chain, and the commutation proof is general.** One hit can now have Life Orb or Expert Belt, a resist berry, a screen, Glaive Rush's volatile, and Solid Rock or Multiscale. The handlers have no order and priority 0, so the pin's `runEvent` sorts them by their holders' speed, then subOrder (a condition 2, an ability 7, an item 8; a side condition has no speed and is last), and shuffles only a complete tie; the engine does not compute that order. It instead needs every order to chain to one value: `dfi_mods_commute` (`src/combat/damage_chain.h`, header only, Heap's algorithm over at most six values, no allocation) and the engine returns E_UNSUPPORTED for a hit whose modifiers do not commute, no silent fallback. Of the 71 possible combinations 53 commute and 18 do not (listed in the next bullet); this replaces G19's rule "three or more with Glaive Rush", which let Expert Belt with a berry and Glaive Rush through (a main gap since G28, found while checking Solid Rock). The refusal is conservative: the order is determined by the state. **Follow-up** (not in G34): compute the order exactly (the holder speeds, the subOrder values, a tie only when the result depends on it) and refuse only a tie that changes the result, verified by a differential campaign over Glaive Rush, Expert Belt, Life Orb, berry, screen, Solid Rock and Multiscale teams; the measured rate of the refusal below decides its priority. The converter (`trace_to_c.py`, `modifiers_commute`) holds the same values for its drop rule of a tied ModifyDamage group (one handler of each kind, at most one berry; a group that is not one hit stays refused as before).
   - **Evidence.** Twelve recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g34_*`): `steel_roller`, `brick_break`, `clangorous_soul`, `gale_wings`, `accuracy` (seed `0x3480`: the accuracy draws of Sleep Powder with Compound Eyes and Fire Blast with Wide Lens sit between the plain and the modified threshold), `base_power`, `damage_chain` (Solid Rock with a Rindo Berry against a Life Orb; Multiscale at full HP and not at the second hit), `data_rows`, and four that the first mutation run asked for: `gale_priority` (Will-O-Wisp at full HP gets no priority; Brave Bird at full HP is +1 and Raichu's faster Quick Attack still goes first), `visible_power` (Psycho Cut, Night Slash, Close Combat and Mach Punch on targets that survive, so Sharpness and Iron Fist show in the damage), `wide_lens_miss` (seed `0x3504`: a draw of 97 misses with the lens, whose accuracy is 93) and `steel_roller_seeds` (a Steel Roller into a Protect leaves the terrain; the next one ends it, and two Raichu with a Psychic Seed of equal Speed draw the TerrainChange tie). `duoforge.interactions.small_rules_rows, duoforge.interactions.modify_damage_chain_order` pins the marks, ids and flag bits and compares `dfi_mods_commute` with a brute-force walk of all 71 subsets. **Mutation checks.** 45 edits of `turn.c` and `damage_chain.h`, each built and run against `duoforge.interactions.small_rules_rows, duoforge.interactions.modify_damage_chain_order` and the pool conformance tests: Gale Wings without the full HP condition, for any move, with +2, without the ability, above half HP; Technician at 80, 1.2 and gone; Iron Fist for any move, with 1.5, on the slicing flag; Sharpness for any move, with 1.2, on the punch flag; Solid Rock for every hit, 0.8, only for a double super effective; Multiscale at any HP, never, 0.75; the refusal gone, only from four modifiers, inverted; the commutation test with a tolerance of one and with the first order only; Compound Eyes 1.1 and gone; Wide Lens 1.3 and gone; Steel Roller without its fail, keeping the terrain, clearing on a miss, without `-fieldend`, without TerrainChange, keeping the terrain turns, ending the wrong terrain; Clangorous Soul without the HP condition, costing a quarter, nothing or a half, without the boosts; Brick Break breaking nothing. 42 failed a test. The first run left eleven alive, which showed that the battles did not exercise them: Gale Wings for any move and with +2 (the Raichu that hit Talonflame took its full HP), Sharpness and Iron Fist wherever the target fainted, Wide Lens 1.3 (no draw between 93 and 99), Steel Roller on a miss and its TerrainChange tie (no Protect, no two seed holders), and the engine's refusal itself (only the helper was tested). The four new battles and the refusal test in `modify_damage_chain_order` (g34_damage_chain with an Expert Belt in the place of the Life Orb: E_UNSUPPORTED from the step of the first Wood Hammer at the Rhyperior) kill them. Three edits cannot fail a test and are not reachable: Technician at `< 60` (no Technician holder has a marked move of exactly 60 power; the pin's `<= 60` is a checked text of the generator), the accuracy modifier on a move that never misses (the modified 0 is 0), and Clangorous Soul at exactly one third (it needs a maximum HP divisible by 100, and Kommo-o has 150 to 182). **Campaigns** under the machine lock, with a throwaway local build (not committed) that logs every firing of the new refusal: `diff_driver.py random --seed 3434 --battles 1500 --pairings DD,DE,ED,EE --workers 4 --domain-rate 0.1` over `tests/reference/teams/team_g34.txt` and `team_g34b.txt` (the fifteen rows in play, distinct items per team): 1500 of 1500 PASS, 26540 steps, 4863 domain requests agreed, **the refusal fired 0 times** (0 battles, 0 steps; no UNSUPPORTED of any kind). A registry-mix run of the realistic rate, `--seed 3435 --battles 2000` over 200 random pairs of the registry teams of `data/teams` (649 ids besides A, B and C): 2000 of 2000 PASS, 30609 steps, 5414 domain requests agreed, **the refusal fired 0 times** (0 of 2000 battles, 0 of 30609 steps). The 18 refused combinations need three or more of the modifiers at once (Expert Belt or Life Orb, a matching resist berry, a screen, the Glaive Rush volatile, Solid Rock or Multiscale); no battle of these runs had one that fails to commute, so the conservative refusal cost the learner nothing that was measured, and the exact-order follow-up is not urgent.
