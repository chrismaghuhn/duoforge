5ac. **G26, Perish Song** (1.58 percent of the replay decisions, decision 0018 tier 1; the first user of the tail's `perish` counter, rev 2 as it is: no new field, no new state). Perish Song keeps a handler id of its own (`PERISH_SONG`, 48 after Disable; UNMODELED moves to 49): its `onHitField` and its condition are code, not columns; the generator checks both texts whitespace aside. One new public value, the owner's assignment of 2026-10-03: `DUOFORGE_VOLATILE_PERISH = 5` (id 4 is Disable's), the library version 0.35.0.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod has no entry of the move: `data/mods/champions/moves.ts` is hashed by the generator and has none; its learnsets give it to seven formes, of which Politoed, Primarina and Wigglytuff have a marked ability). `data/moves.ts:13233-13277`: Normal, Status, accuracy true, PP 5, flags `sound distance bypasssub metronome` (no `protect`), target `all`. `onHitField` (:13242-13260) goes over `getAllActive()` (`sim/battle.ts:1365`: every active Pokemon that has not fainted, side and slot order) and for each: a `false` Invulnerability event misses it (`-miss`, counts as a result; no marked move makes a Pokemon semi-invulnerable), a TryHit that returns null stops it silently (a result too), otherwise, if it has no `perishsong` volatile, `addVolatile` (duration 4) and `-start|X|perish3|[silent]`; a Pokemon that has it is left alone. If nothing was a result the move returns false: `-fail|user` with `[still]` (`moveHit`, `sim/battle-actions.ts:1302-1308`, reached through `tryMoveHit` for a target `all`, :505-507, :838-840, :1039-1042, :1267-1272); otherwise, if at least one Pokemon got the volatile, `-fieldactivate|move: Perish Song`. The TryHit handlers of the battle: Good as Gold (`data/abilities.ts:1630-1641`, a status move of another Pokemon: `-immune|X|[from] ability: Good as Gold`, the user's own is not stopped), Flash Fire and Lightning Rod (read the type, Normal here), Armor Tail and Psychic Terrain (read a priority above 0: refused below); Protect and Wide Guard let a move without the protect flag through (`checkMoveBypassesProtect`, `sim/battle.ts:1300-1309`; `data/moves.ts:13982-14000`), Substitute is bypassed; Soundproof (`:4436-4452`, a sound move of another Pokemon: `-immune|holder|[from] ability: Soundproof`, the volatile is not given, and it counts as a result) was unmarked when this step was written and is marked by step G32, so the cast handles it (`g26_perish_soundproof`). Perish Body (`:3227-3239`, adds the same volatile on contact) is unmarked too.
   - **The condition** (`:13261-13272`): `duration: 4`, `onResidualOrder: 24`, `onEnd` shows `-start|X|perish0` and calls `target.faint()` (hp 0, queued: `sim/pokemon.ts:1581-1593`), `onResidual` shows `-start|X|perishN` with N the duration after the decrement. `Battle.fieldEvent` (`sim/battle.ts:484-575`) sorts every handler of the residual once (order, priority, Speed, sub-order; a tie is a shuffle, so a draw), and for each: a handler with a duration counts down, and at 0 its `onEnd` runs and the loop `continue`s with **no faintMessages**; any other handler (a callback, or a duration that has not run out) is followed by `faintMessages()`, which processes every faint queued so far and checks the win. So the cast turn's residual shows perish3, the next perish2, perish1, and the fourth shows perish0 for each holder in Speed order, then the faints: after the first later handler that does not end (a non-final count of a slower holder, a side condition or terrain that does not run out, White Herb, the stall counter or a charge that does not end), else after the `upkeep` line (`sim/battle.ts:2811-2818`, :2835); a battle that ends there has no `upkeep`. `faintMessages` (:2535-2604) and `checkWin` (:2606-2617): when every side is out the winner is `faintData.target.side`, the side of the last Pokemon processed, which here is the slowest holder. Switching out or fainting clears the volatile with no line (`clearVolatile`).
   - **State and rules in the engine.** The tail's `perish` is the duration: 4 from the cast to the residual of that turn, then 3, 2, 1, 0 (the invariants' bound 4 was already there). `dfi_run_perish_song` (`src/combat/turn.c`) is the cast above; the residual list gets a Perish entry (order 24, sub-order 2, a callback: its tie draws like Encore's), the first loop runs the sorted prefix (the callbacks, with Heal Block's and Throat Chop's duration entries between Encore's 16 and Perish's 24 skipped: they run in their block), and the faint timing of `fieldEvent` is implemented where it shows: `dfi_residual_faints` after a side condition, Trick Room or terrain that does not run out, after White Herb, and before the stall counters when one of them does not end. A Heal Block that ends in the same residual as a Perish count is refused (E_UNSUPPORTED): its end line would come before the counts in the reference and after them in the engine's block; the cheap and the exact order tests of `residual_order.h` count the Perish handler as a volatile of its Pokemon. A move with priority above 0 (Prankster) is refused for the same reason as Armor Tail and Psychic Terrain. Two things that the first campaign found: a holder whose faint is queued (hp 0, `faint()` called) still has its volatiles until `faintMessages` clears them, so its stall counter that does not end is a faint point, while a holder whose faint was processed has none (the engine clears the slot when it processes the faint) and its White Herb handler is skipped (`sim/battle.ts:525-554`: the state is not at its place any more); and the two sides' Tailwind (or Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil: order 26, one sub-order each) tie in the sort, a shuffle that the conversion drops when the two only count down. The same holds for the handlers that have no order (the stall counter, a charge, Protect's and Helping Hand's duration): they come last, in the Speed order of their holders (`comparePriority` puts no order after every order), and a faint point at one that does not end can end the battle with the handlers after it unrun, so the ones before it have ended and the ones after it keep their counters; the engine now runs them in Speed order (it ran them in position order before; three campaign cases showed it). The tie between one Pokemon's Protect volatile and its stall counter (equal keys: one holder, one Speed, one sub-order; every Protect turn has one, a draw `random(start, start + 2)` of the reference's sort) shows in one case only: the battle ends at the faint point that follows the stall counter, and a Pokemon that still stands has kept Protect's volatile when the stall counter ran first and lost it when Protect's own handler did (24 of 24 and 17 of 18 reference survivors in the campaigns that found it). The engine draws it then (`dfi_residual_pair_draws`, `src/combat/turn.c`: the battle ended in the residual and a standing Pokemon has exactly those two handlers; the draw is spent unseen when the end was earlier, at a Perish count or a side condition) and the conversion keeps exactly that draw (`no_order_end_tie`, `tools/reference/trace_to_c.py`, next to `side_end_tie` and `heal_block_end_tie`): the group is the reference's list order, which the state does not hold, so the entry states the outcome (0 the stall counter ran first, 1 Protect's volatile did), and a uniform shuffle makes the engine's own draw the same distribution. A tie of more than the pair (an equal-Speed neighbour, Helping Hand's volatile too) or a survivor with another handler stays `E_UNSUPPORTED` when the battle ends there; a Pokemon that fainted has lost its volatiles, so it never matters. The tie of the sides' screens now shows when a faint is queued and exactly one side's counter ends in this residual (the faints come after the one that does not end, before or after the other's `-sideend`), and when the battle ends at the first side's faint point (the other side's remaining turns show it): both are `E_UNSUPPORTED`. A faint that no damage line announced shows the Pokemon at 0 in the foe's knowledge (`dfi_events_fold_knowledge`): the screen shows it gone, and the knowledge invariant needs it.
   - **Events (the new public value).** `DUOFORGE_EVENT_VOLATILE_START` with `detail` `DUOFORGE_VOLATILE_PERISH` (5) and `amount` the count N (3, 2, 1, 0): one per holder and residual; no END (the holder faints, or leaves with no line). `-fieldactivate|move: Perish Song` is `DUOFORGE_EVENT_ACTIVATE` with cause MOVE, `id2` the move and no position (documented in `duoforge.h`); `-fail` and `-immune` are the existing events.
