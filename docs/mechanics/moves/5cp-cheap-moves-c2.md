5cp. **C2, cheap moves: 22 damaging moves with secondaries or self effects (lane A batch, builder H24; the triage's C2 group).** Twenty-two moves are marked in `src/data/support_manifest.c` through the existing paths only: Zen Headbutt, Pyro Ball, Headlong Rush (self drops), Extrasensory, Chilling Water, Water Pulse, Lunge, Blaze Kick (crit ratio 2, burn), Acid Spray, Parabolic Charge (drain on allAdjacent), Torch Song (a self boost, sound and bypasssub), Lava Plume (burn, allAdjacent), Low Sweep, Skitter Smack, Zing Zap, Iron Tail, Dynamic Punch (confusion, punch), Mud Shot, Cross Poison (poison, crit ratio 2, slicing), Pounce, Mud-Slap, Apple Acid. Its pin facts (power, accuracy, PP, priority, type, target, flags, secondaries with chances, self boosts, drain) are read by `check_c2_facts` (`tools/datagen/gen_closure.py`, data/moves.ts at b2cb775; the three Champions base power overrides of Apple Acid, Mountain Gale and Night Daze are read too). **Not marked** (eight of the thirty): Fire Fang and Thunder Fang (two secondaries; the only handler of two is Ice Fang's freeze and flinch, `DFI_SPECIAL_ICE_FANG`; a status-parameterised handler would be a new special id), Horn Leech (its learners Gogoat and Trevenant have no marked ability), Mountain Gale (its learner Avalugg-Hisui is refused by the converter, `swap_partners` KeyError, as Goodra-Hisui in C1), Bitter Malice and Night Daze (their only learners carry Illusion, whose duplicate-name gap is open), Charge Beam and Wild Charge (the Substitute path of the engine, see below; the lead's separate part marks them after its fixes). **Evidence.** Campaigns are random differential battles of fixed team sets E to I (`diff_driver.py random`, `--keep-traces`, six sets each from the registry's learnsets and marks); round 1 (seed 9002, with Substitute users) gave 279 PASS and 21 DIVERGENCE, round 2 (seed 9003) 297 PASS and 3 DIVERGENCE: every divergent step there has a Substitute, and none is a C2 data row (the Substitute path, see below). Rounds 3 (seed 9004), 4 (9005) and the targeted round 5 (9006, 600 battles, pairings weighted to teams E and I) were run without Substitute users: 300 of 300, 300 of 300 and 599 of 600 PASS (one UNSUPPORTED, fz_9006_10, step 5: a Cursed Body Disable on a Zen Headbutt hit, then a Taunt; the refusal site is not localised yet, see the PR body). **Usage** (rounds 3 to 5 together; round 5 alone in brackets): Zen Headbutt 3913 (2138), Low Sweep 2714 (1591), Iron Tail 2996 (1016), Skitter Smack 2255 (1306), Lunge 1310 (746), Dynamic Punch 1623 (689), Water Pulse 996 (451), Acid Spray 949 (594), Mud Shot 1026 (481), Pounce 880 (549), Cross Poison 789 (345), Chilling Water 667 (271), Headlong Rush 625 (310), Pyro Ball 569 (465), Torch Song 469 (192), Lava Plume 501 (243), Parabolic Charge 419 (176), Extrasensory 327 (246), Zing Zap 369 (298), Blaze Kick 314 (127), Apple Acid 356 (121), Mud-Slap 247 (111). Three of the fourteen open pairs drop out, because no C2 move they react to is left: Rock Head and Reckless (their recoil move Wild Charge is dropped) and Justified (its C2 attacker would be a Dark move: none is left, Night Daze is dropped).
