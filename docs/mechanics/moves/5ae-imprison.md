# 5ae. G38, Imprison

- Area: moves
- Step: G38
- Tests: duoforge.moves.imprison

5ae. **G38, Imprison** (184 of the Reg M-C and 694 of the Reg M-B teams; the volatile that the tail's `imprison` flag of rev 2 was reserved for). One handler id, `IMPRISON` (47; UNMODELED moves to 48); the move is marked; the two public values `DUOFORGE_VOLATILE_IMPRISON` = 8 (VOLATILE_START only) and `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_IMPRISON` = 21 (CANT, the stopped move in `id`) and the producing of view bit 2 (`IMPRISON`, supported bit 12) were approved by the owner.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod has no entry of the move): `data/moves.ts:9489-9523`. `onStart` (`:9501`) prints `-start|user|move: Imprison` (the target is the user, `target: "self"`); the volatile has no duration and ends with the occupant (`sim/pokemon.ts:1508` `clearVolatile`, on a switch-out or a faint, with no line). `onFoeDisableMove` (`:9504-9510`) runs for every active Pokemon in `endTurn` (`sim/battle.ts:1685-1695`) and calls `disableMove(slot.id, true)` for every move of the imprisoner (Struggle aside): a **hidden** disable (`sim/pokemon.ts:1625-1636`; one that is already `true` stays). `onFoeBeforeMove` (`:9511-9519`, priority 4) stops a move that the imprisoner knows (`hasMove`, its current move slots; not Struggle) with `cant|X|move: Imprison|Move` and no PP used: it matters for a move that was queued before the Imprison was used this turn, and for an Encored one. A second Imprison of a Pokemon that has it fails (`-fail`, the move line with `[still]`; there is no `onTry`).
   - **The request.** A hidden-disabled move is shown as enabled in the request of the last active Pokemon of a side and as disabled for the others (`sim/pokemon.ts:1025-1027`, `isLastActive` `:1192`), but the choice is validated against `getMoves()`, where hidden counts as disabled (`sim/side.ts:718-737`): the choice of an imprisoned move is rejected. With every move so disabled `getMoves()` is empty and the choice is Struggle (`sim/pokemon.ts:1119`, `sim/side.ts:699-706`), which Imprison does not forbid. The engine's request (`dfi_slot_candidates`, `request.c`) does not offer the move: it is exactly the set that Showdown accepts, which the domain check of the random mode compares. **Information:** Showdown hides that the imprisoner knows the move (the request shows it enabled for the last active Pokemon and the rejection comes only on the choice); the engine's mask removes it, so the request of the foe tells which moves the imprisoner knows. Under open team sheets (Reg M-C now) the moves are public and nothing leaks; under closed sheets (the planned M14) it would, and a request for a player there has to show the moves as enabled and reject the choice instead (the `hidden` rows of the harness mark them).
   - **Interactions.** *Encore* (`:4754-4775`): its `onDisableMove` disables every other slot, Imprison the encored one: no move is left, the choice is Struggle, and Encore's `onOverrideAction` does not replace Struggle (recorded: `g38_imprison_encore`); an Encored move that is queued when the Imprison comes is stopped by `cant`. *Choice lock* (`data/conditions.ts:324-363`): the lock disables the other slots and Imprison the locked one: Struggle, which the lock's `onBeforeMove` lets pass (`id !== 'struggle'`), and the lock stays (`g38_imprison_choice`). *Two imprisoners* hide the union of their moves; their `onFoeBeforeMove` handlers have the same priority, so a tie of their speeds is a draw in the reference whose order shows in nothing (either handler stops the move with the same line): the converter drops it (`event:BeforeMove` of Imprison handlers only), and the `DisableMove` tie too (every handler only sets `disabled`).
   - **Engine** (`turn.c`, `request.c`): `dfi_run_imprison` (the flag and the start event, or the failure), `dfi_imprison_forbids` and its check in `dfi_before_move` after Throat Chop and Heal Block (6) and before the confusion (3), the request mask. The flag is the tail's (rev 2: no new state), cleared with the occupant (`dfi_tail_clear_occupant`). Under the POOL kinds only. The view: bit 2 of the position's volatiles from the flag, public for both viewers, supported bit 12.
   - **Converter and harness.** `-start|X|move: Imprison` is VOLATILE_START (detail 8), `cant|X|move: Imprison|Move` is CANT (cause 21, id the move); an `-end` of it is refused. The reference state's `imprison` volatile is compared through the lines, the requests and the view bit. `ps_trace.js` records, only while a move is hidden-disabled, `hidden` per active slot next to `enabled` (a state without the key is what every earlier trace has: no harness version change), and `trace_to_c.py` takes the hidden moves out of the request's move mask and makes Struggle of a request with none left.
   - **Evidence.** Eight recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; `g38_*`): `g38_imprison_mask` (the masks of two foes), `_struggle` (only move imprisoned), `_cant` (a queued move stopped, the stopped move the imprisoner's last), `_encore`, `_choice`, `_end` (a second Imprison fails; the volatile ends with a switch-out), `_pair` (two imprisoners), `_struggle_b` (a foe whose only move is imprisoned, in the last active slot: the request still shows it, any `move N` is Struggle, `sim/side.ts:699-706` before the disabled check; the converter's `convert_choice` reads the `hidden` rows for it). `duoforge.moves.imprison` (the tail's flag after every step, the events, both viewers' extension; rows derived from the protocol lines by `test_trace_to_c.py`). Mutation checks (seven edits of `turn.c`, `request.c` and `observation.c`): the request not masking, the mask on the imprisoner's own side, no `cant`, a second Imprison not failing, no view bit and the last move slot not forbidden are each killed by the conformance or `pool_g38`.
