5dn. **Ally Switch: the 92 cases of issue 541 (branch claude/project-thread-fxsdy2, stacked on 5dl and 5dm; found by the differential fuzzing of issue 396).** Eleven causes, one commit each; three in the converter (`tools/reference/trace_to_c.py`), the rest in `src/combat/turn.c`, `src/combat/events.c` and the tail invariant. (a) **The viewer's own Pressure holders** (fz_159_24): the foe's PP a player can attribute (decision 0030 section 1) counts its own holder's extra at the move's target position; the own-side timeline of `dfi_own_event` ignored SWAP, so after an Ally Switch it charged the holder that had stood there. Now SWAP exchanges the two occupants. (b) **The converter's Illusion entry** (fz_302_61): `step_events` read a switching member's roster index from `truth_at` after the step's swaps; it now starts from the entries before them. (c) **A move aimed at the user's own location** (fz_410_72): `useMoveInner` prints `[notarget]` for a null target before TryMove (`sim/battle-actions.ts:457-465`), so a two-turn move charges nothing; the engine charged it. The count-0 aim is now no position when the chosen location is the user's. (d) **A locked two-turn move's target** (fz_140_80): `dfi_swap_position_state` moves `locked_target` with the slot record, as the queued action follows the Pokemon. (e) **Leech Seed on its source's slot** (fz_233_98): the seed's source is a slot (`getAtSlot(sourceSlot)`), so an Ally Switch can put the seeded Pokemon on it; it then drains into itself and the damage line has no `[of]` (`sim/battle.ts:2155`). The tail invariant refused that state (E_INVARIANT); it now allows the holder's own position, and `dfi_leech_drain` prints no source for it. The state model and the pool tail sweep follow, and the live tracker (`python/duoforge_live/tracker.py`) moves the seed and the locked target with the Pokemon on a swap, as the engine's tail and slot records do, and accepts the drain with no `[of]` on the source's slot. (f) **The converter's self-drain heal** (same battle): `leech_heal_source` takes the healed Pokemon from the target when the line has no `[of]`. (g) **A locked turn aimed at the user's own location** (fz_303_30): on the locked turn validTargetLoc refuses the user's location (`sim/battle.ts:2399-2431`), so getTarget draws a random foe; `dfi_move_targets` now does. (h) **The epilogue of a moved user** (fz_168_77): after an Ally Switch the queued action is the mover's at its new slot, so the lock countdown and the fatigue confusion of Outrage run there. (i) **The converter's Pressure tracker and drags** (fz_340_60): `FoePressure._own` follows `drag` lines as well as switches. (j) **Stalwart's original target** (fz_205_98): getTarget returns the original target while Stalwart is active (`sim/battle.ts:2437-2447`, tracksTarget). A MOVE queue record carries it in reserve bits 2 to 4 (`DFI_QRES_ORIGINAL_*`: the occupant + 1, 7 for none), written while a Stalwart holder (or a Mega Evolution that would have it) stands; a record without it under Stalwart is refused (E_UNSUPPORTED). The state model and the codec mutation counts follow. (k) **Stalwart with a randomNormal move**: its original target is the draw resolveAction made at queue time (`sim/battle-queue.ts:268-273`), which the engine does not model: refused (E_UNSUPPORTED). **Evidence.** Nine recordings (`tests/reference/specs/fz541_*.json`, whole battles; seeds and choices of the campaign, traces from the pinned Showdown b2cb775); (k) stops its battle, so `duoforge.state.pool_g80` pins it white-box. Of the 92 cases 88 pass the runner; the other four stop with E_UNSUPPORTED at refusals already documented: (k) (fz_208_93), Stomping Tantrum after an unclassified last result (fz_110_106), Ally Switch beside a possible Illusion holder (decision 0044, fz_279_51) and a locked move that a confusion self-hit stops (fz_432_22). Mutants, each one fix reverted, over the 92 cases: each is killed by its own recording; (f) is killed only by battles that (e) also kills, so the two share one; the recording of (i) also kills (a) (its Pressure holder is swapped before it is dragged); no other cross-kill. Every other reference battle is unchanged: the pool set grows by these nine (1016 battles; live stops 193 to 197; pool tiebreak stops 5524 to 5619). **Not changed here:** the engine's own-side fold ignores a DRAG inside a step (no case); the Zoroark-Trick gap of 5dl stays open.
