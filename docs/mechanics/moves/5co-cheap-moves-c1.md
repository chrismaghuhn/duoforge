5co. **C1, cheap moves (lane A batch, builder H21; the status and plain damage moves of the triage's cheap bucket).** Eighteen moves are marked in `src/data/support_manifest.c` through the existing paths only: Slack Off (heal 1/2), Cotton Guard, Glare, Acid Armor, Feather Dance, Baby-Doll Eyes, Metal Sound, Rock Polish (status and self or target boosts, the primary status of Glare), and Aerial Ace, Seed Bomb, Megahorn, Mega Kick, Drill Peck, Smart Strike, Aqua Tail, Shadow Punch, Brutal Swing and Petal Blizzard (plain damage; the target classes any and allAdjacent; the flags as the pin gives them). No table changes: the POOL table hash is unchanged and the generator's `check_c1_facts` reads the 21 pin entries (power, accuracy, PP, priority, type, target, flags, boosts, status and the heal fraction, with no Champions override). Glare, Feather Dance, Baby-Doll Eyes and Metal Sound are reflectable and join the Magic Bounce handled list of `tests/test_pool_g57.c` (24 becomes 28). **Not marked**: Jet Punch (its only learner, Palafin, has Zero to Hero, which is unmarked); Shelter (its only learner, Goodra-Hisui, cannot be recorded: the converter's switch identifier reads `Goodra`, while `swap_partners` in `tools/reference/trace_to_c.py` keys the roster by `Goodra-Hisui`, a KeyError; a tool finding, no fix in this step); Shift Gear (the pin applies its boosts in the key order, spe +2 before atk +1, `data/moves.ts:16330-16347`; the engine applies a boost in stat order except Shell Smash's special, so this needs a new order rule and a new handler id). **Evidence.** Four random campaigns (`diff_driver.py random`, `--keep-traces`). Run 21021 (four teams with Substitute users, 14 pairings incl. the Sitrus mirrors): 137 PASS, 55 DIVERGENCE, 108 ORACLE_GAP (every battle of the team with Goodra-Hisui). The divergences all occur in battles where a Substitute was created (55 of 152; none of 40 without one) and are the two known main bugs of the Substitute path (the hit-loop Updates of a successful Substitute, and a secondary roll into a Substitute), fixed in other batch 5 parts; a control with registry teams A and B (60 battles, same driver) is 60 of 60 PASS. Run 21022 (the same teams without Substitute users, without Goodra-Hisui): 300 of 300 PASS, with every marked move used at least 126 times (Slack Off, the rarest of the 18: 126 uses, 86 heals) and every one of them hit or applied at least 3 times; every pairing with a Sitrus mirror included. Run 21023 (E against F, the Rough Skin pairs): 40 of 40 PASS, Rough Skin against Shadow Punch in 28 battles. Mutants (five one-edit mutants of the rows, each replayed on the fixed corpus subset of 16 battles of run 21022, `diff_driver.py corpus`; baseline 16 of 16 PASS): Metal Sound's sound flag off (5 of 16 diverge), Cotton Guard's defence +2 instead of +3 (9), Glare at accuracy 90 (2), Baby-Doll Eyes at priority 0 (9), Petal Blizzard's target class normal (5). **Recorded battles.** The usage test of `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py` needs a committed pool battle that shows an effect for each marked move; two campaign battles of run 21022 cover all eighteen (`c1_cover_a`: fourteen moves; `c1_cover_b`: Glare, Rock Polish, Shadow Punch and Slack Off), recorded with the pinned Showdown (`ps_trace.js --check` matches), so the pool battle count is 719 with main's G65 battles and the tiebreak stops 3945.
