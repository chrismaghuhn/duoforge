# 5ad. G33, multi-hit moves and Mirror Armor

- Area: moves
- Step: G33
- Tests: -

5ad. **G33, multi-hit moves and Mirror Armor** (no new public value, no new event kind, no view change, no state). Marked: **Dual Wingbeat** and **Twin Beam** (`multihit: 2`, `data/moves.ts`), **Triple Axel** (`multihit: 3`, `multiaccuracy: true`, `basePowerCallback` of 20 x the hit number) and the ability **Mirror Armor** (`data/abilities.ts:2657-2679`). Tables: two handlers `MULTI_HIT_2` (52) and `TRIPLE_AXEL` (53) after the four of G25, UNMODELED 54 (`G33_HANDLERS`); the hit counts live in the handler ids, not in the static `multihit` columns (decision 0020 item 5 has no engine reader). The hit loop is the Champions format's own (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:427-549`, the base game's `sim/battle-actions.ts:857-979`): every hit rolls its critical hit and its damage, runs the DamagingHit handlers (Rocky Helmet and Rough Skin cost the user HP after every hit) and an Update; the faint messages come once after the loop; a multiaccuracy move checks its accuracy again before each later hit (with its own arithmetic, found by the first campaign: the loop scales the accuracy by the two stages one after the other in floating point with no floor, `accuracy /= boostTable[-boost]` (`scripts.ts:481-510`), where the first hit's `hitStepAccuracy` floors; the accuracy 90 at the stage -1 is 67.5 and the draw 67 hits. The engine has no floating point outside the tiebreak, so it compares `v x D < N` for the exact rational, and `checkG33` proves that this equals the pin's binary64 result for every pair of stages: 169 of 169), a miss ends the loop silently; the loop also ends when the target is at 0 HP, the user has fallen asleep or fainted; recoil, Life Orb and the AfterMoveSecondary handlers run once after the loop. A spread move with more than one hit is refused (`E_UNSUPPORTED`). **Population Bomb stays unmarked** (`multihit: 10`, multiaccuracy): its only user, Maushold, has no marked ability, so no battle that uses it can be recorded; the generator pins its data (`checkG33`) and nothing else. **Mirror Armor**: each lowered stat of the holder that another Pokemon caused is not applied; if that Pokemon is standing, it gets the same drop (a `-ability|holder|Mirror Armor` event, then the boost with Mirror Armor as the effect: no second bounce); a drop that Clear Body or a veil blocks is not bounced. Parting Shot keeps its switch when its drop was bounced (`moves.ts:13174-13180`). **The converter** maps the derived line `-hitcount|P|N` as an assertion that N equals the -damage lines of that move on P and drops it (rule `hitcount-mismatch`); it asserts only when the line is present (the Champions loop prints none for Parental Bond when only the first hit landed, `scripts.ts:547-548`). Whoever builds Substitute must count the hits into a Substitute too (`-activate|P|move: Substitute|[damage]`, `-end|P|Substitute`): they are not -damage lines, yet N counts them. `tools/reference/ps_trace.js` classifies the multiaccuracy `randomChance` called from `hitStepMoveHitLoop` as an ACCURACY draw (it was UNKNOWN; harness version unchanged). **Evidence**: seven recorded POOL battles (`g33_dual_wingbeat`, `g33_dual_wingbeat_items`, `g33_triple_axel`, `g33_triple_axel_accuracy`, `g33_triple_axel_stages`, `g33_twin_beam`, `g33_mirror_armor`: hit counts 1, 2 and 3, Triple Axel misses, a Protected target, a Focus Sash, a Life Orb that knocks the user out, a Dark type that is immune, Intimidate and Icy Wind bounced, Defiant and Competitive answering, Parting Shot bounced twice with the user still switching; and a Sitrus Berry eaten between the two hits, an Emergency Exit holder that leaves after the second); `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` calls the pinned callbacks (`checkG33`). Mirror Armor with a fainted source and with a Grass-type holder are pinned in `checkG33` only, not recorded. **Known gaps**: the later-hit arithmetic is proved for the accuracy 90 only, so a multiaccuracy move with another accuracy is refused (`combat/moves/multiaccuracy.h`; a new such move extends the proof in `checkG33` first); and a target's Emergency Exit after a multi-hit move reads the HP before the whole move, where the pin reads `hurtThisTurn` plus `move.totalDamage` (the two differ only when the target was hurt earlier in the turn and its first hit crosses half; no Emergency Exit holder is a legal target of a multi-hit move of the pool in that way today, so the case is unreachable).
