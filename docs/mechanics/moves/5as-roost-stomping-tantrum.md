# 5as. G42, Roost and Stomping Tantrum

- Area: moves
- Step: G42
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g42

5as. **G42, Roost and Stomping Tantrum** (no new public value: the view bit 40 (ROOST) that decision 0018 names is supported, bit 41 (MOVE_FAILED) is not; no version bump). Roost (`data/moves.ts:15428-15463`) heals the user (the heal of step G10) and then takes the self volatile `roost` (duration 1, residual order 25, `data/moves.ts:15444-15455`): its `onType` removes the Flying type after the other types. An empty list is Normal from gen 5 (`sim/pokemon.ts:2143`), so a pure Flying Pokemon is Normal for the turn. The self volatile is applied only to a target that did not fail (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1088-1096`), so a user at full HP gets a false result and no volatile. Stomping Tantrum (`data/moves.ts:18054-18058`) doubles its base power when the user's `moveLastTurnResult` is `false`, strictly: null is no failure.
  - **Tail.** The Roost volatile is the single_turn bit `DFI_SINGLE_TURN_ROOST` (rev 4, layout unchanged), set at the heal, cleared by the residual of order 25 (silent), and read by `dfi_types_of`, so the Flying filter follows the volatile.
  - **The classifier.** `dfi_run_move` records the pin's `moveThisTurnResult` of each action in `mres`. The pin's rule (`sim/battle-actions.ts` `useMove`): the result is the return value of `useMoveInner`, unless the action itself assigned the value (null for NOT_FAIL, false for `beforeMove`, `nopp` and a `beforeMoveCallback`). In the engine every exit of the move body writes a class: **TRUE** when the effect applied (a damaging hit, a status or boost that changed, a heal, a side, field, weather or terrain that is set, Trick Room, Perish Song with one holder, Wide Guard, Imprison, Helping Hand, Coaching, Follow Me, Rage Powder, Aurora Veil, a Protect that goes up, a pivot's hit); **FALSE** for a `[still]` failure (`dfi_fail_still`: the volatile or condition is already up, Protect repeated and failing, a stall roll, Steel Roller off its terrain, a first-turn-only move, a Sucker Punch against a status move), a miss, an immunity, a Good as Gold stop, an ally that will not move, a boost that changes nothing, Armor Tail's and the TryMove stops (`cant`), `cant nopp`, and no target (`[notarget]`); **NULL** for a move that Protect or Wide Guard took (their `TryHit` returns NOT_FAIL, `data/moves.ts:13998`, `:20838-20848`), a recharge stop, and the charge turn of a two-turn move (its `onTryMove` returns null, `data/moves.ts` solarbeam). The two bits of an action move to bit 5 at the end of its turn (`dfi_end_turn`, `sim/battle.ts:1674-1675`). **UNCLASSIFIED** (bit 4 this turn, bit 5 the last turn, both result values zero) is written only when no exit writes a class: a static audit of every exit of the move body (the list in the PR body) finds none, so the bit is the safety net of `dfi_run_move` and no battle or campaign has it. Stomping Tantrum doubles only on a FALSE last result and refuses (E_UNSUPPORTED) on the last unclassified bit.
  - **Enumeration.** The 200 marked moves by the class of their exit (the counts of the support manifest against the pool rows): damaging with secondary, recoil, drain or plain 116 (among them the pivots U-turn, Volt Switch and Flip Turn); damaging with a handler rule 16; damaging with a condition 6 (Fake Out, First Impression, Feint, Sucker Punch, Sacred Sword, Darkest Lariat); damaging multi-hit or spread 3; the protect family 3 (Protect, Detect, Spiky Shield); self boosts 9 (Coil, Swords Dance, Calm Mind, Nasty Plot, Agility, Bulk Up, Dragon Dance, Quiver Dance, Iron Defense); heal 3 (Recover, Roost, Life Dew); boosts of the target 2 (Charm, Fake Tears); ally support 2 (Coaching, Helping Hand); primary status to target 6 (Hypnosis, Poison Powder, Sleep Powder, Stun Spore, Toxic, Will-O-Wisp); volatile or item effect to target 11 (Covet, Disable, Encore, Imprison, Parting Shot, Soak, Switcheroo, Taunt, Thief, Trick, Yawn); side conditions 7 (Light Screen, Reflect, Tailwind, Spikes, Stealth Rock, Sticky Web, Toxic Spikes); Aurora Veil 1; Wide Guard 1; weather 4; terrain 5; Trick Room 1; Perish Song 1; redirection 2 (Follow Me, Rage Powder); charge 1 (Solar Beam).
  - **Changed in the review.** The status effect that changes nothing is FALSE (it was unclassified); a self boost that succeeds is TRUE (Coil was unclassified); side, hazard, weather, terrain, Trick Room, Imprison, Wide Guard, Aurora Veil, Perish Song and the Helping Hand and Coaching successes are TRUE; Armor Tail's stop, `cant nopp`, Good as Gold's stops and a boost that changes nothing are FALSE; the charge turn is NULL.
  - **Refusals of the first campaign.** Three step refusals (fz_4242_61 at step 35, fz_4242_62 at step 20, fz_4242_102 at step 14) were identified with a diagnostic build (`turn.c` printing each E_UNSUPPORTED site; reverted): all three come from `dfi_residual_pair_draws` ("a bigger group: its shuffle is not modelled"): three or more holders of equal Speed whose no-order residual handlers (Protect's volatile and the stall counter) tie at the end of a turn. That refusal is on origin/main and is the limit of decision 0015 G26; it is not a G42 refusal, and none of the three is the unclassified refusal of Stomping Tantrum.
  - **Roost under Heal Block** is not reachable: Heal Block is not a row of the legal pool. **A pure Flying Roost user** is not reachable either: no legal Champions pure Flying Pokemon learns Roost (the Normal rule of `dfi_types_of` is the reasoning above).
  - **The view.** `POSITION_EXT_ROOST` (volatiles bit 20; supported bit 40) is set while the Roost turn is open: only at a boundary inside the turn, the pivot of `g42_roost_pivot`, since the residual ends the volatile at every turn end. `move_failed` is 1 iff the last result is FALSE. Bit 41 stays clear in `dfi_support.view_ext_features` (the decision is the lead's; the safety net of the unclassified bit is not yet shown never to fire in the field).
  - **Evidence.** Eleven recorded POOL battles (genders are in the specs, each checked with `ps_trace.js --check`): `g42_roost_full` (Roost at full HP fails; Stomping Tantrum doubled after the failure; a Tantrum blocked by Protect is null and not doubled after it), `g42_tantrum_switch` (the Tantrum after a switch-in is not doubled), `g42_roost_heal` (a Roost at less than full HP: the heal, the Flying type off), `g42_tantrum_paralysis` (a full paralysis `cant` on turn 1; turn 2 doubled), `g42_tantrum_miss` (a Rock Tomb miss; turn 2 doubled), `g42_roost_ground_order` (a Ground move before the Roost is immune, after it hits, the next turn immune again; a second cycle), `g42_roost_pivot` (a U-turn after the Roost in the same turn: the pivot boundary shows the volatile, the pivoting position keeps its results until its replacement switches in), `g42_protect_tantrum` (Protect on turn 1 is TRUE, the Tantrum of turn 2 is not doubled), `g42_protect_stall_tantrum` (Protect on turn 1 TRUE, a repeated Protect fails FALSE on turn 2, the turn-3 Tantrum is doubled), `g42_tailwind_tantrum` (Tailwind TRUE, Tantrum not doubled, a repeated Tailwind FALSE, the next Tantrum doubled), `g42_coil_tantrum` (Coil TRUE on each turn, the Tantrums after it not doubled).
  - **Tests.** `tests/test_pool_g42.c` (`duoforge.state.pool_g42`): at each of the 196 boundary positions of the eleven battles, the two-bit `this` and `last` values, the unclassified bits and the ROOST bit, against a table derived from the pinned protocol lines (TRUE, FALSE, NULL and UNDEFINED as above; a faint or a switch-out clears a position; a pivot boundary shows the turn so far); the view of both viewers after each step; the unclassified refusal (bit 5 set on the Dragonite of `g42_tantrum_switch` before its Stomping Tantrum: E_UNSUPPORTED); the support mask (bit 40 yes, bit 41 no); the invariant check after every step. The conformance and tiebreak pool data run the eleven battles (335 pool battles, 1934 tiebreak stops). Counts updated: `test_pool_tables` (151 marked moves in the pool tables, the closure ids excluded), `test_observation_ext` (the mask), `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py`, `tools/datagen/test_gen_closure.py` (ROOST 63, STOMPING_TANTRUM 64, UNMODELED 65). `tools/reference/trace_to_c.py` takes the `roost` volatile into IGNORED_VOLATILES.
  - **Mutation.** Eleven mutants of the rules, each caught by a targeted test: M1 Roost sets no volatile; M2 the volatile is set at full HP; M3 the move result survives the occupant's leaving; M4 Stomping Tantrum doubles on null and on undefined; M5 the turn end does not shift this turn into last; M6 the Roost bit is never cleared; M7 no Flying filter; M8 a miss is classified null; M9 a successful status or self boost is not TRUE; M10 a set side condition is not TRUE; U the unclassified refusal of Stomping Tantrum is removed. Eleven of eleven caught (the list of targets is in the PR body).
  - **Campaigns.** (a) 300 random POOL battles, seed 4242, teams D and E (the first run had Sand Force, which is not modelled, and was rerun with Sand Rush): PASS 297, UNSUPPORTED 3 (the step refusals above), no DIVERGENCE; domain 1240 of 1260 agreed. (b) 300 random POOL battles, seed 4343, teams F and G (Protect and Stomping Tantrum users, Dragonite with Roost and Stomping Tantrum): PASS 300, UNSUPPORTED 0, no DIVERGENCE; domain 1089 of 1089 agreed. Unclassified refusals counted separately: 0 in both. Team F and G are composed sets from the legal pool (the registry's 654 real teams hold no Stomping Tantrum or Roost): they are not ladder pulls.
  - **Not in this step.** Roost under Heal Block and the pure-Flying Normal case (above).
