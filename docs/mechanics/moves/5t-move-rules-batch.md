# 5t. G28, a move-rules batch

- Area: moves

5t. **G28, a move-rules batch** (six moves and one item; the first step after the AWS triage that adds engine rules for several moves at once). The step marks Shell Smash, Acrobatics, Blizzard, Ancient Power, Feint and Earthquake, and the item Expert Belt, each from the pin and the Champions mod (`data/mods/champions` overrides none of them). No public value, no new event, no view change: Feint's line is the generic ACTIVATE event with the move as its cause, Ancient Power's boosts are a self change with no line of their own. Tables: `SHELL_SMASH`, `ACROBATICS`, `BLIZZARD` and `FEINT` are new named handler ids 29 to 32 (`G28_HANDLERS` of the generator; UNMODELED moves to 33 (Spiky Shield of G20 is 28)), the boost-role column has the new value `SECONDARY_SELF` (5, internal, `pool_tables.h`) and the secondary kind `SECONDARY_SELF_BOOST` (7), the target class allAdjacent (11) is now an engine target (`ENGINE_TARGETS`), and Expert Belt is an `ENGINE_ROWS` item, so the POOL table hash and the fingerprints change as for every pool step.
   - **The pin.** `data/moves.ts`: Shell Smash 16255-16275 (`boosts` in the order def, spd, atk, spa, spe), Acrobatics 117-141 (`basePowerCallback`: doubled while `!pokemon.item`), Blizzard 1491-1510 (accuracy 70, `onModifyMove` sets `accuracy = true` in snow, 10 percent freeze, allAdjacentFoes), Ancient Power 396-418 (secondary, chance 10, `self: { boosts: {atk, def, spa, spd, spe: 1} }`), Feint (priority 2, `breaksProtect`, no protect flag), Earthquake 4390 (allAdjacent; Grassy Terrain's `onModifyDamage` 7692-7703 halves earthquake, bulldoze and magnitude at a grounded target), `data/items.ts` Expert Belt 1901-1914 (`onModifyDamage` 4915/4096 when `target.getMoveHitData(move).typeMod > 0`), `sim/battle-actions.ts` `hitStepBreakProtect` 755-780 (after the accuracy step, 568-571), `sim/pokemon.ts` `getMoveTargets` 809-817 (allies first, then foes). The generator reads the callbacks as `G28_FACTS` and `G28_ITEM_FACTS` (whitespace aside) and `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` calls the pinned handlers (`checkG28`).
   - **Shell Smash** changes the stats in the order of its entry: a pass over the stats that fall (Defense, Special Defense), then the ones that rise (Attack, Special Attack, Speed): `negatives_first` of the boost path, the stat order within a pass is the table's. A White Herb then restores the lowered stats after the last change (recorded). G21 had unmarked it for this event order.
   - **Acrobatics** reads the item the user holds now (`item_now`): doubled to 110 with no item, also when it was knocked off, eaten or used up. **Blizzard** never misses in snow (`base_accuracy` 0 in the weather, the accuracy draw is not made) and keeps 70 otherwise. **Ancient Power** gives the user all five stats +1 when its 10 percent secondary roll succeeds. **Feint** removes the Protect volatile of every target it hits and the Wide Guard of the target's whole side (Quick Guard, Crafty Shield and Mat Block are not marked); when anything broke it prints `-activate|target|move: Feint` and deletes the stall volatile of that target (its chain and stall counter go to 0 even if it did not protect; a partner that set the guard keeps its own); nothing breaks at a miss (Feint's accuracy is 100, so the break-at-a-miss mutation is equivalent in every recorded battle).
   - **Earthquake** is allAdjacent: `getMoveTargets` takes the ally first if it stands, then the foes, each answers for itself (a Flying foe is immune, Wide Guard of a side stops it at that side's targets, the ally's own protection counts), the spread modifier 0.75 applies when more than one target is left, and the move line carries `[spread]`. **Grassy Terrain halves it** at a grounded target (a Ground move is not a Grass move, so the terrain's two branches never both apply); the halving is the first branch of the same handler, chained with the other modifiers. This was found while writing the battle (`g28_earthquake_ally`); no earlier step had a Ground spread. Bulldoze and Magnitude have the same halving at the pin; Bulldoze is data-modelled now (allAdjacent) and stays unmarked (the engine's check names Earthquake alone).
   - **Expert Belt**: 4915/4096 in the ModifyDamage chain when the type effectiveness is above neutral. The ModifyDamage chain of three modifiers with 4915, 2048 and 2732 gives the same result in every order (checked by script), so Expert Belt joins the chain as one more modifier; the existing guard on four modifiers stays.
   - **What became data-modelled but stays unmarked** (the generator now reads their rows, so they leave the UNMODELED list without being marked): the nine allAdjacent moves other than Earthquake (Boomburst, Brutal Swing, Bulldoze, Discharge, Lava Plume, Parabolic Charge, Petal Blizzard, Sludge Wave, Surf; Earthquake itself is marked) and the nine secondary self-boost moves (Aqua Step, Charge Beam, Fiery Dance, Flame Charge, Meteor Mash, Psyshield Bash, Steel Wing, Torch Song, Trailblaze). UNMODELED moves 299 to 276, items 43 (after G23-A) to 38. A later step marks them with their own battles (`test_pool_rows.py` still pins what nothing reads).
   - **The spectator fold of Feint** (`python/duoforge_live`, its own commit, reviewed by M11): `duoforge.python.replay` stopped on `-activate move: Feint` (`lines.Stop: line:-activate move: Feint`, found on `g28_feint_protect`). `lines.py` folds the line; `tracker.py` clears, on the converter's ACTIVATE event with the cause MOVE and the Feint id, the target's Protect flag, chain and stall and its open `guard_undo`, and removes the Wide and Quick Guard markers of the target's side from the turn's single-turn set (the markers are kept per side for this: `_guards`), as `hitStepBreakProtect` does; a partner's chain stays.
   - **Evidence**: eleven recorded POOL battles (genders stated, seeds by predicate): `g28_shell_smash_order` (the unboosts before the boosts; a second Shell Smash at +6), `g28_shell_smash_white_herb`, `g28_acrobatics_item` (the same Acrobatics at the same target with Leftovers and, after a Knock Off took them, without), `g28_blizzard_snow` (a spread hit at both foes that never misses), `g28_blizzard_no_snow` (a miss), `g28_ancient_power` (the five rises), `g28_feint_protect` (Feint at a Protect, the next Protect of the target needs no roll, Feint at a Wide Guard of the side, and at a target that had both), `g28_feint_partner_guard` (Feint at a target that did not protect while its partner's Wide Guard is up: the line comes from the side condition alone, and the partner's chain stays: its next Protect is rolled and fails), `g28_earthquake_ally` (the ally Milotic hit first, a Flying foe immune, Grassy Terrain's halving, `[spread]` of all three), `g28_earthquake_wide_guard` (the guarded foes unhurt, the ally hurt), `g28_expert_belt` (super effective at Milotic, neutral at Incineroar). 183 pool battles and 992 stops (172 and 926 before; 11 battles with six stops each, counted by hand from `tiebreak_ref.h`: 66 added, none removed), 85 marked moves (79 before), the generator and row tests, `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` and `tiebreak_pool_data`. Mutation checks (sixteen, each one edit of src/combat/turn.c, rebuilt and run against conformance_pool_data, tiebreak_pool_data, pool_terrain, the family test and the corpus): thirteen make conformance_pool_data fail (Shell Smash in table order, the only one that tiebreak_pool_data survives; Acrobatics never or always doubled; Blizzard rolling in snow or never missing without it; Feint breaking nothing or keeping the stall counter; no Ancient Power boost; Earthquake sparing the ally; no Grassy halving; Wide Guard not stopping Earthquake; Expert Belt absent, on a neutral hit, or 1.1) and three survive: Feint breaking at a miss (equivalent: its accuracy is 100 and no recorded Feint misses), Earthquake not tagged spread (the tag is read only in the AfterMove step and only when the user fainted from the move, which Earthquake (no recoil, no contact) cannot cause: an equivalent mutant for now), Expert Belt on a neutral hit (survived the first ten battles, killed once g28_expert_belt had a neutral target). Campaign: two new teams (H: Blastoise, Staraptor, Garchomp, Abomasnow, Infernape, Aggron; I: Rillaboom, Garchomp, Raichu, Pelipper, Meowscarada, Blastoise with White Herb) against each other, themselves and teams A and B, seed 2801, 240 battles under the machine lock: 240 PASS, 880 sampled requests all agreed. Not covered by a recorded battle: Quick Guard (not marked), Shell Smash under Contrary, Feint at a spread target with a partner's guard on the other side.
