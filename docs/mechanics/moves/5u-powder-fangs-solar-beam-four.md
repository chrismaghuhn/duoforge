# 5u. G30, powder, Fangs, Solar Beam and four abilities

- Area: moves
- Step: G30
- Tests: duoforge.mechanics.sand_snow_weather, duoforge.state.pool_g30

5u. **G30, powder, Fangs, Solar Beam and four abilities** (the biggest blockers of the 2026-10-03 survey of the Reg M-C teams of VGCPastes and Limitless, `munchstats-2026-10-03`: Rage Powder 525 teams, Flame Body 389, Psychic Fangs 270; plus Solar Beam, Clear Body, Hospitality and Overcoat, and the rows that only waited for a recorded battle). Fourteen rows: the moves Rage Powder, Sleep Powder, Stun Spore, Poison Powder, Psychic Fangs, Solar Beam, Matcha Gotcha, Giga Drain, Energy Ball and Play Rough, and the abilities Flame Body, Clear Body, Hospitality and Overcoat. No new public value (see View), no new state.
   - **The pin** (the Champions mod overrides none of the entries: `data/mods/champions/moves.ts` and `abilities.ts` have none of them but Mega Sol's `isNonstandard`). Moves (`data/moves.ts`): Rage Powder :14598-14632 (priority 2, flag `powder`, `volatileStatus: 'ragepowder'`, `onTry` `activePerHalf > 1`, a one-turn condition whose `onFoeRedirectTarget` at priority 1 returns the user when `source.runStatusImmunity('powder')` fails to stop it and `validTarget` holds); Psychic Fangs :14060-14078 (`onTryHit` removes Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil of the target's side, in that order); Solar Beam :17224-17259 (`onTryMove`: `-prepare`, in sun `[still]` and `-anim` and the attack goes on, else `twoturnmove`; `onBasePower` x0.5 in rain, sand and snow); Sleep Powder :16851, Stun Spore :18274, Poison Powder :13512 (data, flag `powder`); Matcha Gotcha :11027, Giga Drain :6559, Energy Ball :4840, Play Rough :13420 (data). Abilities (`data/abilities.ts`): Flame Body :1316-1328, Clear Body :523-542, Hospitality :1874-1885, Overcoat :3108-3123. Engine: `hitStepTryImmunity` (`sim/battle-actions.ts:666-689`: the natural powder immunity first, then the Prankster test), spreadMoveHit's singleEvent `TryHit` (:1044, inside the hit loop after the accuracy check), `Battle.resolvePriority` (`sim/battle.ts:951-992`: an ability's handler sorts before an item's at one speed), `fieldEvent('SwitchIn')` and `getCallback` (:482-, :1020-1030: an ability's `onStart` is its SwitchIn handler at its `onSwitchInPriority`). The type chart's `powder: 3` is Grass alone. The generator reads the whole text of every callback and condition (`RAGE_POWDER_ONTRY`, `RAGE_POWDER_CONDITION`, `PSYCHIC_FANGS_ONTRYHIT`, `SOLAR_BEAM_CALLBACKS`, `G30_ABILITY_FACTS`: whitespace aside, with the Champions mod not overriding them) and the Grass-only powder immunity (`POWDER_IMMUNE_TYPES`), and fails on any other text.
   - **Tables.** Three handler ids: `RAGE_POWDER` 33, `PSYCHIC_FANGS` 34 and `SOLAR_BEAM` 35 (after G28's four, which follow G20's `SPIKY_SHIELD` 28), so UNMODELED is 36: the pool tables, their hash and the POOL fingerprints change (CLOSURE and TEAM_C do not). The powder flag is bit 16 of the second flags byte (`DFI_MOVE_FLAG2_POWDER`; the public static flag `DUOFORGE_MOVE_STATIC_FLAG_POWDER` of decision 0020 has no engine reader by rule, so the engine reads the byte). Flame Body, Clear Body, Hospitality and Overcoat are `ENGINE_ROWS` (handler NONE, run by id).
   - **Rage Powder needs no new state.** It shares the position's `DFI_VOL_FOLLOW_ME` bit with Follow Me, and the last move of the occupant (the tail's `last_move`, `dfi_last_move_id`) tells them apart: the bit is set by the move that its occupant is using, so that move is its `last_move`, and nothing but the occupant's next move changes it, which is after the residual has cleared the bit; a switch clears both; under a non-POOL kind `last_move` is always 0, so the bit is Follow Me's there. Follow Me's meaning is unchanged. The redirect loop of `dfi_run_move` skips a Rage Powder holder when the attacker is immune to powder (a Grass type, or Overcoat; Safety Goggles are not in the pool) and goes on to the next handler or the aimed target, with no line. A Follow Me and a Rage Powder that both apply on one side need the two handlers' order and are `E_UNSUPPORTED`, as two Follow Me holders were.
   - **View.** Rage Powder is the extension's `DUOFORGE_POSITION_EXT_RAGE_POWDER` (decision 0018 section 3.4, supported bit 39, public, a PIVOT boundary only: the residual ends it), filled from the same derivation; the basic view's `DUOFORGE_POSITION_FLAG_FOLLOW_ME` is not set for a Rage Powder user (it would say that every foe move is drawn). Both values were declared by decision 0018, so nothing is added to the header; the manifest sets bit 39. The Flame Body draw is the internal site `DFI_SITE_FLAME_BODY` 18 (17 is CURSED_BODY of G27), `DFI_SITE_COUNT` 19; the ps_trace harness names it `flamebody:DamagingHit` and the converter `FLAME_BODY`.
   - **Powder immunity and Overcoat** (`turn.c`). `hitStepTryImmunity`: a move with the powder flag stops at a Grass type of another Pokemon with a plain `-immune`, before the Prankster test; Overcoat's `onTryHit` (priority 1, the TryHit step with Good as Gold, `-immune ... [from] ability: Overcoat`) stops it for any other target; its `onImmunity` makes the holder immune to Sandstorm damage (`dfi_sand_immune`, and the sand check of `duoforge.mechanics.sand_snow_weather` no longer lists it). Overcoat is breakable; Mold Breaker is not marked.
   - **Psychic Fangs.** The singleEvent `TryHit` inside the hit loop: after the accuracy check (a miss or a Protect keeps the screens), before the damage of the hit, so that the hit is already without them; one `-sideend` line per screen that was up, in the order Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil.
   - **Solar Beam** is the two-turn lock of Electro Shot (`charge_turns`, `locked_move`, `locked_target`; the converter's `solarbeam` volatile is `electroshot`'s: the lock is the state's `locked`, twoturnmove stays to the residual) without the Special Attack boost, with sun in place of rain, and x0.5 (a 2048 modifier at priority 0, after Knock Off's) in rain, sand and snow. The pin's `effectiveWeather` asks Utility Umbrella and Mega Sol and `ChargeMove` is Power Herb's event: none is marked, `duoforge.state.pool_g30` checks it.
   - **Flame Body, Clear Body, Hospitality.** Flame Body: the target's own DamagingHit handler, after Rough Skin and Rocky Helmet, before the attacker's Poison Touch; a contact move draws `randomChance(3, 10)` (`random(10) < 3`, one draw per target, also for a holder that the hit knocked out, as Rough Skin) and burns the attacker (`-status ... brn [from] ability: Flame Body [of] holder`; a Fire type, a status, a Flower Veil and Thermal Exchange stop it as for any status). Clear Body: Flower Veil's place in `dfi_boost` (after Contrary and the cap, a drop that another Pokemon causes is deleted; `-fail|holder|unboost|[from] ability: Clear Body|[of] holder` unless the effect is a move's secondary, which is a FAIL event with the ability as its cause and the holder as `other`, as Inner Focus's (step G22): no new event; the holder's own drops, Draco Meteor, pass; Parting Shot does not switch its user out); a Grass type with Clear Body or Trace would need an order with Flower Veil's handler and the pool has none (checked). Hospitality: a SwitchIn handler of priority -2 (after the entry abilities and the seeds, with White Herb's, the ability of one Pokemon before its own herb): the adjacent ally is healed by a quarter of its base maximum HP (`[-heal] ally [from] ability: Hospitality [of] holder`), nothing at full HP or under Heal Block; at once when Trace copies it.
   - **Not in this step.** Stomping Tantrum (309 teams) needs a last-move-failed flag: the position's flags byte is full and the tail has no field for it; a new revision is planned with HauptSession (rev 4) and the move stays unmarked. Cotton Spore (primary boosts on foes) and Magic Powder (a type change) stay unmodelled. Safety Goggles, Utility Umbrella and Power Herb are not in the pool; Mega Sol and Stomping Tantrum are unmarked, and `duoforge.state.pool_g30` says so.
   - **Evidence.** Fifteen recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g30_*`, genders stated, seeds picked by predicates): `g30_rage_powder_a` (Garchomp's single-target move is drawn, Rillaboom's, a Grass type, is not), `_pivot` (the redirected U-turn's switch request inside the turn: the RAGE_POWDER bit at that boundary only), `g30_overcoat_rage` (Overcoat is not drawn; its sand immunity), `g30_overcoat_powder`, `g30_powder_a` and `_b` (paralysis, poison and sleep from the powders, the Grass, Electric, Steel and Dark immunities, the Prankster fail, a status that fails on a statused target), `g30_psychic_fangs` (a Protect that stops it, three screens, two screens), `g30_solar_beam_sun_rain` (sun skips the charge, rain replaces the sun: charge, lock, half power) and `_sand_snow` (half power in each, a Protect on the locked turn), `g30_flame_body` and `_ko` (burns, a Fire attacker, the holder knocked out), `g30_clear_body` (the entry Intimidates, Parting Shot, Snarl, Spirit Break, Draco Meteor), `g30_hospitality` (a hurt ally healed, an ally at full HP) and `_trace` (Trace copies it), `g30_matcha_drain_rows` (Matcha Gotcha on both foes with the burn, Giga Drain, Energy Ball, Play Rough). `duoforge.state.pool_g30` replays them and reads what the reference does not show (the extension and the basic flag of every position after every step) and the facts above; the converter handles every new line (`-fail ... unboost`, `-singleturn ... Rage Powder`) and the draw. **Mutation checks.** Forty edits of `turn.c` and `observation.c`, each built and run against `duoforge.state.pool_g30` and the pool conformance tests: no natural powder immunity, no Overcoat TryHit, its line without the ability, no Overcoat sand immunity, a powder move that ignores a Grass type, Rage Powder redirecting a powder-immune attacker (Grass or Overcoat, each alone), no volatile, Follow Me's redirect inverted, the view showing Follow Me's flag or no RAGE_POWDER bit, Psychic Fangs breaking nothing, leaving Aurora Veil, the wrong order, no `-sideend`, Solar Beam charging in sun, skipping in rain, boosting Special Attack, full power in rain, sand and snow, halved in sun, quarter power, Electro Shot skipping in sun, Flame Body's threshold (3 in 10 against 4), not for a holder that is down, not for a standing holder, without contact, burning the holder, Clear Body showing for a secondary, blocking the holder's own drop, blocking nothing, without its line, Hospitality healing an eighth, never at the entry, twice after a Trace copy, not through Trace, healing the foe side: each fails a test, but one: the powder immunity checked for the user too is an equivalent mutant (Rage Powder, the only powder move that targets its user, returns before the hit loop). Two of them (the threshold and the missing contact test) needed a second look: the first recording had no draw of exactly 3 that a burn could follow and no non-contact hit on a Flame Body holder, so `g30_flame_body` got a seed with both and Incineroar's Snarl. **Campaign.** One mirror campaign over `tests/reference/teams/team_g30.txt` (Sinistcha, Venusaur, Metagross, Volcarona, Reuniclus, Whimsicott: every new row but Matcha Gotcha's secondaries in play; all items distinct, Item Clause) under the machine lock: `diff_driver.py random --battles 1500 --seed 3030 --pairings GG --workers 4 --domain-rate 0.1`, 1500 of 1500 PASS, 6246 requests sampled for the domain check and all agreed (a first attempt with repeated items was refused at the setup by Item Clause, 1500 `create` refusals, and is not evidence).
