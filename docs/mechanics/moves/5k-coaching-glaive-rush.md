# 5k. G19, Coaching and Glaive Rush

- Area: moves
- Step: G19
- Tests: duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data, duoforge.state.pool_g19

5k. **G19, Coaching and Glaive Rush** (17 and 15 of the 227 survey teams; two mechanics that share no engine code, one PR because both change the generated pool tables). The handler column gets one new id, `DFI_SPECIAL_GLAIVE_RUSH` (the id of UNMODELED moves up by one), the boost-role column one new value, `DFI_BOOST_ROLE_PRIMARY_ALLY` (4, internal, defined in `pool_tables.h`); the POOL fingerprints change (`38556ca8...` for the tables, `a1dc59ca...` for POOL and `2a46ebda...` for POOL_DEV), CLOSURE and TEAM_C do not. Two rows leave UNMODELED (305 to 302 with the move that G16 left). No public value changes: nothing of either move is a new event, cause, draw site or choice.
   - **Coaching, the pin.** `data/moves.ts:2590-2605`: Status, accuracy true, PP 10, flags `bypasssub`, `allyanim` and `metronome` only (no `protect`: Protect does not stop it), target `adjacentAlly`, `boosts: {atk: 1, def: 1}`; the Champions mod has no entry. The generator gives a status move with that target and primary boosts the role `PRIMARY_ALLY` (the same boosts on any other non-self target stay refused). The turn code runs it after the target step that Helping Hand uses: with no standing ally there is no target (`[notarget]`, `-fail`), a Good as Gold ally is immune (`-immune|ally|[from] ability: Good as Gold`: a status move of another Pokemon), and otherwise the ordinary boost path (`dfi_boost`, `sim/battle.ts:2020-2088`) raises the ally: Contrary or a cap at the ally change it, Defiant and Competitive answer only a drop that a foe causes (`source` on the other side), Flower Veil blocks only drops. `-boost|ally|atk|1`, `-boost|ally|def|1`, no `[from]`.
   - **Glaive Rush, the pin.** `data/moves.ts:6647-6678`: Physical, 120, accuracy 100, PP 5, `self: {volatileStatus: 'glaiverush'}` and a condition: `onStart` shows `-singlemove|X|Glaive Rush|[silent]` (the line is in the log with the `[silent]` flag, which a client does not display; the converter drops silent lines and the engine has no event for it), `onAccuracy` returns true, `onSourceModifyDamage` returns `chainModify(2)`, `onBeforeMovePriority: 100`, `onBeforeMove` removes the volatile. The Champions mod has only its learnset (Baxcalibur). The self effect is `selfDrops`' (`sim/battle-actions.ts:1096, 1317-1335`), so as for the recharge: a hit gives the volatile, a miss, a Protect or an immunity do not. The holder of an `onSource...` handler is the defender (the event's source): the moves that hit the user cannot miss (the `Accuracy` event, `sim/battle-actions.ts:736-738`: `true` means no draw, so the engine has no ACCURACY draw for them) and do twice the damage (`ModifyDamage`: `chainModify(2)`, 8192/4096). The user's next action of any kind starts with the removal (priority 100, before flinch 8, the recharge 11 and everything else), so it is gone when that action is a flinch, a recharge turn or a move that then fails; leaving the field or fainting ends it too. The user stays vulnerable for the rest of the turn it used the move and until its next action begins: a faster foe on the next turn hits it doubled, a slower one after it does not.
   - **State and view.** `glaive_rush` (0 or 1) of the position is the state (tail rev 2, nothing new); the view is the existing `GLAIVE_RUSH` bit of `position_ext.volatiles`, supported bit 20 (decision 0018 section 6.1: set by the move line that hit, cleared by the user's next `move` or `cant` line and by leaving). The silent line is no event; the view bit carries it.
   - **The one case not modelled.** The ModifyDamage modifiers are chained from 4096 with a rounding at each step; Life Orb (5324), a resist berry (2048) and a screen (2732) commute in every order, and so does the doubling with any two of them, but all four together give 3552 or 3551 by their order (decided by the holders' speeds, and a tie draw). That combination (a Life Orb attacker, a resist berry eaten, a screen, and a Glaive Rush target) is refused with `E_UNSUPPORTED`, not guessed. The converter drops a ModifyDamage tie of Glaive Rush with the other modifiers (all subsets of at most three commute) under that precondition.
   - **Evidence.** Ten recorded battles under the POOL kind: `g19_coaching` (+1/+1 for an ally with Defiant, twice; Protect does not matter), `_contrary` (Serperior is lowered), `_good_as_gold`, `_no_ally` (the ally was knocked out earlier in the turn: `[notarget]`, `-fail`), `_protect`, `g19_glaive_rush` (turn 2: Raichu hits the user doubled and cannot miss, Milotic, slower than the user, hits it normally and misses; turn 3 normal), `_protect` and `_immune` (no drawback: the foe's Muddy Water can miss), `_flinch` (Fake Out the turn after: doubled, `cant|flinch`, the drawback is gone for the slower foe), `_mega` (Mega Baxcalibur, step G18: Glaive Rush on the turn it Mega Evolves). `duoforge.reference.conformance_pool_data` compares every event, HP, request and the tape; `duoforge.state.pool_g19` reads the tail and both viewers' extension after every step against rows that `test_trace_to_c.py` derives from the protocol lines; `tools/datagen/pool_families.js` checks the pinned facts of both moves. Mutation checks (the volatile not set, set without a hit, never cleared, cleared only without a flinch, the accuracy draw kept, no doubling, tripling, no view bit, Coaching boosting its user, Coaching ignoring Good as Gold) each fail a test. Two differential campaigns under the machine lock (teams of Baxcalibur, Sneasler, Raichu, Serperior, Gholdengo and Incineroar against the closure teams, the recharge and Encore teams and themselves; 400 battles, seeds 9191 and 9192): all PASS, with 322 Coaching moves and 595 Glaive Rush moves (435 hits) in 310 battles, and the engine offered what Showdown accepts in all 1562 sampled requests.
