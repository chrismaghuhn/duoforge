# 5h. G16, Knock Off

- Area: moves
- Step: G16
- Tests: duoforge.combat.item_family, duoforge.items.knock_off

5h. **G16, Knock Off** (19 of the 227 survey teams; the first user of the tail's `item_now`, rev 2). Knock Off keeps its handler id as a row of the whole pool, and the turn code implements it; Sticky Hold, which it reads by id, becomes an engine row. Both are marked, with the view bit 11.
   - **The pin.** `data/moves.ts:9959-9984` (the Champions mod does not override the move): Dark, Physical, 65, accuracy 100, contact, protect, mirror. `onBasePower` (priority 0, so the last handler of the chain: after Fairy Aura 20, the type items 15, Helping Hand 10 and the terrains 6) chains 1.5 when `singleEvent('TakeItem', item, ...)` of the target's item answers true and the item exists; the item's own `onTakeItem` is all that is asked there (Sticky Hold is not: a Swalot holding an item is hit for the boosted power). `onAfterHit` is `target.takeItem()` and, when it gave an item, `-enditem|target|Item|[from] move: Knock Off|[of] user`. **The Champions mod runs the after-hit without asking whether the user still stands** (`data/mods/champions/scripts.ts:411-415`; the base game has `&& pokemon.hp` at `sim/battle-actions.ts:1123`, and the research note of the survey, `champions-reg-mc-report-draft.md:211`, says so): a user that the Rocky Helmet knocked out takes the item all the same (`g16_helmet_faint`). The after-hit runs for every damaged target, a target that this hit knocked out included (its faint is not processed yet), after `DamagingHit` (Rocky Helmet's damage, Stamina) and before the Emergency Exit check.
   - **`takeItem` and `TakeItem`** (`sim/pokemon.ts:1851-1866`; `sim/battle.ts:956-972`, the ability's handler before the item's): no item, nothing (a Sitrus Berry already eaten, a Focus Sash that this very hit used up, an item taken before: no boost and no line). Sticky Hold (`data/abilities.ts:4622-4635`) of a Pokemon that is **alive** shows `-activate|X|ability: Sticky Hold` and the item stays; of a fainted one it does not block, so a Swalot that the hit knocked out loses its Leftovers (`g16_sticky_hold`). Unburden's `onTakeItem` (`data/abilities.ts:5240-5242`) adds its volatile at this point. The item's own `onTakeItem`: the **only** items of the pool that have one are the 81 Mega Stones, each of which answers false for its own species (`!item.megaStone?.[source.baseSpecies.baseSpecies]`; Floettite and Meowsticite compare by name, with the same result; the source of `takeItem()` is the Pokemon itself), so a Mega Stone on its own species is neither boosted nor taken, before and after the Mega Evolution (`g16_stones`), and a Mega Stone on another species is taken like any item (`g16_removal`, Charizardite Y on a Sneasler). No mask, plate, drive, orb or Z crystal is in the pool. Neither the item's `End` nor `AfterTakeItem` has a handler in the pool.
   - **State.** `item_now` of the member (decision 0018, section 7 here) is the one item the engine reads: `dfi_item_code(b, m)` in `src/combat/items/held.h` is its accessor, as `dfi_ability_code` is for the ability (the sheet's item unless it was used up, or the tail's: 255 is "holds nothing", 1 to 254 an item id + 1, which nothing writes yet). Every rule that read `dfi_member.item` reads it: the Choice Scarf's Speed and lock, Life Orb, Rocky Helmet, Light Clay, Leftovers, White Herb, Sitrus Berry, Grassy Seed, the type boosters and resist berries and Focus Sash (the pure functions of `combat/items/families.h` take the item code now, `tests/items/test_item_family.c` follows with the same cases). A taken item is gone **for good**: `item_now` stays across a switch-out and a faint (`sim/pokemon.ts:1851-1866` restores nothing; the only trace of the old rule, `itemKnockedOff`, is read for generation 4 alone), which is what the tail comment said; `dfi_tail_clear_occupant` does not touch it (it clears the position, the soak type, the current ability and the toxic stage). A Pokemon that comes back in has no item and no Unburden volatile (the volatile ends with the position).
   - **Rules of the turn.** The boost is the last link of the BasePower chain. The removal comes from `dfi_knock_off` after the DamagingHit handlers, for every hit target in order: Sticky Hold (alive) shows its `-activate` and stops; a Mega Stone on its own species stops silently (`mega_capable`: exactly "holds the stone of its own base forme", kept after the Mega Evolution); otherwise `item_now` becomes 255 and ITEM_END shows it. An Unburden holder that is alive gets its volatile (Speed x2: in `g16_unburden` the Sneasler moves before the Meowscarada that was faster from the next turn on). **A Choice lock ends with the item** (`choicelock`'s `onBeforeMove` and `onDisableMove` remove it for a Pokemon without a Choice item, and a move that was already chosen still runs, so ending the lock here is the same): the next request offers every move, and the lost Scarf is no longer in the Speed (`g16_scarf_helmet`). A Sitrus Berry is taken before the `Update` that would eat it (`g16_removal`: the hit leaves Pelipper at half or less and nothing is eaten, nothing heals). The Unburden volatile of a holder whose stone then refuses (Hawlucha with Hawluchanite) was the one place where the pin and the engine differed in what is set; the Mega batch (5n), which marks Hawluchanite, sets it as the pin does.
   - **Invariants.** A choice lock needs the Choice item that the member still has (it said "no item is ever lost while it holds"); Unburden's volatile needs the item gone, used up or taken.
   - **The lock outlives the item until the pin ends it (a conformance fix of this step's first reading).** Knock Off took the lock with the Scarf at once; the pin does not: choicelock ends in its own `onBeforeMove` (`data/conditions.ts:332-336`, the holder's next move, when it holds no Choice item) and `onDisableMove` (`:349-352`, run for every active Pokemon in `endTurn`, `sim/battle.ts:1691`); a Choice item's own `onStart` ends it when the holder gets one (`data/items.ts:966-972`). So a stop in the middle of a turn after a holder that has moved lost its Scarf (a replacement, a pivot, the end of the battle) still shows the lock. The engine: `dfi_choice_lock_ends` in `dfi_run_move` (after BeforeMove let the holder move) and in `dfi_end_turn`, nowhere else; the invariant (`invariants.c`) accepts a lock without its Choice item under the POOL kinds at every boundary but the turn's. Found by the first mirror campaign of step G29 (30 of 1200 battles; 4 with Knock Off alone); the recorded battle `g16_scarf_lock_stays` shows it. No version bump: no recorded battle or golden changed.
   - **Event (new public value, owner's OK of 2026-10-02).** `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_ITEM_TAKEN` = 17, a cause of ITEM_END: the item was taken by a move; `id` the move, `other` the user, `id2` item + 1; the old events of an item used up are unchanged. The old `item_used` of the view shows the item gone for a taken item as for a used-up one (the reference's own state does, and the foe's knowledge flag follows the event; the invariant of the knowledge accepts a taken item), `item_now` tells them apart. The converter reads `-enditem|X|Item|[from] move: M|[of] Y` as one and refuses any other shape of a `[from] move:` line; `-activate|X|ability: Sticky Hold` is the ACTIVATE of the ability, as every other one.
   - **Deviation from the plan sent to the coordinator (owner OK, 2026-10-02: under POOL the base view's consumed flag may also mean "taken by Knock Off").** The plan left the base view's `item_used` alone. It is changed: the consumed flag of the base view now also means "taken", because the reference's own state shows a taken item as gone in `item_used`. In the code, `events.c` marks the member's item consumed (the foe's knowledge) for an ITEM_END of any cause, and the knowledge invariant accepts `item_consumed == 1` or `item_now == NONE` (255). The ext overlay `item_now` (NONE) is what tells a taken item from a used-up one. Used up or taken, the old events of an item used up are unchanged. This is POOL-only in practice: an ITEM_END with the cause ITEM_TAKEN only exists there (Knock Off is marked in no other kind).
   - **View.** Decision 0018: `item_now` = 255 (`DUOFORGE_ITEM_NOW_NONE`) of the member, public (the `-enditem` line is in both streams), both sides' roster, supported bit 11 `ITEM_CHANGE`. **Trick, Switcheroo and Thief stay unmarked**, so setup refuses them and no accepted battle has a swapped item: `item_now` is 0 or 255 in every battle that runs, and the bit is set for the item that a move took alone (as G8 set Heal Block's bit with Psychic Noise alone).
   - **Tables.** The special id KNOCK_OFF (24) is new, so UNMODELED moves to 25 and every UNMODELED move row changes by a byte: the pool tables, their hash and the POOL fingerprints change (CLOSURE and TEAM_C do not). The generator checks the whole text of both callbacks (whitespace aside) against the pin, and `ENGINE_ROWS` has Sticky Hold. 305 moves and 180 abilities are unmodeled (306 and 181 before).
   - **Evidence.** Six recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; seeds found by predicates over recorded battles): `g16_removal` (Pelipper's Sitrus Berry taken at 38 percent, not eaten and no heal; Sneasler's Charizardite Y taken; Sneasler switches out and comes back with nothing; the Gholdengo that comes in loses its Life Orb; Knock Off at an empty hand has the plain power and no line), `g16_unburden` (Sneasler's Sitrus Berry taken: the next turn it moves before the Meowscarada that was faster; a Gholdengo's Life Orb), `g16_stones` (Staraptite and Salamencite stay, also after the Mega Evolution), `g16_sticky_hold` (Swalot's Sticky Hold alive: -activate and the Leftovers stay; a Raichu's Focus Sash used by the hit and nothing taken after it; the Meowscarada's own Black Glasses taken by the Swalot's Knock Off; the knocked-out Swalot loses its Leftovers), `g16_scarf_helmet` (Annihilape's Choice Scarf goes with its lock, Archaludon's Rocky Helmet hurts the user first and goes) and `g16_helmet_faint` (the user that the helmet knocks out takes the helmet all the same). `duoforge.items.knock_off` reads the tail's `item_now` of every member, the events of the step (counts, move, user, item) and both viewers' whole extension after every step against rows that `tools/reference/test_trace_to_c.py` derives from the protocol lines (the `-enditem ... Knock Off` and `-activate ... Sticky Hold` lines); the invariants are checked on states of the battles. Mutation checks of the implementation, 26 edits, each fails `duoforge.items.knock_off` or the conformance: the boost dropped, given to an own stone or to an empty hand; Sticky Hold ignored, silent, or blocking a fainted holder; an own stone taken; Unburden not set on removal or set on a fainted holder; the lock kept; `item_now` not written; the removal needing a standing user or skipping a fainted target; the cause, the move or the user of the event dropped; the accessor ignoring the tail; the Scarf Speed, the Leftovers heal, the Rocky Helmet or the Sitrus Berry reading the sheet instead of the accessor; a switch-out giving the item back; the foe's knowledge not following the event; the view without `item_now`; the Unburden and the Choice lock invariants loosened.
