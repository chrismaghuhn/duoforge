# 5r. G20, Spiky Shield and tail rev 3

- Area: moves
- Step: G20
- Tests: duoforge.mechanics.spiky_shield, duoforge.state.pool_tail

5r. **G20, Spiky Shield and tail rev 3** (the second half of G20; new state, so its own PR). Spiky Shield (data/moves.ts:17532-17584, Champions: only the PP changes) is Protect with a contact punishment: the same `onPrepareHit` (priority +4, fails unless an action is pending, the shared stall counter), the same `onHit`, `-singleturn|X|move: Protect` (Protect itself prints `-singleturn|X|Protect`), and an `onTryHit` that stops every move with the protect flag (status moves included) with `-activate|X|move: Protect` and, when the move makes contact, `this.damage(source.baseMaxhp / 8, source, target)`: floor(max HP / 8) of the attacker, at least 1, `-damage|attacker|hp|[from] Spiky Shield|[of] holder`.
   - **Marked.** Spiky Shield only (Chesnaught, whose Overgrow is marked, plays it; Glimmora's abilities are not marked). **Baneful Bunker** (poison on a contact attacker) and **King's Shield** (-1 Attack, status moves pass) stay unmarked: their only learners, Toxapex (Merciless, Limber, Regenerator) and Aegislash (Stance Change), have no supported ability, so no accepted battle could use them and nothing could record them; Silk Trap, Burning Bulwark and Obstruct are not in the Champions pool, and Quick Guard and Endure are out of this step. A Protect-variant row that gets a player later needs only its handler and a value of `protect_kind`, no layout change.
   - **State (tail rev 3, 0x0303).** The variant has to survive a snapshot, because a PIVOT boundary inside the turn (a foe's U-turn after the shield went up) is a boundary where a state is encoded, and the flags byte of the slot is full. HauptSession's decision: tail rev 3 defines the byte at +26 of a position as `protect_kind` (rev 2 reserved it), the schema is 0x0303 (same size, 1257 bytes), a decoder accepts exactly that triple and refuses 0x0203 like it refuses 0x0103, the other reserves stay documented as unassigned (43 of the 248), CLOSURE and TEAM_C bytes and digests do not change. In memory a position gets an always-zero pad byte (its 27 data bytes with two u16). Internal: no public constant, no version bump by itself.
   - **Rules of the turn.** `dfi_run_protect` takes the variant and stores it in the user's tail with the volatile; the residual clears it with the volatile. After the `-activate` line of a stopped contact move the punishment runs, also for an attacker that it knocks out. Two holders of a punishing variant stopping one contact spread move would show their lines in the speed order of one TryHit event (a tie draws): `E_UNSUPPORTED`, never a guess. Outrage's lock reset is not modelled (no marked move locks).
   - **Event (additional use of an existing value, agreed with the coordinator).** The damage is `DAMAGE` with cause `DUOFORGE_CAUSE_MOVE`, the move in `id2` and the holder in `other`, as Rocky Helmet uses `CAUSE_ITEM`; documented at the constant in `duoforge.h`. No new cause, no new draw site.
   - **Tables.** The special id SPIKY_SHIELD (28) is new, so UNMODELED moves to 29 (the generator checks the whole condition text and that the other fields are Protect's).
   - **Evidence.** Three recorded battles under the POOL kind (genders stated; seeds picked by predicates): `g20_spiky_shield_a` (a contact move stopped and punished, a spread move that is not contact stopped without damage, Protect then Spiky Shield against the shared stall counter, a Spiky Shield that fails its roll), `_ko` (the punishment knocks the attacker out inside the hit), `_pivot` (a foe's U-turn pivots mid-turn while the shield is up, and the next contact hit of the turn is punished after the replacement). `duoforge.mechanics.spiky_shield` compares protect_kind of every position after every step against rows that `test_trace_to_c.py` derives from the protocol lines, and round-trips the state through its encoding at every boundary; `duoforge.state.pool_tail` has the byte sweep, the schema tests (0x0303 accepted, 0x0203 and 0x0103 refused) and the invariants. The harness converter reads the volatile `spikyshield` as Protect's bit. 144 pool battles and 771 stops (counted by hand). Mutation checks (no punishment, a sixth, no contact test, plain Protect punishing, the variant never stored or never cleared, no cause, the holder swapped, the variant not encoded or not decoded, rev 2 accepted, no invariant, no pad check) each fail a test; the minimum of 1 is an equivalent mutant (a member has at least 8 HP) and the two-punishers `E_UNSUPPORTED` has no battle (no test reaches it). A campaign under the machine lock (120 battles, seed 2002, `team_spiky_shield.txt`) was 120 PASS under POOL, 236 Spiky Shield uses in 66 battles.
