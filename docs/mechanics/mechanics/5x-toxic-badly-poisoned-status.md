# 5x. G36, Toxic and the badly poisoned status

- Area: mechanics
- Step: G36
- Tests: duoforge.state.pool_g36

5x. **G36, Toxic and the badly poisoned status** (the tail's `toxic_stage` per roster member, rev 2 as it is, and the ailment `DUOFORGE_AILMENT_TOX` = 6 that decision 0018 and the header already have: **no new public value**, no new event or cause, no layout change). Toxic and Poison Fang become modelled rows (the generator's pool status table gains `tox` = 6; under CLOSURE and TEAM_C the tables are byte for byte the same).
   - **The pin.** Toxic (`data/moves.ts:19733-19748`): Poison, Status, PP 10, accuracy 90, flags protect, reflectable, mirror, `status: 'tox'`, no Champions override. A Poison-type user never misses with it: the accuracy step and the invulnerability step test `move.id === 'toxic' && pokemon.hasType('Poison')` for gen 8 and later (`sim/battle-actions.ts:627` and `:731`), so no accuracy draw. Poison Fang (`:13463-13480`): 50 percent `status: 'tox'` secondary, contact and bite. The condition `tox` (`data/conditions.ts:138-161`): `onStart` sets the stage to 0 and prints `-status|X|tox` (with `[from] item: Toxic Orb`, or `[from] ability: NAME [of] source`, which no pool battle has); `onSwitchIn` sets the stage to 0 again (the status stays on the bench; Gen 9 has no switch-out cure); `onResidualOrder: 9` (psn's is 9 too, `:123-137`; no sub-order, so the two sort in one group with burn's 10 after them): the stage goes up by one to at most 15 first, then `damage(clampIntRange(baseMaxhp / 16, 1) * stage)`; the line is `-damage|X|HP tox|[from] psn` (the source it names is psn). A status that is set already refuses another (`-fail|X|tox` for the same one, `-fail` for another), and Poison and Steel types are immune to tox through the `psn` key of the type chart (`sim/pokemon.ts:1706-1722`: `runStatusImmunity(status.id === 'tox' ? 'psn' : status.id)`, `-immune` for a move's own status), unless the source has Corrosion.
   - **State, view, tables.** The member's status byte may be 6 under the POOL kinds (their bound was 5; the other kinds are unchanged), with no status counter; the stage lives in the tail (0 at the start and after a switch-in or a faint, the invariant: a stage needs the status tox and a member on the field, which is now reachable). The view shows the ailment Tox in the old observation's status field (the stage is hidden, as the game hides it) and sets the supported bit 10 (`AILMENT_TOX`). UNMODELED moves go from 262 to 260 (Toxic and Poison Fang were modelled except for `status tox`).
   - **Rules of the turn.** The status goes through the same `dfi_try_status` as the others (immunities, Flower Veil, the fail lines); the residual entry of poison also takes tox (order 9, sub-order 0, a callback: two of them of equal Speed shuffle), and its handler raises the stage and deals the damage above.
   - **Not in this step (and why).** Toxic Spikes (`data/moves.ts:19749-19788`) and Toxic Debris (`data/abilities.ts:5104-5117`, Glimmora) need a side condition with layers that a switch-in reads: a new `SIDE` value and the hazard state, a step of their own; both stay unmodelled. Not in the pool: Toxic Orb (`data/mods/champions/items.ts:970`, "Past"), Malignant Chain ("Past"), Flame Orb, Toxic Chain (no pool forme has it; `data/abilities.ts:5089-5103`). In the pool but unmarked, so no accepted battle has them: Corrosion (Salazzle, Glimmora: it would let Poison and Steel types be poisoned, `data/abilities.ts` corrosion and `sim/pokemon.ts:1708-1710`), Poison Heal (Gliscor), Merciless (Toxapex), Immunity (Snorlax), Pastel Veil, Magic Guard, Synchronize, Guts and Quick Feet, and the berries that cure poison (Pecha, Lum), Venoshock and Hex. A step that marks one of them reads these lines for tox. The 15-stage cap is in the code and the pin, and no recorded battle reaches it (a tox stage of 15 needs 15 residuals without a faint).
   - **Evidence.** Seven recorded battles under the POOL kind (`g36_*`, seeds by predicates; 227 pool battles, 1237 tiebreak stops, 46 view and tail rows in `duoforge.state.pool_g36`): `g36_toxic_a` (a Poison-type user without an accuracy draw, a Dark-type user with one, the stages 1 to 7 of the residual damage, a switch-out and back that restarts the stage while the status stays, a second Toxic that fails with `-fail|X|tox`), `g36_toxic_fail` (the immunities of a Steel and a Poison type), `g36_poison_fang_a` and `_b` (the 50 percent secondary, both outcomes), `g36_toxic_mirror_a` and `_b` (two Umbreon of equal Speed badly poisoning each other: the damage lines in both orders of the shuffle) and `g36_toxic_burn` (tox before burn in the residual, whatever the Speed).
   - **Mutation checks.** Fifteen edits (`g36_mut.py`), each killed by the conformance, the tiebreak stops, `duoforge.state.pool_g36`, the pool setup or the observation test, except one that cannot matter: the damage floor of 1 (every pool Pokemon has 16 or more maximum HP).
   - **Campaigns.** Two differential campaigns under the machine lock (seeds 36001 and 36002, 1200 battles; a team of Arbok, Umbreon, Mawile, Houndoom, Qwilfish and Sneasler with Toxic and Poison Fang against a team of attackers and each other): 1200 PASS, 0 DIVERGENCE, 0 UNSUPPORTED, with 2521 badly poisoned Pokemon, 8201 residual tox damages (stages up to 7), 843 failed Toxics, 3406 Toxics refused by the immunities and 236 Toxics that missed.
