# Team registry

One Showdown paste for each team (`<id>.txt`) and an index (`index.json`), shared by the differential loop and the learner.

Ids are stable and never reused, and a file never changes under its id: a correction is a new team with a new id (the old entry gets `superseded_by`). The files are LF (`.gitattributes`), and the `sha256` of the index is that of the text with CRLF turned into LF. Every gender is stated, Level 50, EVs are Stat Points, nothing else is allowed in a set.

The format, the index, the rules and `tools/reference/import_paste.py` (which makes a registry file of any paste) are described in `tools/reference/README.md`, section "The team registry". `python tools/reference/team_registry.py <repo root>` checks the registry.

Teams of the VGCPastes Reg M-C survey of 2026-10-02 have the id `PP_` and the upper case pokepast.es id of their paste (`source.url` is the paste; the survey's own row, `MC<n>`, is in the name and the notes). A re-import on 2026-10-03 (main 0.31.0, steps up to G15) added the teams that had begun to pass; the entries of the first import are untouched. New teams only ever get new ids; `docs/research/expansion/data/registry_blockers.json` lists what keeps the others out.

## Sources and credits

Every team is somebody's work, and each entry of `index.json` names where it is from (`source.url`, and `event` and `placing` where they are known; the `notes` say how it was fetched and every change `import_paste.py` made):

- **VGCPastes Repository** ([@VGCPastes](https://twitter.com/VGCPastes)): the teams `PP_<upper case pokepast.es id>` are its pastes, with their Pokepaste link as the source. They came in with the survey of 2026-10-02, its re-import of 2026-10-03, and the imports below.
- **Limitless** (online tournament results, [play.limitlesstcg.com](https://play.limitlesstcg.com)): the teams `LL_<tournament id, upper case>_<position in the standings>` are the team lists of its online Reg M-C and Reg M-B tournaments (open team sheets), read through the public [Limitless API](https://docs.limitlesstcg.com/developer.html) on 2026-10-03; the source is the tournament's standings page, with the tournament and the placing (or the record, for a player without a placing). A team list has **no EVs and no genders**: the registry has no EVs line for such a team (every Stat Point is 0), the importer wrote the species as the pokedex spells them, added "Level: 50" and the genders (the species' fixed one, else male: the owner's standard, as everywhere in this registry), and the entry says so.
- **MunchStats** ([munchstats.com/teams](https://munchstats.com/teams)) combines both and updates every 12 hours; its listing (`/teams/api/`) was how the VGCPastes teams were found. Its robots.txt asks for a crawl delay of 10 seconds, which was kept (the pastes themselves were read from pokepast.es, 2 seconds apart, the tournaments from the Limitless API, 6.5 seconds apart).

`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` repeats this as a notice. **The import of 2026-10-03** added 591 teams that pass Reg M-C setup under the POOL kind of main 0.33.0 (the other teams of the sources, 3,704 Reg M-C and 25,346 Reg M-B ones, do not, and are not here): 18 VGCPastes pastes, 303 Limitless Reg M-C teams and 270 Limitless Reg M-B teams that are legal in Reg M-C. A team that the registry or an earlier import has under another id (the same species, items, abilities, natures and moves) was not added again. New ids only: no file or entry that was there changed.

**The import of 2026-10-09** (`tools/reference/import_vgcpastes.py`, main 0.43.0) read the VGCPastes Repository's own sheet for Champions Reg M-A, M-B and M-C (the owner's HTML export; the featured tabs too), every row whose EVs column says Yes: 2,241 pastes, fetched from pokepast.es one at a time (at most one request a second) and kept outside the repository. It added the **393** teams whose paste the engine's setup under the POOL kind accepts and supports (Reg M-C 150, Reg M-B 120, Reg M-A 123), each entry with the row (`MC…`, `MB…`, `PC…`), the description, the author, the date, the event and the placing, and every change `import_paste.py` made. Left out: 1,626 legal teams that need a mechanic the library does not support yet (their ids, links and blockers are in `docs/research/expansion/data/vgcpastes_pending.json`, without the pastes: a later import adds them as their blockers fall), 65 that Reg M-C setup refuses (most write a Mega's ability on the base forme, which the importer does not guess), 19 pastes that are no registry paste (IVs lines, a misspelled nature), 5 with names outside the tables, 1 whose sets lack EVs, 442 duplicates (the featured rows and teams the registry or an earlier row has), and 71 pastes the registry had. A run keeps the pool it was started with (its teams' ids and hashes); the new teams are there for the next one.
