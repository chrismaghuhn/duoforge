## Team data (`data/teams/`)

The team registry holds Pokémon Champions teams that players published. The sets are the work of their players; DuoForge normalises their form (the genders and the level that a paste leaves out, see `data/teams/README.md`) and keeps every team's source link, tournament and placing, where known, in `data/teams/index.json` (`source` and `notes`). They are research data and are not under the licence of this repository. Anyone who wants a team taken out can ask the repository owner.

- **VGCPastes Repository** ([@VGCPastes](https://twitter.com/VGCPastes), the community-run repository of VGC teams and Pokepastes): the teams with an id `PP_` are its pastes, each linked to its pokepast.es paste (the existing 57 teams of the Reg M-C survey of 2026-10-02 among them). The imports of 2026-10-09 added 390 and then 372 more from the repository's Champions Reg M-A, M-B and M-C sheet (the owner's export of it), and `data/teams_pending/` holds the pastes of 1,239 further teams of it that are legal but not playable yet; each entry names the sheet row and the paste's author, and the pastes were read from pokepast.es one at a time, at most one request a second.
- **Limitless** ([online tournament platform](https://play.limitlesstcg.com), [API](https://docs.limitlesstcg.com/developer.html)): the teams with an id `LL_` are the team lists of its online tournaments (standings and open team sheets), read through its public API, which needs no key. Its [Terms of Service](https://play.limitlesstcg.com/tos) were read before: they do not forbid the use.
- **MunchStats** ([munchstats.com/teams](https://munchstats.com/teams)): the aggregator of the VGCPastes Repository and of the Limitless results through which these teams were found. Its robots.txt (a crawl delay of 10 seconds) was kept.

## Smogon usage statistics (`data/belief/`)

`data/belief/smogon-spreads-regmc-2026-09.json` is derived from [Smogon's usage statistics](https://www.smogon.com/stats). With thanks to Smogon and its statistics maintainers. The live search draws its belief of a foe's spreads from it.

- **Source:** the "chaos" JSON files of `gen9championsvgc2026regmc` and `gen9championsvgc2026regmcbo3`.
  - Month 2026-09, rating cutoffs 1760, 1630, 1500 and 0.
  - Fetched on 2026-10-12.
  - Each file is pinned by its SHA-256 in the table's header and in `python/duoforge_search/smogon_spreads.py` (`SOURCES`).
- **What is committed:** only the derived table, never the raw files (owner, 2026-10-12). Per species, it holds the stat-point spreads of the highest cutoff with enough weight, at most 32 of them, as whole draws.
- **Regenerating:** `python -m duoforge_search.smogon_spreads --stats DIR` builds the table from a cache of the raw files. `--fetch` downloads a missing one, and `--check` verifies the committed table.

## Smogon dex VGC sets (`data/book/`)

`data/book/smogon-dex-vgc-2026-10.json` is derived from [Smogon's Champions dex](https://www.smogon.com/dex/champions/). With thanks to Smogon and its VGC analysis writers. It checks the archetype tag table (`data/book/archetype-tags.json`) against the roles Smogon's sets are named for (issue #458).

- **Source:** the dex pages of the 100 species with the most points on [Limitless VGC](https://limitlessvgc.com/pokemon) (Reg M-C, 2026-10-13); kept are their sets of VGC26 Regulation M-A and M-B. Fetched on 2026-10-13, one request every 3 seconds.
- **What is committed:** only facts of each set (its name, items, abilities, moves, stat points and natures). The analyses' text is never copied, and the raw pages stay in a cache outside the repository (owner, #458).
- **Regenerating:** `python -m duoforge_search.smogon_dex --cache DIR` builds the table from the cached pages; `--fetch SLUGS` downloads missing ones, and `--check` verifies the committed table.
